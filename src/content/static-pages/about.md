Wander Atlas is a curated, editor-reviewed resource for international visitors. We're transparent about how it's produced.

## The editor

Every guide on this site is reviewed by **Pixer** — a travel editor based in Vietnam who has personally traveled twelve countries, including all of Vietnam and Thailand, most of South Korea, plus Singapore, Laos, Cambodia, Indonesia, Hong Kong, Macau, China, the US and Australia. Pixer is a pen name; we tell you that up front rather than inventing a persona. Guides for countries the editor has visited are marked on the home page, and we never claim personal visits we haven't made.

## AI assistance

Our guides are drafted with AI assistance and then checked against live data. We disclose this on every article. AI helps us keep coverage broad and up to date; it does not replace factual verification.

## Where our facts come from

- Business details, ratings, and hours: verified commercial map and venue data providers.
- Crowd and busyness estimates: measured foot-traffic data, clamped to each venue's opening hours.
- Practical info (visa, emergency, transport): official government and tourism sources.

## Our verification standards

Because coverage is broad, we hold every venue guide to concrete, automated standards rather than opinions:

- **Quality floor:** when verified rating data is available, we only feature places rated 4.0★ or higher with a meaningful number of reviews.
- **Freshness:** venue data is re-checked on a schedule; anything that has closed is automatically unpublished, so we don't send you to a place that no longer exists.
- **Licensed images only:** every image comes from an approved, licensed source (Wikimedia Commons, Foursquare, Openverse, or public/open data) with attribution — never a scraped photo.
- **No invented details:** our drafting process is explicitly instructed never to fabricate hours, prices, or addresses; specifics come from data, not guesswork.

Guides are produced by the Wander Atlas editorial team using this process, and reviewed by our editor before and after publication. We do not claim personal visits we haven't made — we're a curation-and-verification resource, and we say so.

## Images

We only use images we are licensed to use — public-domain and openly-licensed sources (e.g. Wikimedia Commons, Korea Tourism Organization open data, Openverse), or images we credit and link to their source. We do not republish others' photos without a license.

## Corrections

Found something out of date? Places close and prices change. We update automatically, but if you spot an error, let us know.
