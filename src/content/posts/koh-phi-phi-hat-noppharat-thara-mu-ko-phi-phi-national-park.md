## What this park actually covers

The name is confusing because it's really two places under one park designation. Hat Noppharat Thara is the long mainland beach near Ao Nang; Mu Ko Phi Phi is the island group 45km offshore, including Phi Phi Don, Phi Phi Leh, Bamboo Island and Maya Bay.

Most visitors booking a "Phi Phi National Park tour" are heading for the second half: limestone cliffs, karst gorges and the coral reefs made famous by *The Beach*. Entry is ticketed at park checkpoints on the islands, separate from any boat-tour fare.

## Maya Bay and the island-hopping route

Maya Bay reopened with strict visitor management: boats now dock at a rear pontoon, not the main sand, and swimming is restricted to protect the reef. Expect a queue system during peak hours.

Standard day-trip routes from Phi Phi Don also stop at:

- Pileh Lagoon, a near-enclosed cove ringed by sheer cliffs
- Viking Cave, on Phi Phi Leh's east side, still used for bird's-nest harvesting
- Monkey Beach, where long-tailed macaques patrol the shoreline
- Bamboo Island, quieter coral reef good for snorkelling

Park gates run 8am-4:30pm every day, so any boat scheduled to land after 4:30pm won't get you through. Confirm your operator's return time; official closing catches out slow tour groups.

## Timing the crowds

Weekday midday tends to be calmer at the park entry points. Boats that left Phi Phi Don at dawn have mostly moved on, and the lunch lull thins arrivals.

Weekends are the opposite. Between 8am and 10am, speedboats from Phuket, Krabi and Ao Nang converge on Maya Bay simultaneously, backing up the pontoon queue. If you can only travel on a Saturday or Sunday, push your departure later than the 8am rush.

Longtail and speedboat combinations run daily from Phi Phi Don pier and from Ao Nang/Krabi on the mainland. Either route lands you inside the same park boundary.

## Booking and getting in without wasted time

Most travelers buy island-hopping tours through Phi Phi Don guesthouses or online operators rather than arranging park entry themselves; the tour price usually excludes the park fee, collected separately at the gate in cash.

- Bring a printed or digital ID/passport copy; rangers sometimes check at Maya Bay
- Reef shoes help on Pileh Lagoon's rocky entry points
- Sunscreen bans (reef-safe only) are enforced at some checkpoints, so buy it before boarding
- Full-day tours (around 6-8 hours) cover more stops than half-day Maya Bay-only trips

This is a well-known, heavily visited park, not a quiet find. Book your boat a day ahead in high season (November to April) since the better-reviewed operators fill their morning slots first.
