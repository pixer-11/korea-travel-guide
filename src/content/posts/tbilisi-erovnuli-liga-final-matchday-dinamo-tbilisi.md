## The last whistle of the 2026 season

On December 5, 2026, Georgia's top flight plays its final matchday. The Erovnuli Liga is the country's highest football division, and this is where the 2026 season ends. Dinamo Tbilisi go into the day as league leaders, playing at home in the capital.

Dinamo carry more weight than any other Georgian club. They won the European Cup Winners' Cup in 1981 and have the most domestic titles in the country. When they lead the table on the final day, the home end fills with blue and white scarves.

## Boris Paichadze Dinamo Arena

The stadium is a concrete bowl from the Soviet era. It opened in 1976 and is named after Boris Paichadze, the club's great forward of the 1930s and 40s. It is Georgia's largest football ground and also hosts the national team. The steep, open tiers run all the way round, so sightlines are good from most sections.

It sits on the left bank of the Mtkvari, north of the old town, in the Chughureti and Didube side of the city. Ways to get there:

- **Metro:** the Saburtalo line runs through Station Square (Vagzlis Moedani) and Tsereteli, both within walking distance of the ground.
- **Taxi:** Bolt is widely used in Tbilisi. Drop-offs on match days usually stop a block or two short of the gates.
- **On foot:** from Marjanishvili and Aghmashenebeli Avenue it is a flat walk north, and many fans take it.

## Tickets and the matchday

Kickoff time, the opponent and ticket sales for the final matchday all come from Dinamo Tbilisi and the league. Domestic league tickets are usually sold by section, and the side stands are the easiest choice for neutral visitors. Georgian league crowds are normally much smaller than this stadium's capacity. A title-deciding home game is the exception that brings out the city.

Most seats are uncovered, and the rows are high and open to the wind. Early December evenings in Tbilisi often drop close to freezing, so locals come in heavy coats, hats and gloves. Prices are not covered here. The club's ticket page and the box office show the current ones.

## In the stands with the Dinamo crowd

The organised home support stands behind one goal. That is where the flags, drums and almost nonstop chanting are. Visitors who want to sit and watch the football usually pick the long sides. The Georgian word for the club is simply "Dinamo", and that is the chant you hear most.

- **Paying:** card payment is standard in central Tbilisi. Small kiosks near the ground sometimes take only lari in cash.
- **Food:** before the game, fans eat in the cafés and khachapuri bakeries around Marjanishvili and the Fabrika area. Inside the stadium the food is basic.
- **Bags:** stadium security searches bags at the gates. Pyrotechnics and glass are not allowed in.
- **After the match:** the crowd heads for the metro in one go. Many fans walk to Marjanishvili or Aghmashenebeli Avenue for dinner and wait for the queues to clear.

The old town, Rustaveli Avenue and the Dry Bridge market are all a short metro ride south. That makes it easy to spend the day sightseeing and go to the match in the evening.
