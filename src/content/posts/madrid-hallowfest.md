## Halloween weekend in a Humanes warehouse

Hallowfest is a Halloween party scaled up to festival size. The 2026 edition was set for October 31 to November 1. It mixes electronic music with urban music, so hard techno, hardstyle and UK rap share one weekend and one site.

The site is Fabrik, in Humanes de Madrid. It is one of the biggest club venues in the Madrid region. It sits on the industrial edge of a small town south-west of the capital, far from the city-centre clubs around Gran Vía and Malasaña. Events here mean a big sound system, a big room and a long night.

The dates mattered too. October 31, 2026 fell on a Saturday, and November 1 is Todos los Santos (All Saints' Day), a national holiday in Spain. The festival weekend lined up with a long weekend in Madrid.

## Three headliners, three crowds

The billed names show how wide the programme was:

- **Charlotte de Witte**: the Belgian techno DJ who founded the KNTXT label, known for fast, dark, relentless sets.
- **Brennan Heart**: a Dutch hardstyle veteran, and the draw for fans of hard kicks and euphoric melodies.
- **D-Block Europe**: the London rap duo of Young Adz and Dirtbike LB, carrying the urban side of the weekend.

Each name pulled from a different scene. Techno, hardstyle and UK rap rarely share one bill, and that mix was the point of the programme.

## Reaching Humanes de Madrid

Humanes de Madrid is at the end of Cercanías line C-5, the commuter rail line that runs through Atocha. The train takes roughly 45 minutes to an hour from central Madrid.

Fabrik is not next to the station. It is in the industrial estate on the edge of town, so the last stretch from Humanes station is a taxi, a ride-hailing car or a long walk along roads built for lorries. Driving is the other common way to reach the venue.

For big events, organisers sometimes run shuttle buses from Madrid to Fabrik.

## The ride home is the hard part

Cercanías trains do not run through the night. Late events at Fabrik finish after the last trains have gone, or before the first early-morning service, so the journey back in the small hours depends on road transport or a wait for the first train.

- Taxis and ride-hailing apps are the usual way back to Madrid after a late finish.
- Phone signal can be patchy when thousands of people are in one place.
- The first Cercanías train from Humanes leaves in the early morning, and its time is on the Renfe Cercanías timetable.
- Taxi queues at out-of-town venues tend to build fast at closing time.

## Costumes, queues and the door at Fabrik

Costumes are a big part of Halloween nights out. In Spain, Halloween leans towards horror over cute: zombies, skeleton face paint and blood-spattered outfits. Practical costumes work best at a venue like this, with no bulky props, nothing that looks like a real weapon, and shoes made for hours on your feet. Mask and prop rules are set by the venue.

Spanish nightlife starts late. At club events the crowd typically builds towards the main sets rather than at opening. Security queues are usually shorter early in the night, before the floor fills.

Age checks at Spanish club events are routine, and venues expect physical ID; a photo on a phone often isn't accepted. Tickets bought from anyone other than the official seller carry the risk of a duplicate or fake QR code being turned away at the door.
