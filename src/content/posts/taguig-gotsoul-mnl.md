## A DJ's room in Forbestown

A tapas bar with a proper booth is a deliberate choice. Small plates suit a crowd that stands, shifts and keeps ordering as the night goes on. That beats a table that eats once and leaves. Here, the sound system is the point, not an afterthought.

Jojo Flores is the draw for many guests. His name carries weight in the Manila club scene, and the bar is his project. It's still a recent opening and is getting talked about, so it draws people who want a lively night out over a long, quiet conversation.

If you want to hear your dinner companion clearly all evening, book somewhere else. If you want dinner that turns into a set, this is the place.

## Getting to Forbestown

Forbestown is a compact strip of restaurants and bars in BGC, Taguig. It sits around Burgos Circle, a small roundabout park. Bonifacio High Street's outdoor mall is a short walk south, and Uptown Bonifacio is a few minutes' drive north.

Your options:

- **Grab or taxi:** the simplest choice at night. Drop off on Forbestown Road and walk the last stretch.
- **Rail plus bus:** take MRT-3 to Ayala Station, then the BGC Bus from the EDSA Ayala terminal into the Fort.
- **Walking within BGC:** the grid is pedestrian-friendly. If you're already at High Street or on 5th Avenue, you can walk.

Going home late, use Grab. Bus service thins out late in the evening, and Forbestown's curb gets congested at closing time.

## Ordering, booking and settling up

These are the practical habits that make a night here go smoothly:

- **Reserve ahead,** especially for a Friday or Saturday or a night with a featured set. Newly opened bars in BGC fill quickly, and walk-ins may wait.
- **Confirm the hours and the programme** before you go. A DJ-led bar may change its schedule when there's an event, so check its official social channels on the day.
- **Pay by card** if you like. Venues in BGC widely take cards and e-wallets, and the bill usually adds a service charge. Read it before you add a tip on top.
- **Order in rounds.** With tapas, a few plates at a time keeps the table moving and the food hot.
- **Respect the booth.** Ask before filming the DJ up close, and don't lean on the equipment.

Filipinos are relaxed about how a night unfolds. Groups often arrive in stages, so a table booked for eight may fill slowly. Tell the host when you book if your party is arriving in waves.
