## One date, one open-air stage

Dubai gets one night of this tour. Indian singer-songwriter Anuv Jain brings his Dastakhat World Tour to the Dubai Millennium Amphitheatre at Expo City Dubai on **November 20, 2026**.

*Dastakhat* is the Hindi and Urdu word for a signature, and it suits a songwriter whose name has become shorthand for a certain kind of song: quiet, handwritten and built around one guitar. Expect a crowd that already knows every word.

The tour's ticket seller holds the seat categories and prices. This guide doesn't print figures it can't stand behind.

## The voice behind "Baarishein"

Anuv Jain comes from Ludhiana in Punjab. His audience grew online, song by song, rather than through film soundtracks. "Baarishein" made his name, and "Gul", "Alag Aasmaan", "Husn" and "Jo Tum Mere Ho" followed it into millions of playlists.

The sound is stripped back. His soft Hindi vocals sit over acoustic guitar, with lyrics about longing and small domestic moments. These are his best-known songs, not a setlist for this show.

What's reliably true is how his shows feel. The quiet songs turn into a crowd singalong, often louder than the stage.

## Inside the Millennium Amphitheatre

The amphitheatre is one of Expo City Dubai's outdoor venues. It's an open-air bowl on the former Expo 2020 site, with tiered seating curving around the stage. There's no roof between you and the sky.

That suits November. Late autumn is when Dubai's evenings cool off, typically into the mid-20s Celsius, and outdoor concerts come back after the summer heat. A light layer covers the breeze late in the night.

The Expo grounds are built for walking: wide shaded paths, signposted plazas and plenty of seating. Getting from the station or the car parks to the amphitheatre is a walk across the site, not a quick hop.

## Reaching Expo City from the rest of Dubai

Expo City sits in Dubai South, a long way from Downtown and the Marina. The Metro is the simplest way in.

- **Metro:** the Red Line's Route 2020 branch ends at **Expo 2020 station**, beside the site. The line splits at Jabal Ali, so riders take a train signed for Expo 2020, not the other branch.
- **Payment:** fares are paid with a Nol card, topped up at station machines or counters.
- **Road:** taxis, Careem and Uber all serve Expo City, and the site has large car parks for drivers.
- **After the show:** thousands of people leave at once, so the queues at taxi ranks and the station platforms build fast.

Coming from Downtown or Deira, the Metro ride is long. Many fans eat in the city first or on the Expo site itself.

## Concert manners under the Dubai sky

Watch the crowd and you'll pick up the etiquette quickly. At an Anuv Jain show, people sing along by default. Shushing the singalong goes against the spirit of the night.

- **Phone lights:** fans usually raise phone torches for the slowest songs. Filming whole tracks blocks the view of the people behind you.
- **Seats:** in a tiered amphitheatre, standing up for long stretches blocks the rows behind. Most people stand for the big choruses and sit for the rest.
- **Metro rules:** eating and drinking are banned on Dubai Metro trains and platforms, and fines apply. One carriage on each train is reserved for women and children, and the Gold Class carriage needs a Gold Class fare.
- **Dress:** smart-casual is the Dubai norm in public spaces. Covered shoulders are the safe choice for walking around Expo City.

Concert-goers usually turn up well before showtime. That way the walk from the Metro isn't a rush and they're in their seats for the first notes.

## Around the amphitheatre

The rest of the old Expo site is still there to explore before the show.

**Al Wasl Plaza** is the centrepiece. Its huge steel trellis dome works as a 360-degree projection screen after dark. **Surreal**, the water feature where walls of water fall and rise again, is nearby. **Garden in the Sky** is a rotating observation tower that lifts you over the grounds.

Cafés and restaurants line the main boulevards, which makes a meal on site easy. Staying on the grounds after the encore also lets the station queue thin out before you join it.
