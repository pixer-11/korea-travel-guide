## A violin where the lead guitar should be

LUCY doesn't sound like other bands. The South Korean group is built around a violin, and it often carries the melodies a guitar would play in most rock bands. The result is bright, fast and hard to mistake for anyone else.

The band came out of JTBC's band-building audition show *Super Band* in 2019. Since then it has grown a following for songs like "Flowering", which mix pop hooks with live, played-in-the-room energy. On December 4, 2026, that sound plays The Danforth Music Hall in Toronto.

## Inside The Danforth Music Hall

The building started life as a cinema in 1919. You can still read that history in its long, sloping room and the marquee hanging over Danforth Avenue. It's a mid-sized theatre, so even the back rows feel close to the stage.

For most shows the layout splits into two zones:

- **The floor:** usually standing room in front of the stage, where the most devoted fans gather
- **The balcony:** raked fixed seating with a clear sightline down to the stage

The ticket type tells you which zone you're in. Fans who want to be near the front of the floor tend to queue outside on the Danforth sidewalk well before doors open.

## Getting to Broadview

The hall sits at 147 Danforth Avenue, on the edge of Riverdale and Greektown. The easiest way there is the subway.

- **Broadview Station (Line 2 Bloor–Danforth):** the closest stop, a few minutes' walk east along Danforth
- **Chester Station (Line 2):** one stop further east, also walkable
- **Streetcars:** the 504 King runs north to Broadview Station

TTC fare gates take a tap of a contactless credit card or phone, so visitors rarely bother buying a separate transit card for one night. Street parking nearby is limited. Most concertgoers come by subway, which makes the station crowded around the end of the show.

A few habits attendees tend to follow:

- **Coats:** early December in Toronto is cold, so people layer up for the sidewalk queue and use the coat check or carry a light jacket once inside

## Before the lights go down

The Danforth Music Hall puts you right on one of Toronto's best restaurant strips. Greektown runs east along Danforth Avenue, with souvlaki counters, tavernas and bakeries a short walk from the hall. Many fans eat there before doors.

To the west, the Prince Edward Viaduct carries Bloor Street and the subway over the Don Valley. Its pedestrian deck gives a long view of the downtown skyline. Just south of the hall, Riverdale Park East has one of the city's classic skyline views from its hilltop, though after dark in December it's a cold, windy spot.
