## Who Israel & New Breed are

Israel Houghton formed New Breed in the 1990s as a multicultural worship band. It mixes gospel, funk, Latin rhythm and praise-and-worship choruses. The group's live albums became staples of contemporary Christian music, and Houghton has won several Grammy Awards.

## Singing it back: the Jakarta worship crowd

Indonesian worship crowds are loud and fully involved. Many fans grew up in charismatic and Pentecostal churches, where hands go up, people stand for the whole set, and choruses repeat until the worship leader stops them.

Expect the room to sing the English lyrics word for word. A worship band touring Asia usually leaves space for the crowd to carry a chorus alone. Some fans also sing a line in Bahasa Indonesia from local church translations.

Some people at a concert like this kneel or pray quietly between songs. Others dance through the up-tempo numbers. Both are normal, and nobody in the room treats either as strange.
