## Where it actually is

Start with the map. The listing files this museum under Franz Josef, but the address is 14 Rosina Corlett Lane, off Aerodrome Road, Omaka, Blenheim. That is the dry, sunny north-east of the South Island, not the rainforest-and-ice West Coast.

From Franz Josef the drive takes most of a day. Head north on State Highway 6 through Hokitika, Greymouth and the Buller Gorge. Then cut across the Wairau Valley on State Highway 63 to Renwick and Blenheim. If you are coming off the Interislander or Bluebridge ferry at Picton instead, it is roughly half an hour south.

## Inside the hangars

The museum is a working aerodrome hangar complex, and the collection is all about early air war. The best-known hall, Knights of the Sky, displays First World War aircraft, many of them from Sir Peter Jackson's collection. Weta Workshop staged them as frozen moments, with life-size figures mid-action.

What to look for:

- **The Red Baron scene**: the diorama of Manfred von Richthofen's final crash, with soldiers stripping the downed triplane.
- **Original and reproduction WWI fighters and scouts**, displayed beside cases of pilots' kit, letters and medals.
- **The WWII hall**: later aircraft and theatre-of-war settings, with the same diorama approach.

Allow two to three hours. Anyone who reads every caption will need longer. The lighting is low and dramatic in places, so phone photos work better with flash off and a steady hand.

## Beating the weekend crowd at Omaka

This is a well-known stop on Marlborough itineraries. Weekends can get crowded, and timing matters more than anything else you plan.

- **Avoid weekends 10am–2pm.** That is usually the busiest stretch, when the diorama halls fill up.
- **Weekend quiet windows:** 9am–10am and 3pm–4pm.
- **Weekdays:** quietest 3pm–4pm. With the doors currently closing at 4pm, that leaves only an hour, so a weekday morning start with a slower finish is often the better plan.

Hours are currently 9am–4pm every day of this week's schedule. Museum hours can shift around holidays, so check the official site before the long drive. Tickets are sold at the front desk, and current prices are posted there and online.

A practical tip: plenty of visitors call in on the way to or from the ferry. Leave luggage locked out of sight in the car, and plan the visit so you are not watching the clock against a sailing time.

## Fitting it into a Marlborough day

The aerodrome is a few minutes' drive from central Blenheim, so the museum pairs easily with the Wairau Valley vineyards around Renwick and Brancott Valley. Book a designated driver or a wine tour if tastings follow.

Every second Easter, in odd-numbered years, the aerodrome hosts the Classic Fighters airshow. Collection aircraft fly then, and Blenheim accommodation books out well ahead. Outside airshow weekends, the hangars are the main event.

Coming from Franz Josef, it makes sense to break the drive with a night in Westport, Murchison or Nelson Lakes. Then you reach Omaka at 9am rather than arriving tired at 3:30pm.
