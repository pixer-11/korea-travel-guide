## Two days of running on a Caribbean island

The dates are December 5–6, 2026. The Maya Marathon carries the theme "Run in the Jungle." It is held on Cozumel, the large flat island off the Yucatán coast. San Miguel de Cozumel is the town on its west shore.

Inland from the town, the island turns into low, dense scrub forest. That is the jungle in the race's name. Cozumel has no hills to speak of, so the challenge here is heat and humidity rather than climbing.

Early December falls at the start of the dry season, when mornings are warm and sticky. The sea breeze mostly stays on the coast. Under the trees the air is still.

## Reaching San Miguel

There are two ways onto the island:

- **Ferry from Playa del Carmen.** Passenger boats cross the channel and dock at the pier on the San Miguel waterfront, a short walk from the central plaza. The crossing takes under an hour, and the channel can be choppy.
- **Flight into Cozumel International Airport (CZM).** The airport sits just north of town, a few minutes from the centre by taxi.

Runners flying long-haul often land at Cancún and continue south to Playa del Carmen for the ferry. The drive down the coast takes roughly an hour.

## Race weekend in town

Most visitors stay in San Miguel itself. From the town you can walk to the ferry pier, the waterfront promenade along Avenida Rafael E. Melgar and the restaurants around the main plaza. The hotels further south along the coast road are quieter, but you'll need a taxi to get into town.

Registration, distances and the start location all sit with the organiser, not the island's tourism office. This guide has no figures for entry fees or start times. For the morning itself, runners usually pack:

- Electrolytes, since humidity under the canopy drains you faster than the temperature suggests
- Insect repellent for the forest sections
- Reef-safe sunscreen, which local operators expect on any beach or dive outing afterwards

The island makes for an easy recovery day. You can snorkel off the west-coast reefs, walk the waterfront at sunset or visit the Maya ruins at San Gervasio in the interior.

## Getting around Cozumel on race weekend

Cozumel's taxis are run by a local drivers' union and charge set fares by zone. Ride-hailing apps don't operate on the island. Drivers wait outside the ferry pier and the airport, and the fare is agreed before you set off.

Mexican pesos are the everyday currency. Hotels and larger restaurants take cards, while small taco stands and market stalls run on cash. A tip of 10–15 percent is customary in sit-down restaurants.

A little Spanish goes a long way with marshals and volunteers:

- **salida**: the start
- **meta**: the finish
- **abastecimiento**: an aid station
- **corredor / corredora**: runner
