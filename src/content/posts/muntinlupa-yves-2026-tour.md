## Inside The Filinvest Tent

The Tent is in Filinvest City, a planned district of offices, condos and wide boulevards in Alabang. The building is a large semi-permanent tented hall, not a stadium, so the stage stays fairly close even from the back sections.

Concerts, trade fairs and graduation ceremonies all use the space. It has a flat floor with temporary seating set up for each event, which means one show's layout can look nothing like the last. The organiser's seat plan for this concert is the one that counts.

Because it is a hall rather than an arena, the sound fills the room quickly. You can usually tell a crowd's fan chants from the stage audio at the same moment.

## Reaching Filinvest City

Alabang sits at the southern edge of Metro Manila. That changes the trip for anyone staying in Makati, BGC or around the airport.

- **By car or Grab:** Take the South Luzon Expressway (SLEX) and exit at Filinvest/Alabang. Most concertgoers come this way.
- **By bus:** Point-to-point (P2P) buses and provincial buses run between central Manila and Alabang. Transport terminals near Alabang Town Center and Festival Mall are a short ride from the Tent.
- **Parking:** Filinvest City has a lot of mall and building parking. Lots near the venue tend to fill on big event nights.

After the show, ride-hailing pickups around the Tent get crowded. Many fans walk to Festival Mall or along the Filinvest boulevards and get their ride there, where drivers can pull in more easily.

## Eating and staying nearby

Festival Mall (Festival Alabang) is the obvious place to eat before and after. It is a big mall within walking distance of the Tent, with food courts, chain restaurants and a riverside dining strip.

Alabang Town Center is a short drive away. It is more open-air, with sit-down restaurants and cafés. Both get busy on concert nights, so groups often eat at off-peak hours.

Fans travelling from outside Metro Manila usually stay in Alabang itself. Filinvest City and the area around Alabang Town Center have business hotels within a short ride of the venue. That saves a late-night drive north in traffic after the encore.
