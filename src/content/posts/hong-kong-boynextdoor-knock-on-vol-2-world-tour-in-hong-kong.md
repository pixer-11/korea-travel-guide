## Three nights in January

The run lasts three nights. BOYNEXTDOOR play Hong Kong from January 8 to 10, 2027, as one stop on the KNOCK ON Vol.2 World Tour. That gives the city one of the longer stays on an Asian K-pop tour schedule.

The group has six members: Sungho, Riwoo, Jaehyun, Taesan, Leehan and Woonhak. They are signed to KOZ Entertainment, the HYBE label founded by Zico. Their fandom is called ONEDOOR. Many of them follow the tour across more than one city, so you see a lot of overseas fans as well as Hong Kong locals.

## Dates, tickets and the hall

The dates are fixed: January 8, 9 and 10, 2027. Facts about the venue and pricing aren't part of what this guide holds, so neither appears here. The organiser publishes the hall, seat map, ticket tiers and sales links on its official site.

Hong Kong arena concerts normally sell through a single licensed ticketing platform rather than at a box office. Tickets are usually tied to a named buyer, and resale outside official channels is a common source of fake tickets. Fans who travel in for one night often choose the middle date, which gives them a buffer on both sides for flights.

## Reaching a Hong Kong arena

Nearly every large concert hall in Hong Kong is on the MTR. The Hong Kong Coliseum is above Hung Hom Station. AsiaWorld-Expo, beside the airport on Lantau, has its own stop on the Airport Express. Taxis are slow after big shows because thousands of fans leave at the same moment.

- **Octopus card:** works on the MTR, buses, ferries and in convenience stores.
- **Layers:** January evenings in Hong Kong are cool and dry, but the halls are warm once full.
- **Power bank:** queueing for merch and the train home drains phones fast.

Fans coming from abroad usually stay in Tsim Sha Tsui, Hung Hom or Mong Kok. All three are a short MTR ride from the Kowloon venues and cover the airport via the Airport Express or bus routes.

## Lightsticks, merch lines and the ride home

At Hong Kong K-pop shows the official lightstick is what the crowd uses. Fans usually pair it with the venue's Bluetooth sync so the whole arena changes colour together. Fan-made slogans and banners are handed out free near the entrances, mostly at the busy MTR exits.

Official merch booths usually open hours before doors, and the queues there are often longer than the entry line. People pay by card and by mobile wallets such as Octopus, Apple Pay and Alipay HK. Many fans buy their merch on the day before their own show to skip the crush.

Inside, staff are strict about recording rules and large camera lenses. Fan chants for each song circulate online before a tour starts, and the crowd follows them closely. Afterwards the MTR station fills up fast. Locals often walk one stop along before they get on, or stay for a late meal nearby while the platforms clear.
