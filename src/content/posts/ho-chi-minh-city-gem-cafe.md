## Why go

GEM Café is the café inside GEM Center, the large events and conference venue on Nguyễn Bỉnh Khiêm in the old District 1 of Ho Chi Minh City. It is not a corner coffee stop. The room is tall and bright, and the whole place feels like a step up from an ordinary café.

It suits an unhurried coffee with someone, a dessert date, or an afternoon when you want somewhere a little special.

## From our visit

Our editor, Pixer, visited in late November, as the winter season was starting. The first impression was of a café with class and a lovely atmosphere.

For the holidays, the room had been turned into a snowy winter scene. Frosted Christmas trees lined the walls, cotton snow and icicles hung from the ceiling, and dozens of silver and white baubles and mirror balls floated overhead. Tall white towers shaped like stacked snowballs stood in the middle of the hall, and a staircase lit from below glowed at the far end. It really did look as if snow had fallen indoors. The café calls its 2026 holiday concept The Frozen Dreamscape.

The ordering counter sits under the same snow, with a glass pastry case and a gelato case in front. We ordered a strawberry slush, an iced red drink with a cinnamon stick, a blue doughnut decorated with white sugar snowflakes and a burnt cheesecake topped with physalis, and took them to a small white table.

It is a very good date spot. It is also a photo spot: many of the guests on our visit, a lot of them young women, had come mainly to take pictures, some of them posing on the lit staircase.

## Getting there

GEM Center is at 8 Nguyễn Bỉnh Khiêm, in Sài Gòn Ward, in what used to be District 1. It is at the northern end of the old downtown, close to the Saigon Zoo and Botanical Gardens. A Grab car or bike is the easiest way to get there.

The café is inside the GEM Center building.

## Tips for your visit

If you want photos without a crowd, go earlier in the day, since the room fills up with people taking pictures.

The winter decoration is seasonal. Outside the holidays the room may look quite different, so check the café's social pages if the snowy setting is the reason you are going.

We did not confirm opening hours, and venues like this often change them around the holidays and private events. Check a map app before you go.
