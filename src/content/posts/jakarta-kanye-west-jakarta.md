## One night in Senayan

The date is October 24, 2026. American rapper Kanye West, who now goes by Ye, plays Gelora Bung Karno Main Stadium. Indonesia's largest stadium sits inside the GBK sports complex in Senayan, Central Jakarta.

The bowl opened for the 1962 Asian Games and was rebuilt for the 2018 edition. It now seats roughly 77,000. A steel ring roof covers the stands, but the pitch is open to the sky. That matters in October, when Jakarta's rainy season is starting.

## Getting to GBK past the Sudirman traffic

Jalan Sudirman jams on any evening, and a stadium crowd makes it worse. Rail is the reliable option.

- **MRT Jakarta (North–South Line):** Istora Mandiri station puts you on the eastern edge of the complex. Senayan station is one stop north.
- **TransJakarta Corridor 1:** the Gelora Bung Karno stop is on the Sudirman busway.
- **KRL Commuter Line:** Palmerah station, on the Rangkasbitung line, sits on the complex's western side and is walkable.
- **Gojek and Grab:** ride-hail pickups jam after big shows. Most people walk out to Senayan City or Plaza Senayan before ordering.

MRT and TransJakarta are cashless. Riders tap a bank e-money card (Flazz, e-money, Brizzi) or pay by QR in the operators' apps.

## Wristbands, gates and the walk in

Big stadium shows in Indonesia usually follow the same routine.

Tickets are often tied to the buyer's name, so fans carry the ID they bought with, such as a passport or KTP. Many promoters swap e-tickets for a physical wristband at a redemption booth before entry. Each ticket category goes in through a numbered gate (pintu), and the walk between gates around the stadium ring takes a while.

Security usually bans umbrellas, so fans bring a cheap plastic poncho. Outside food, power banks over a set size, and professional cameras are commonly refused too. Tall stalls along the plaza sell water and snacks. Vendors take QRIS payments as readily as cash, and most phones with Indonesian e-wallets or international QR apps can pay.

Locals say "GBK" (geh-beh-kah) for the whole complex. Drivers know the gate numbers better than street names.

## Around the stadium

You can walk to four big malls from the complex.

- **Plaza Senayan and Senayan City:** across Jalan Asia Afrika, with food courts and sit-down Indonesian chains. Fans usually eat here before the show.
- **FX Sudirman:** on the eastern side, near Istora Mandiri MRT.
- **Senayan Park (SPARK):** by the lake on the complex's western edge.

Inside GBK, the jogging track and Hutan Kota GBK, an urban forest park, fill the space around the stadium. These shaded paths are where crowds gather before gates open.

Out-of-town visitors usually stay right in Senayan or along the Sudirman corridor, in hotels such as Fairmont Jakarta, Hotel Mulia Senayan and The Sultan. From those, the stadium is a walk and not a taxi ride. Blok M, two MRT stops south, has cheaper rooms and late-night food streets. Trains still run between the two after the show, so getting back is easy.
