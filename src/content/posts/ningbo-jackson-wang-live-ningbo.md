## One night, one arena

Jackson Wang brings his solo show to Ningbo on December 6, 2026. The Hong Kong-Canadian artist plays Ningbo Olympic Sports Center Gymnasium, the indoor arena inside the city's Olympic sports complex.

Most international audiences first knew him from GOT7, the K-pop group he debuted with in 2014. His solo work leans harder into hip-hop and R&B. His 2022 album *Magic Man* turned him into a stadium-scale act across Asia, and that is the catalogue mainland fans come to hear.

Before music he fenced competitively for Hong Kong. That restless energy shows up in his shows, which mix sharp choreography with long stretches of him talking to the crowd in Mandarin, Cantonese and English.

## The gymnasium and getting to it

The gymnasium is the arena half of the Ningbo Olympic Sports Center. Its open plazas give queues room to spread out on concert nights.

Most visitors coming from outside the city arrive by rail:

- **From Shanghai:** high-speed trains to Ningbo Railway Station take roughly two hours.
- **From Hangzhou:** the trip is shorter, often under an hour on the fastest services.
- **By air:** Ningbo Lishe International Airport handles domestic and some regional flights.

From the station, attendees usually take the Ningbo Metro or a ride-hail car through DiDi. After the encore, the taxi queues at the complex get long. Many fans walk a few blocks away from the venue before hailing a car.

## Getting through the gate in Ningbo

Big concerts in mainland China use real-name ticketing. Each ticket carries the holder's name and ID number, and staff match it against your document at entry. Foreign visitors buy and enter with their passport. A ticket bought from a reseller in someone else's name is the most common reason fans get turned away.

The gate routine usually runs like this:

- **ID scan:** your passport or Chinese ID is checked against the ticket.
- **Security:** bags are screened, and power banks, lighters, aerosols and professional cameras are typically refused.
- **Lightsticks:** official fan lightsticks are normally allowed. Fans often trade handmade slogans and stickers outside the gates.

Payment around the venue runs almost entirely on Alipay and WeChat Pay. Both apps accept linked international cards. Vendors with only a cash drawer are rare, and many expect a QR scan.

## Staying over and making a weekend of it

Fans travelling in usually stay one or two nights rather than rushing for a late train. Hotels around the Sanjiangkou riverside, where the Yong, Yao and Fenghua rivers meet, put you near the old Bund-style waterfront and the Laowaitan bar strip.

Daytime options close to the centre:

- **Tianyi Pavilion:** a Ming-dynasty private library, among the oldest surviving in China.
- **Moon Lake:** a lakeside park with walking paths in the old town.
- **Ningbo Museum:** designed by Wang Shu, its walls are built from recycled bricks and tiles.

December in Ningbo is cool and damp, and evenings drop close to single digits Celsius. Queueing outside the gymnasium is colder than people expect, so concertgoers usually wear layers they can shed once inside.
