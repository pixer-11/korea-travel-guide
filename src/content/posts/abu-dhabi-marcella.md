## Fire on the waterfront

Marcella runs on two things: a chef's name and a wood fire. Mario Loi is chef-patron, so the concept is his and so is the kitchen. The menu is modern Iberian and Mediterranean, and meat and seafood go over wood.

Don't let the Italian-sounding name fool you. The reference points are Spain, Portugal and the wider Mediterranean coast, not pizza and pasta. Since opening it has become a busy go-to on Yas Bay. In September 2026 it adds a weekend brunch, which gives you a daytime way in.

## Getting to Yas Bay

Yas Bay Waterfront is a promenade of restaurants and bars next to Etihad Arena on Yas Island. Yas Marina Circuit and Yas Mall are a short drive away. Nearly everyone arrives by car or taxi.

- **From central Abu Dhabi:** around half an hour by taxi on the E12, depending on traffic.
- **From Zayed International Airport:** roughly 15 to 20 minutes by road.
- **From Dubai:** allow about an hour and a quarter on the E11.
- **Ride apps:** Uber and Careem both work across the island.
- **Parking:** the Yas Bay complex has parking, but it fills up quickly on arena event nights.

## Booking a table on Yas Island

People come here on purpose, so the tables fill. These habits help:

- **Reserve ahead,** and confirm the opening hours when you book. We weren't given a schedule, and a new restaurant can change its hours.
- **Check the Etihad Arena calendar.** On concert and fight nights, crowds and taxis pile up along the promenade before and after the show.
- **The UAE weekend is Saturday and Sunday.** The new brunch runs at weekends, but ask which day and sitting when you book.
- **Paying:** cards are standard across Yas Bay. UAE bills often include a service charge already, so read the bill before you tip. If no charge is listed, around 10 percent is the usual gesture.
- **Dress:** smart casual is the norm on the waterfront. Beachwear is out of place at dinner.
- **Season:** October to April is when evenings by the water are comfortable. From June to September the heat sends everyone indoors.

## Building a day around dinner

Yas Island is set up for a long day out, and Marcella makes a good last stop. By day, Ferrari World, Yas Waterworld and Warner Bros. World are all a few minutes away by taxi. After dinner, walk the length of the Yas Bay promenade, or head to Yas Marina, where boats tie up beside the circuit.

The Abu Dhabi Grand Prix, held at Yas Marina Circuit in late November or early December, packs the island. Restaurant bookings and hotel rooms go fast that week, so book well ahead or pick another week. For a gentler trip, go for weekday dinner in the cooler months, when you can get a table more easily and the waterfront is pleasant after dark.
