## Rainforest to reef under one roof

The route through the building follows water downhill. You start with the freshwater of Queensland's tropical north and end in the open ocean. Across 9 ecosystems it holds more than 16,000 specimens. For a first-time visitor that is a useful preview: the tanks show what the reef boats and rainforest tours will point at later.

It's one of the most-reviewed attractions in Cairns and consistently well rated. The whole visit is indoors and air-conditioned, so it works as a fallback on wet-season afternoons when boats stay in port. Give it two to three hours. Families with small children often take longer at the touch-level tanks.

## Finding Florence Street

The address is 5 Florence St, Cairns City, inside the compact CBD grid.

- **On foot:** most central hotels and hostels are a walk of under 15 minutes away. From the Esplanade, head inland along Florence St.
- **Pair it with:** the Esplanade Lagoon and boardwalk, a few blocks toward the water, and the Cairns Night Markets for dinner afterwards.
- **Reef day tie-in:** the Reef Fleet Terminal is at the southern end of the Esplanade. The aquarium makes an easy half-day before an early boat or after a late return.

The aquarium is open daily from 9:30am to 3:30pm. Check the official Cairns Aquarium website before you go, including on public holidays.

## Timing the tanks

Our crowd data is clear about when to go. On weekdays, foot traffic stays light across the whole 9:30am–3:30pm stretch, so any time of day works.

Weekends are harder. Try not to arrive between 11am and 2pm. That's when family groups fill the galleries and the eatery gets crowded. Go for the first part of the morning or come after 2pm, and you'll have more room at the glass for photos without other people's reflections.

## Getting more from your visit

A few habits help here.

- **Book online first.** Current ticket prices and any combo or annual-pass options are listed on the official website. Buying ahead skips the desk queue on busy weekends.
- **Check the daily talks and feeds.** Scheduled presentations run through the day, and planning your route around one is the easiest way to see the animals moving. Ask for the schedule when you enter.
- **Ask about guided experiences.** Behind-the-scenes tours and after-hours programs are advertised on the aquarium's site. They sell out, so book ahead if one interests you.
- **Eat with a view.** The on-site eatery looks straight into the aquarium, so time lunch for after the weekend rush, once 2pm has passed.
- **Paying:** card and tap-to-pay are standard across Australia, and tipping isn't expected at the counter.
- **Glass etiquette:** switch off your flash. It bounces off the acrylic, ruins the shot and stresses the animals.

If you're heading to the reef, come here first. Once you've seen the fish up close in the tanks, you'll recognise them far more easily through a snorkel mask.
