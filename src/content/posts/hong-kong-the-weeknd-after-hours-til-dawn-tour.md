Kai Tak Stadium is Hong Kong's newest large-scale venue, built on the site of the former Kai Tak Airport in Kowloon. ## Getting to Kai Tak Stadium

The stadium sits in the Kai Tak development, on the Kowloon side of the harbour, not far from Kowloon Bay and Kowloon City.

- MTR: Kai Tak Station on the Tuen Ma line has exits that feed directly into the Kai Tak development; from there it's a signposted walk to the stadium.
- Taxi or ride-hail: drop-off points are set up around the stadium perimeter on event nights, but expect long queues immediately after the show ends.
- From Hong Kong Island: the cross-harbour tunnel routes get heavy on show nights, so budget extra time if you're coming from Causeway Bay, Wan Chai or Central.

Arriving by MTR rather than car is generally the less stressful option for a stadium show of this size, purely because road access funnels through a limited number of points once tens of thousands of people are trying to leave at once.

Locals heading to big Kai Tak shows tend to arrive with time to spare rather than right at doors, since the walk from the MTR station through security can take longer than expected once thousands of people are moving the same direction.

- Buy an Octopus card (Hong Kong's tap-to-pay transit card) before the night if you don't already have one; it works on the MTR and at most stadium concession stands, and skips the fumbling for exact change.
