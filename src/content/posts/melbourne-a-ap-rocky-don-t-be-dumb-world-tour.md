## Three nights at Melbourne Park

A$AP Rocky stays in Melbourne for more than one night. The Don't Be Dumb World Tour plays Rod Laver Arena across multiple nights from December 5 to 7, 2026, so this is a residency-style stop rather than one show.

That gives you options. Each night is a separate ticketed show. Melbourne fans often pick a date to suit their weekend plans, since early December lands in the run-up to Christmas and the start of summer.

## The room on the river

Rod Laver Arena is the centre court of the Australian Open. Every January it hosts the tennis, and for the rest of the year it hosts concerts. For a show, the floor is cleared for a stage at one end. Steep tiered seating rises around the remaining sides, so even the upper rows look down onto the stage instead of across at it.

The arena sits inside Melbourne Park, the sports precinct beside the Yarra River on the CBD's eastern edge. Neighbours include:

- **Margaret Court Arena** and **John Cain Arena**, next door in the same precinct
- **The MCG** and **Yarra Park**, just across the rail lines
- **Birrarung Marr**, the riverside park leading back toward Federation Square

## Getting in and out

Most fans leave the car at home. The precinct sits next to the city's busiest rail corridor, and post-show traffic on Batman Avenue and Olympic Boulevard is slow.

- **Train:** Richmond and Jolimont stations are both a walk away. Flinders Street Station is about 15 minutes on foot along the river through Birrarung Marr.
- **Tram:** Route 70 runs along Flinders Street and Wellington Parade and stops at Rod Laver Arena on Olympic Boulevard.
- **Walking:** From Federation Square, the riverside path is flat, lit and well signed for Melbourne Park.

Public transport runs on the myki card or contactless payment. Riders tap on when they board trams and pass through the station gates. After the encore, crowds head mostly toward Richmond and Flinders Street. Jolimont is often the calmer platform.
