## Humo Arena: where the stage goes up

The concert is held at Humo Arena, Tashkent's largest indoor venue. It was built for ice hockey and is home ice for the Humo Tashkent club. The name comes from the Humo bird, the mythical bird on Uzbekistan's state emblem.

For concerts, the floor becomes a standing area and seated tiers ring the bowl. That gives you two different nights:
- **Floor:** close to the stage, on your feet the whole show, with the densest crowd.
- **Tiers:** a full view of the light show and a seat for the quieter songs.

Most people get there by Yandex Go, the ride-hailing app Tashkent runs on. The app displays the fare up front, which is useful when demand peaks after the show. The Tashkent Metro is also an option for the trip there. Its Soviet-era stations, such as Kosmonavtlar, are worth seeing on another day of your stay.
