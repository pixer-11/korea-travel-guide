Rolo's sits in Ridgewood, a Queens neighborhood right on the border with Bushwick, Brooklyn, known for its rowhouses, industrial-turned-creative spaces, and a growing wave of destination restaurants and bars. Ridgewood is best reached via the M train, which runs along Metropolitan Avenue through the neighborhood — get off at one of the Ridgewood stops (Forest Avenue, Seneca Avenue, or Fresh Pond Road, depending on which is closest to your exact address) and expect a short walk. Given the volume of reviews and its Bib Gourmand status, Rolo's draws steady crowds, especially on Friday and Saturday nights.
