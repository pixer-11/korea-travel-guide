## The view from 520 feet

The trip up takes under a minute. Skylon's yellow glass elevators run up the outside of the concrete shaft, so the falls come into view while you're still rising. At the top, the observation level stands about 520 feet above the street. The Horseshoe Falls fill the frame on your left, and the American and Bridal Veil Falls sit across the gorge.

The deck has two levels. One is indoor and glassed, and the other is open-air behind safety fencing. On a clear day you can see Lake Ontario, and on the best days the Toronto skyline as well. The tower opened in 1965, and the same flying-saucer pod has been on the Niagara skyline ever since.

## Getting up Robinson Street

The tower sits at 5200 Robinson St in the Fallsview area, uphill from the river. Ways in:

- **On foot:** about ten minutes up from the Clifton Hill strip, or uphill from Table Rock along the Niagara Parkway.
- **Falls Incline Railway:** the short funicular links Table Rock with the Fallsview ridge, which saves you the climb.
- **WEGO bus:** the city's visitor bus network stops in the Fallsview area.
- **Driving:** there's paid parking at the base. The rate is posted at the lot entrance.

For the deck alone, allow 45 minutes to an hour. If you're eating upstairs, allow about two hours.

## Timing the queue for the lifts

The lifts carry a limited number of people, so the line at the base is the real bottleneck. The quietest windows:

- **Weekdays:** 9am–11am and 9pm–11pm
- **Weekends:** 9am–11am and 10pm–11pm

Try not to arrive on a weekend between 12pm and 10pm, because that's when the lines build. Right now the tower opens at 9am and closes at 11pm, staying open to midnight on Saturdays. Observation decks change their hours with the seasons, so check this month's times on the official site.

The late slot pays off. The falls are lit with coloured lights every night after dark, and in summer there are scheduled fireworks over the gorge. Both look best from directly above.

## Eating in the clouds, and how locals play it

Two restaurants sit above the deck. The Revolving Dining Room turns a full circle about once an hour, so the view moves past your table during a sit-down meal. The Summit Suite, one floor up, is the buffet option. The tower is very popular, so book ahead for sunset tables, particularly on summer weekends.

Some practical points:

- **Dining includes the ride:** a restaurant reservation covers the trip to the top. If you were going to buy deck tickets and eat nearby anyway, compare the two options before you pay.
- **Paying:** cards are accepted everywhere. Current ticket and menu prices are posted at the base and on the official site.
- **Window seats:** ask for one when you book. The tables on the inner ring see less.
- **The arcade at the base:** it's loud and full of kids. Go straight through to the elevator line.
- **Weather check:** look up before you buy. Low cloud or mist from the falls can hide the view, and on those days it's worth waiting a few hours.

The Fallsview casino strip and the Clifton Hill attractions are both within walking distance. You can pair the tower with the Journey Behind the Falls at Table Rock, which shows you the same water from underneath.
