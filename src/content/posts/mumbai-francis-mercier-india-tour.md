## Christmas Eve, Afro house, Mumbai

Francis Mercier is back in India this December. The Haitian-American DJ and producer brings a five-city tour to the country, and his Mumbai date falls on December 24, 2026. That puts his set on the busiest party night of the city's year.

Mercier comes out of Afro house. That means long, percussive builds, warm basslines and vocals drawn from across the African diaspora. He also founded the label Deep Root Records, which releases the same sound. Expect a set made for steady dancing, not big drops every few minutes.

The tour runs through five cities:

- Mumbai (December 24, 2026)
- Bengaluru
- Delhi NCR

## Getting through Mumbai on December 24

Christmas Eve traffic in Mumbai is heavy, especially around Bandra and South Mumbai. Many attendees skip the roads and ride Metro Line 3, the underground Aqua Line. It runs north to south between Aarey JVLR and Cuffe Parade, with stops at BKC, Worli and Siddhivinayak along the way.

For the trip home after the set, most people book an Uber or Ola. Surge pricing is normal after midnight on a holiday night. The suburban trains stop for several hours overnight. Visitors who only come for the show usually stay in Bandra, Lower Parel or Worli, so the ride home is short.

December is the dry season and evenings are mild. One light layer covers an outdoor venue.

## Doors, bars and the dance floor

Mumbai crowds tend to arrive late. Rooms usually fill up in the hour before the headliner, and the openers play to the early arrivals. Fans who want a spot near the booth usually come in during the support sets.

Paying inside works like this:

- UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) work almost everywhere
- Cards are widely accepted at bars
- Many events use cashless wristbands or token counters, topped up at a desk

Maharashtra has strict drinking-age laws, and bars at events check ID again. On a crowded holiday night, the bar queue is usually longer than the one at the gate.

For Afro house sets, people tend to dress smart-casual. Shoes you can dance in for hours matter more than anything else. Filming the whole set on your phone is frowned upon. A short clip of the drop is fine.
