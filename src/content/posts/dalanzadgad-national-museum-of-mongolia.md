## Ulaanbaatar, not the Gobi

Get this right before you plan your days. The museum is listed under Dalanzadgad, but its address is Travel Street 1 (Juulchin Gudamj), Chingeltei district, Ulaanbaatar. Dalanzadgad, the capital of Ömnögovi province, is a short domestic flight or a long day's drive south.

Almost every Gobi trip passes through the capital, so slot the museum into a transit day. It sits a few minutes' walk northwest of Sükhbaatar Square, so you can walk there from most central hotels. In Mongolian it is the Mongolyn Ündesniy Muzei.

## What the galleries cover

It is Mongolia's main history museum. Galleries run in order across several floors, from the Stone Age to the present. The broad sweep:

- **Prehistory and early states:** stone tools, rock-art material and finds from the Xiongnu and the Turkic khaganates.
- **The Mongol Empire:** armour, weapons and documents from Chinggis Khaan's era and the empire that followed.
- **Ethnography:** traditional dress and headdresses of Mongolia's many ethnic groups, with the elaborate silver hair ornaments of married Khalkh women.
- **Nomadic life:** a furnished ger with saddles, tack and household objects.
- **The 20th century:** the socialist period and the 1990 democratic revolution.

Once you are out on the steppe, the nomadic galleries make sense of what you see. Felt-covered gers, horse gear and the way a camp is laid out all start to read clearly.

Dinosaurs are the gap. The fossils found at Bayanzag (the Flaming Cliffs) near Dalanzadgad are mostly shown at the Central Museum of Mongolian Dinosaurs, a separate building a short walk from Sükhbaatar Square. If the Gobi's fossil beds are on your route, pair the two museums.

## Fitting it around the flight

Domestic flights link Chinggis Khaan International Airport and Dalanzadgad. The paved road south takes most of a day. Two ways to fit the museum in:

- **Before the Gobi:** spend a morning here and fly or drive south the next day. The ethnography halls are a good primer for nights in a ger camp.
- **After the Gobi:** visit on your return day. This works well if you land early.

Plan around the current schedule. The museum is closed Monday and open 9am–6pm Tuesday through Sunday. Museum hours in Mongolia shift with the season and around holidays, notably Tsagaan Sar and Naadam, so check the official site for this month's times. A Monday arrival from the Gobi means waiting until Tuesday.

## At the ticket desk and inside

The process is simple. Buy your entry at the desk by the entrance. Photography usually needs a separate permit bought at the same desk, so ask before you raise your camera. Prices are posted at the desk. Carry some tögrög in case the card machine is down.

Weekends are the easy choice. Crowd data shows Saturday and Sunday quiet across the full 9am–6pm day, so there is no early-morning rush to beat. School groups are more likely on weekdays during term.

- From late autumn to spring, leave heavy coats in the cloakroom. Indoor heating is strong.
- Start on the ground floor with prehistory and work upwards. The galleries are laid out in date order.
- Allow at least an hour for the empire and ethnography floors, which hold the most detail.
