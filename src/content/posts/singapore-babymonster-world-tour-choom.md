## Eating before and after the show

Kallang Wave Mall sits right next to the Indoor Stadium. It is the default pre-show stop, with a food court, chain restaurants and supermarket snacks. It is also air-conditioned, which matters after an hour in an outdoor merch queue.

For a proper hawker meal, **Old Airport Road Food Centre** is two stops away at Dakota MRT (Circle Line, CC8). It is one of the city's best-known hawker centres, with long rows of stalls selling fried Hokkien mee, satay and rojak.

At hawker centres, locals "chope" a table by leaving a tissue packet on it. Leave that seat alone. Most stalls take cash, and many also accept PayNow or card.

After the last encore, many fans skip the scrum at Stadium station. They walk along the Kallang waterfront for a late supper, giving the trains time to clear.
