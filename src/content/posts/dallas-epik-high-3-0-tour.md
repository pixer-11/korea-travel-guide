An Epik High show runs on two engines. Tablo and Mithra Jin trade verses at the front of the stage, and DJ Tukutz works the decks behind them. The crowd ends up doing a good share of the vocal work, because so many of the hooks were built for it.

- "Fly", the 2005 single that broke them into the mainstream
