## The Russian Market backdrop

Toul Tom Poung, universally nicknamed the Russian Market, is one of Phnom Penh's oldest shopping grounds. Its narrow aisles sell everything from silk scarves to motorbike parts, and by late afternoon the surrounding streets shift from market chaos to a quieter grid of cafes and guesthouses.

Sundown Social Club sits within that shift. You climb up and away from the ground-level bustle, and the neighbourhood's tin roofs and tangled wires spread out below instead of pressing in around you.

It's a short tuk-tuk ride from most guesthouses in the BKK1 or Toul Tom Poung areas, and worth pairing with a market wander earlier in the day before the heat and crowds peak.

Arrive a little before that point if you want a seat with a clear sightline, since rooftop bars in popular expat circuits tend to fill as the light starts changing. Staying past sunset usually rewards you with a calmer, more sociable second wind once the after-work crowd thins out.
