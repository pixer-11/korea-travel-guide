## A toilet-brush pit in Tbilisi

Gutalax plays Junkyard Club in Tbilisi on December 12, 2026. The band comes from the Czech Republic and plays goregrind, a form of grindcore built on blast beats, guttural vocals and songs that often end inside two minutes.

The name is a pun on a laxative brand, and the whole act runs on toilet humour. Toilet brushes get waved overhead. Rolls of toilet paper fly across the room. Plenty of fans wear white disposable hazmat-style overalls. It is loud, filthy and mostly good-natured.
