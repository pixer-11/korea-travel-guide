## Getting to Axiata Arena

Axiata Arena sits inside the Bukit Jalil National Sports Complex in southern Kuala Lumpur, alongside Bukit Jalil National Stadium and Stadium Putra — so on show night expect the whole precinct to be busy, not just the arena gates.

- **By train:** Bukit Jalil LRT station (Kelana Jaya Line) is the most direct option, roughly a 10–15 minute walk to the arena via the covered link bridge.
