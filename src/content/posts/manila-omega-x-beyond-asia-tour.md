## A first Asia tour, with a Manila night

This is OMEGA X's first tour across Asia. The group is a South Korean boy band that debuted in 2021 with the single "Vamos", and "Beyond" is the title of the tour. Manila gets it on Saturday, November 28, 2026.

The Philippines has one of the most committed K-pop audiences in Southeast Asia. Metro Manila is a standard stop for Korean acts touring the region, and fans here have followed OMEGA X closely since debut. A first regional tour means many people in the crowd are seeing the group live for the first time.

## Concert day the way Manila fans do it

Concert day here starts hours before doors open. These are the usual habits:

- **Freebies.** Fans hand out photocards, stickers and printed fan kits outside the venue. It is a big part of the day, and many people bring a small bag of their own to trade.
- **Merch lines.** These form well before doors. Attendees usually go to merch first and queue for entry later.
- **Getting there.** Most people ride Grab or take a taxi. Commuters use the MRT-3 and LRT lines to get close, then walk the rest. After the show, ride-hailing surge pricing is normal, and groups often walk a few blocks away from the venue to request a car.
- **Paying.** Cash and GCash both work at street stalls and small vendors near Manila venues. Official merch booths usually take cards as well.
- **Bag checks.** Security at Manila arenas usually searches bags at the gate. Fans tend to carry light, and lightsticks get their own pouch.

Fan chants and slogan banners are a big part of the show. Many attendees learn the group's official fanchants before the night.

## The long weekend around November 28

The timing helps visitors. Monday, November 30 is Bonifacio Day, a national holiday, so the concert opens a three-day weekend. Out-of-town fans usually turn it into a short trip, and Metro Manila hotels fill faster than on an ordinary weekend.

Late November is also the end of the month, and Christmas season in the Philippines is in full swing by then. Mall traffic in Pasay, Makati and Quezon City gets heavy, especially around the payday at the end of the month. Visitors usually stay near the venue to keep the trip home short.

The weather is changeable. November is the tail of the rainy season, and late typhoons do reach Luzon in some years. A compact umbrella or rain jacket is standard for anyone waiting outside.
