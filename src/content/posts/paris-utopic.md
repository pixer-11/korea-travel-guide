Anne-Sophie Pic has not had a Paris address since La Dame de Pic closed, and that absence is exactly why Utopic matters. She's one of the very few female chefs in the world holding multiple Michelin stars, and her return to the capital didn't start with a dining room. It started with a bar, inside the Fondation Cartier.

That sequencing is deliberate. Utopic is the aperitif to something bigger: her full restaurant is opening in the same building, and this cocktail bar is the buzzy, lower-stakes way in first. If you've been waiting for Pic to plant a flag back in Paris, this is the earliest you can taste it.

## What makes the drinks different here

Pic built her cooking reputation on aromatic precision, and that's exactly what's been carried over into the glass. Her signature move is steeping ingredients into one another rather than simply mixing or garnishing, so a spirit might carry the scent of something that was never poured into it directly.

The result is a cocktail list that reads less like a bar menu and more like a small experiment in smell and memory. Expect drinks built around infusion and extraction rather than syrups and shakes.

- Steeped, not stirred: ingredients are infused into spirits before the drink is ever built
- Aroma-first construction, a direct carryover from her plating philosophy
- A menu that changes as the concept beds in, so ask what's currently pouring

## The Fondation Cartier address

Location shapes the whole experience. The Fondation Cartier is one of Paris's major contemporary art institutions, known for its glass-walled architecture and rotating exhibitions, and Utopic operates from inside that building rather than as a standalone bar on a random street corner.

That means an evening here can realistically be paired with whatever exhibition is currently showing next door. Check what's on before you go: a gallery visit followed by a drink built around Pic's aromatic technique is a natural double-header, and it's part of what's making this opening feel like an event rather than just another bar launch.

## Why it's the talk of the moment

A few things are colliding at once here: a star chef re-entering the Paris scene, a culinary technique translated into drinks for the first time, and a location inside a landmark art foundation rather than a typical bar district. That combination is why Utopic is being talked about well beyond the usual cocktail crowd right now.

It also functions as a signal. Utopic is small-scale and lower-commitment compared to a full restaurant launch, which makes it a way for Pic to re-establish her Paris presence before the main event opens in the same building. If you want to say you were there before the restaurant took over the conversation, this is the window.

## Booking a table before word spreads further

Given the attention around the opening, treat this like a reservation-first venue rather than a walk-in bar. Because Utopic is newly opened and tied to a high-profile name, demand is likely to outpace a first-come approach, especially on evenings when the Fondation Cartier has a new exhibition drawing its own crowd.

- Book ahead rather than assuming you can walk in
- Confirm current opening hours directly before you go, since early-stage venues often adjust their schedule
- Ask when you arrive whether the cocktail list is fixed or evolving, since a bar this new may still be tweaking pours

## Ordering like you know what you're drinking

The novelty here is technique, so it's worth engaging with it rather than defaulting to whatever's most familiar on the list. Ask the bartender what's been steeped into what: part of the appeal of Pic's approach is that the aroma often doesn't match your first guess at the ingredients.

Tipping and payment in Paris follow standard French bar norms: card is widely accepted, service is typically included in the price, and a small extra tip in cash is a courtesy rather than an obligation. Dress leans toward what you'd wear to a contemporary art opening rather than a casual pub, given the setting inside the Fondation.

## Pairing it with the rest of the visit

Utopic works best as one stop in an evening rather than the entire plan. Arriving before an exhibition closes lets you see the art first and talk about it over a drink after, which is really the sequence the space seems designed for.

If Pic's full restaurant has opened in the building by the time you visit, it's worth asking at Utopic whether same-night reservations are possible, since the bar and restaurant sharing an address may eventually mean shared logistics. Until then, Utopic stands on its own as the clearest, most accessible way to experience her return to Paris.
