## The chef behind the booking frenzy

Gaggan Anand's reputation was built in Bangkok. His original restaurant, Gaggan, was ranked number one on Asia's 50 Best Restaurants list for four consecutive years, from 2015 to 2018. He spent time in the research kitchen of elBulli, the Spanish restaurant that pioneered modernist techniques.

His style is usually described as progressive Indian cuisine. It takes familiar Indian flavours and reworks them through playful, often theatrical presentation. At his Bangkok restaurants, menus have been served as long tasting sequences, sometimes written in emoji rather than dish names.

None of that is a promise about what Raga serves. It is useful context, though. People booking here are mostly booking the chef, and knowing his track record explains why seats are so sought after.

## What's in a name: Raga

In Indian classical music, a raga is a melodic framework. It is a set of notes, phrases and moods that a musician improvises within, so no two performances are identical even when the raga is the same.

It is a fitting word for a chef who works inside the grammar of Indian food while refusing to play it straight. Whether the restaurant leans on the musical idea in its menu or room is something to discover when you sit down. We have not been told, so we are not guessing.

## Finding it in a three-city sprawl

Delhi NCR (the National Capital Region) is not one city. It covers Delhi itself plus satellite cities including Gurugram and Noida, and crossing between them can take well over an hour in traffic. Before anything else, confirm exactly which part of NCR Raga is in.

Once you have the address, a few practical points apply across the region:

- **Delhi Metro** is the fastest way to beat road traffic. The Yellow Line links central Delhi with Gurugram, and the Blue Line runs out to Noida.
- **App-based cabs** such as Uber and Ola work throughout NCR and are the easiest door-to-door option after dinner.
- **From the airport**, the Airport Express (Orange) Line runs from Indira Gandhi International Airport into central Delhi.

## Picking your season

Delhi's weather shapes any visit. October to March is the most comfortable stretch, with cooler evenings that suit a long dinner and a stroll afterwards. Winter can bring heavy smog, though, so check air-quality readings if you are sensitive to them.

May and June are fiercely hot, with daytime temperatures often above 40°C. The monsoon follows from roughly July to September, and heavy rain can snarl traffic across NCR. Factor this into travel time if your booking falls in those months.
