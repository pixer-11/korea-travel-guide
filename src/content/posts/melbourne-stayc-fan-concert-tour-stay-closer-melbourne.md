## What a fan concert looks like

A fan concert works differently from a standard concert tour. K-pop fan concerts usually mix live performances with:

- talk segments between songs, often with members switching between Korean and English
- games and challenges on stage
- moments that reference the fandom directly, such as fan-submitted messages or fan-voted songs

Expect the room to sing along. STAYC's fandom is called SWITH. The audience usually skews young, and families with teenage fans are a normal sight. The show runs on audience interaction as much as choreography.
