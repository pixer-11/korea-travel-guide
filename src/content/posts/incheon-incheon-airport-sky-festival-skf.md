## Three nights of K-pop beside the runways

The 2026 edition runs from November 7 to 9. The Incheon Airport Sky Festival puts exclusive K-pop concerts at the country's main international gateway, and the audience is travellers as much as locals. Plenty of visitors schedule the show around a flight in or out.

Incheon International Airport stands on reclaimed land on Yeongjong Island, west of the Incheon city centre. Out here you get flat coastal ground, wide skies and aircraft on approach overhead. The festival takes its name from that setting. It is organised in connection with the airport itself, and the organiser publishes the lineup, stage details and ticket links on its official channels.

## Reaching Yeongjong Island from Seoul

The festival offers transport packages from Seoul, which is how many out-of-town fans travel. Without a package, the usual route is the train.

- **AREX all-stop train:** leaves from Seoul Station and stops at Hongik University and Gimpo Airport on the way. It continues to Incheon Airport Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 stations.
- **AREX express:** goes nonstop from Seoul Station to the airport. It is a separately ticketed service and is faster than the all-stop.
- **Airport limousine buses:** connect the terminals with most Seoul districts and with hotels.
- **By car:** you cross either the Yeongjong Bridge or the Incheon Bridge. Both are tolled.

Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 are a few kilometres apart and linked by a free shuttle bus. Which terminal is nearest your concert area is worth knowing, because the walk between them is long.

## Staying near the airport

Lots of attendees sleep on the island so they don't face the late train back to Seoul. Paradise City and other resort hotels sit right beside the terminals. Business hotels near Unseo Station on the AREX line are a cheaper option.

Daytime visitors usually head to Eurwangni Beach on the island's west coast for seafood restaurants and a walk on the sand. November is off-season there, and the beach is quiet.

November nights on the Yellow Sea coast are cold and windy. Fans at the outdoor sets usually wear proper coats and bring gloves.

## Concert habits at SKF

Korean concert crowds follow a few habits, and fitting in with them makes the night easier.

- **Lightsticks:** fans bring each group's official lightstick, and the crowd is often a sea of matching colours.
- **Fan chants:** many songs have set chants, shouted during instrumental breaks. Groups' fandoms post them online.
- **Paying:** cards and mobile payments work almost everywhere at the airport. A T-money card covers the AREX all-stop and the buses with a tap.
- **Leaving:** crowds tend to pack the AREX platforms straight after the final act. Many people wait in the terminal food courts until it clears.

Lots of international fans leave their luggage at the terminal's baggage storage counters before they enter. Since it's an airport, those counters are part of the building.
