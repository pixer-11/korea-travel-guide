## A K-pop New Year at the Kallang waterfront

Midnight in Kallang gets a soundtrack this year. On 31 December 2026, the Kallang Countdown hands its New Year's Eve stage to Highlight, the South Korean group whose four members have been performing together since 2009. The concert is held at Stadium Roar, inside the sports precinct around the National Stadium dome.

The show builds through the evening and ends with the turn into 2027. That makes it a different night from the crowds packed around Marina Bay. It is a ticketed concert crowd facing a stage rather than a promenade crowd facing the water.

## Who plays: Highlight and the local bill

Highlight began life as BEAST, one of the defining second-generation K-pop acts. The group took its current name in 2017. Its four members are Yoon Doo-joon, Yang Yo-seob, Lee Gi-kwang and Son Dong-woon. Their fans call themselves Light, and a headline set from this group usually means a crowd that knows every fan chant.

The rest of the lineup leans Singaporean:

- **Yung Raja**, the Singapore rapper known for blending English and Tamil verses
- **Sobs**, the Singapore indie-pop band
- **Mimifly**
- **The Freshman**

Read that list as a full evening rather than one act with warm-ups. Many attendees come for the homegrown names as much as for the headliner.

## Getting to Stadium Roar

The MRT is the easy answer. Stadium MRT (CC6) on the Circle Line opens straight onto the Sports Hub grounds, and the walk to the stadium side takes only a few minutes. Kallang MRT (EW10) on the East-West Line is a longer walk via the Kallang riverside paths. It is the station people often use to avoid the Stadium platform crush.

Taxis and ride-hailing cars are a hard sell on New Year's Eve. Roads around the precinct and the city centre fill up, and surge pricing on Grab is normal after midnight. Most concertgoers ride the train in and walk out to a quieter street before calling a car.

## Tickets, entry and what to expect inside

The Kallang Countdown organiser handles ticketing and entry conditions. Prices and seating categories are not given here. Your ticket and the organiser's terms set what is allowed through the gates.

Expect bag checks at entry, as at most Sports Hub concerts. Large umbrellas, outside food and professional cameras are commonly restricted at Singapore stadium shows. Fans generally carry light: phone, card, power bank, and a lightstick if they own one.

December falls in Singapore's northeast monsoon season. Short, heavy downpours are common, and the air stays warm and humid after dark. An open-air crowd that size holds the heat.

## Counting down with the Light crowd

A K-pop show has its own customs, and it helps to know them.

- **Lightsticks:** Highlight has its own official lightstick, and a sea of them glowing at midnight is part of the spectacle. You don't need one, but holders often sync up during the headline set.
- **Fan chants:** Lights shout members' names in set places during songs. Joining in is welcome, and staying quiet is fine too.
- **Filming:** Phones go up for the countdown itself. Through the rest of the set, people near you will thank you for keeping your screen below head height.
- **Rain gear:** People bring ponchos rather than umbrellas, because an open umbrella blocks the view for everyone behind it.

Paying around the event is mostly cashless. Singapore's MRT gates take contactless bank cards and phones directly through SimplyGo, so you can skip the queue at the ticket machine. In previous years, train operators have run extended services on New Year's Eve. Fans who stay for the final song still usually face long, slow-moving queues back at Stadium station.

## Before and after midnight in Kallang

The precinct gives you places to fill time before the show. Kallang Wave Mall sits beside the stadium and has food courts and casual chains. Many attendees eat there rather than queueing at concession stands. The Kallang Basin waterfront makes for a flat, breezy walk.

After the show, the rest of the city is still celebrating. Marina Bay is a few MRT stops away on the Circle Line. Geylang Serai and Old Airport Road are close by for supper, and some hawker stalls in the area stay busy past midnight on New Year's Eve.
