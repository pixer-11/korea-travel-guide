## Lightsticks, chants and the floor

K-pop crowds come with their own customs. Knowing them makes the night far more fun.

- **Lightsticks.** Fans of most groups carry an official lightstick and hold it up through the set. In a room this size, a few hundred of them light up the brick.
- **Fan chants.** Many songs have set chants: members' names shouted in order, or lines called back between verses. Fans often learn them from chant guide videos before the show.
- **Slogans and banners.** Small printed slogans held up during a particular song are common. Big banners or signs on sticks block the view in a standing room, and fans generally keep things handheld.
- **Merch.** Tour merchandise is usually sold at the venue, and merch queues at K-pop shows often form well before the music starts.
- **Floor manners.** On a standing floor, people hold their spot rather than pushing forward once the show begins. Phones go up for favourite songs, and fans tend to drop them for the rest.

Payment at London bars and merch stands is overwhelmingly by contactless card or phone. Tipping at a club bar isn't expected.
