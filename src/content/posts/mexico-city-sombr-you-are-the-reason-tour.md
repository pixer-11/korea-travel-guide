## One night on Insurgentes Sur

The date is Friday, January 29, 2027. Sombr, the New York singer-songwriter whose aching, synth-washed breakup songs took over phones and playlists, brings his You Are the Reason world tour to Pepsi Center WTC. This is his Mexico City stop.

Expect a room full of people who know every word. Songs like "back to friends" and "undressed" grew up on short-form video, so the crowd tends to sing the hooks back louder than the PA. The tour has not published a setlist or an opening act for this show, and this guide doesn't guess at either.

## Inside the WTC complex

Pepsi Center WTC is an indoor arena built into the World Trade Center Mexico. That's the tall glass tower that dominates the skyline of Colonia Nápoles in Benito Juárez borough. The arena sits at the base of the complex, so it's tucked into the convention-centre block rather than standing on its own.

Next door is the Polyforum Cultural Siqueiros. Its faceted outer walls are covered in David Alfaro Siqueiros murals, and inside it holds his enormous "La Marcha de la Humanidad". Plenty of concertgoers use that painted building as their meeting point. Nobody misses it.

Inside, the layout is a general-admission floor facing the stage, with tiered seating rising around it. Sightlines from the stands are close by arena standards.

## Getting to Colonia Nápoles

The easiest route runs straight down Insurgentes Sur. **Metrobús Line 1**, the red articulated buses in their own centre lane, stops at **Poliforum**, almost directly in front of the complex. From Roma, Condesa or Zona Rosa, it's a straight ride south with no transfers.

Other options people use:

- **Metrobús Line 1 from the north**: Insurgentes, Glorieta de Insurgentes and the Roma stops all feed directly in.
- **Rideshare**: Uber and DiDi are both widely used across the city. Drop-offs on Insurgentes itself get tangled near show time.
- **Walking**: from southern Condesa or Del Valle, it's a flat walk along leafy side streets.

The neighbourhood also holds Plaza México, the huge bullring, and Estadio Ciudad de los Deportes, a few blocks west. Parque Hundido, a sunken park full of old trees, sits further south along Insurgentes.

## Leaving Nápoles after the encore

The real skill here is getting home. Thousands of people spill onto Insurgentes at the same moment, and ride-hailing prices jump. Regulars usually walk two or three blocks into Nápoles or Del Valle before requesting a car. Pickups on the quieter residential streets move far faster than ones at the arena doors.

The Metrobús runs on the rechargeable Tarjeta de Movilidad Integrada (the city's transit card), and you can't pay cash on board. Locals load it in advance at station machines so they don't end up queueing at a machine after the show.

A few more habits worth copying:

- **Ticket language**: tickets are *boletos*, and the box office is the *taquilla*.
- **Street food**: taco and *esquites* stands usually set up around big venues on show nights. Small cash is the norm there, while rideshares and most restaurants take cards.
- **Merch**: unofficial shirts are sold on the pavement outside. The official stand is inside.

## Dressing for a January night at altitude

Mexico City sits at about 2,240 metres. January falls in the dry season, with clear days and noticeably cold nights. Inside, the arena warms up fast once the floor fills. Outside after midnight, the temperature can drop to single digits Celsius.

Most concertgoers wear a light layer they can tie around their waist during the set. The altitude also catches visitors out. A day of walking plus an evening of jumping and shouting adds up, and seasoned travellers drink more water than they would at sea level.

## Making a weekend of it

A Friday show leaves the weekend open. Nápoles is mostly residential, so visitors usually base themselves in Roma Norte or Condesa. Both are a straight Metrobús ride up Insurgentes and full of cafés, bars and late-night taquerías.

On Saturday, the Polyforum murals deserve a proper look in daylight. Coyoacán's plazas are a short ride south-east. For anything else on the tour, Sombr's official channels list every city and its ticket links.
