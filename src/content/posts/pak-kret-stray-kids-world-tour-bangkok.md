## Two nights in Muang Thong Thani

Stray Kids play IMPACT Arena on January 16 and 17, 2027. The Bangkok shows are two stops on the JYP group's world tour, and the eight members fill an indoor bowl that holds roughly 12,000 people. That makes it one of the larger rooms Thailand gives K-pop acts.

The arena isn't in central Bangkok. It's in Muang Thong Thani, a planned district in Pak Kret, Nonthaburi province, just over the city line. Around it sit IMPACT Challenger's exhibition halls, a lakeside promenade and rows of condo towers. Thai concerts tend to run on Bangkok's January dry season, so the evenings are mild.

## Reaching IMPACT Arena

Getting there takes longer than the map suggests. Coming from Sukhumvit or Siam, the drive often runs past an hour once traffic on the expressway and Chaeng Watthana Road backs up. Concert nights add to it.

- **MRT Pink Line:** the monorail runs along Chaeng Watthana Road and serves Muang Thong Thani. It connects with the BTS at Wat Phra Sri Mahathat and the MRT Purple Line at Nonthaburi Civic Center.
- **Taxi or Grab:** this is easy on the way in. After the show, pickups around the arena jam up, so many fans walk out toward Chaeng Watthana Road first.
- **From Don Mueang Airport:** the airport is a short drive east, which helps fans flying in on regional carriers.

## Where fans stay and eat

Out-of-town STAYs usually choose between two options. Some book Novotel Bangkok IMPACT and the condo rentals inside Muang Thong Thani, so they can walk home. Others stay in central Bangkok for the nightlife and ride back afterwards.

The lake and the streets around the halls fill with food stalls and convenience stores. Inside the venue complex there are chain restaurants and food courts. The arena's own concessions get crowded before the show, so many attendees eat in the district in the afternoon instead.

## Lightsticks, freebies and the STAY etiquette

A Thai K-pop crowd follows its own routines. Fans usually show up hours before doors to queue for official merchandise and take photos at the banner displays. Many also swap fan-made "freebies": photocards, stickers and snacks handed out by fan groups in Thai, Korean and English.

- **Lightstick:** the official Nachimbong lightstick syncs with the show over Bluetooth. Fans pair it by entering their seat details in the official app before doors open.
- **Fan chants:** STAYs chant the members' names in a fixed order during intros. Thai fans learn them from fan-account guides.
- **ID and tickets:** Thai promoters regularly match the ticket to the buyer's ID at the gate. Fans carry their passport or Thai ID card.
- **Bags:** large bags and professional cameras are usually turned away. Fans travel light.
- **Payment:** stalls around the halls take PromptPay QR codes. Foreign visitors pay in cash or by card at the official merch booths.
