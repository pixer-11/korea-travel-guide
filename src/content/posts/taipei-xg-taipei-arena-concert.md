## The venue: Taipei Arena

Taipei Arena sits in Songshan District, at the corner of Dunhua North Road and Nanjing East Road. It opened in 2005 and remains Taipei's main indoor arena for touring pop acts, seating roughly 15,000 depending on stage configuration.

The building is circular, with concourses wrapping the seating bowl, so wayfinding is mostly a matter of following the signage to your gate number rather than hunting through corridors. A shopping mall (ATT4FUN-style retail plus restaurants) sits at ground level, useful for a pre-show meal without leaving the complex.

## Getting there and parking

The arena connects directly to Taipei Arena Station on the Brown Line (Wenhu Line), one of the few venues in the city with a station named after it.

- Exit directly into the arena concourse; no street crossing needed
- The Songshan Airport MRT stop on the same line is one stop away, useful if flying in same-day
- Taxis and rideshare drop-offs cluster at the Nanjing East Road side; expect this lane to back up in the thirty minutes before doors
- On-site parking exists but fills fast on concert nights; the MRT is the more reliable option
