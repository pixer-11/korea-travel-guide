The Taipei dates sit inside a broader 2026 Asia Tour that follows stops in Seoul and precedes Singapore and Bangkok, so Taipei is getting a full arena production rather than a scaled-down regional stop. For Taiwanese and traveling fans, two consecutive nights (August 29–30, 2026) means a real chance at tickets even if the first night sells out fast.

## Getting there and where it's happening

The shows are set for Taipei Arena (台北小巨蛋), the city's main indoor arena in Songshan District, Taipei's default venue for international touring acts. It sits directly above Taipei Arena Station on the Wenhu (Brown) Line and the Songshan Line (Green Line), so you can walk from the platform straight into the venue without surfacing far from the doors — look for exits near the arena's main concourse. If you're coming from Taoyuan International Airport, the Airport MRT connects to Taipei Main Station, where you transfer to the Songshan Line and ride directly to Taipei Arena Station, roughly 45–60 minutes door to door depending on connections.

Taipei concertgoers typically arrive with plenty of buffer time — arena doors and bag checks can create real lines, so locals often show up 60–90 minutes early rather than right at doors-open. Inside the arena, Taiwanese concert etiquette leans toward enthusiastic but orderly participation — glow sticks and synchronized fan chants are common for J-pop/J-rock shows, so it's worth listening for call-and-response cues from the crowd rather than freelancing your own.
