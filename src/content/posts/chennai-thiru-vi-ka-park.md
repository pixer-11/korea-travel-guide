## What this park actually is

Thiru Vi Ka Park sits on Thuru Vi Ka Park Road in Shenoy Nagar, a residential pocket of central Chennai. It's a walled, gated green space built around a walking track, not a garden of monuments or ruins. Locals use it as a fitness loop: joggers, walkers, and older residents doing morning stretches on the grass verges. The pond and landscaped beds inside make it a step up from a bare municipal ground, which is why it holds a strong 4.5 rating across over 2,000 reviews.

## The two-window schedule

The gates open only twice a day, every day of the week:

- 5:00 – 9:00 AM
- 5:00 – 9:00 PM

There's no midday access. If you show up at noon expecting a stroll, you'll find it shut. Plan your visit around one of these two windows and build in 45 minutes to an hour if you want to walk the full track rather than just pass through.

## Morning versus evening

Morning (5–9am) is the quieter, cooler option, especially before 7am when the heat hasn't built up. This is when regulars do laps before work; the pace inside is brisk and purposeful rather than social. Evening (5–9pm) draws families with kids, groups doing yoga on the lawn, and a heavier crush near closing time as the after-work crowd arrives. If you want space to actually enjoy the track rather than dodge groups, aim for the first hour after the morning gate opens.

Being a well-known neighbourhood park rather than a tourist landmark, it fills up with local regulars, not visitors, so don't expect it to feel undiscovered. The upside is that the crowd is genuinely local and the park runs like a real piece of daily Chennai life instead of a sight staged for outsiders.

## Walking the loop without getting lost in it

There's a single main paved track ringing the park, wide enough for two-way walking traffic. Etiquette here follows Indian park norms: keep left on the track, don't block it to chat in groups, and give joggers room to pass.

- Wear closed shoes; the track surface can be uneven in patches.
- Carry water, especially for the evening slot when humidity is higher.
- Entry is typically free for a park like this, but there's no official confirmation here, so treat it as unconfirmed and check a signboard at the gate.
- Skip bringing outside food onto the lawns if you see posted restrictions; check the gate noticeboard on arrival.
- Photography is fine for personal use; this isn't a spot with restricted zones.

Getting there is straightforward by autorickshaw or taxi to Shenoy Nagar; the park address (No 13, Thuru Vi Ka Park Road) is well known to local drivers. Shenoy Nagar Metro station, on the Chennai Metro Blue Line, is the nearest rail link, putting you within a short auto ride of the gate. Pair a morning visit here with breakfast at one of the small idli-and-filter-coffee stalls that dot the surrounding residential streets, a low-key way to see how the neighbourhood actually runs its day.
