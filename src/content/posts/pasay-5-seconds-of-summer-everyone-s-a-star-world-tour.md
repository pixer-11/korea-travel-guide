## Two nights on the bay

Manila gets a double booking. 5 Seconds of Summer, the Australian pop-rock band from Sydney, brings the Everyone's a Star! World Tour to the SM Mall of Asia Arena on November 11 and 12, 2026. Those dates fall on a Wednesday and a Thursday.

The arena is a round, saddle-roofed building at the northern edge of the SM Mall of Asia complex. It sits on reclaimed land along Manila Bay. Since it opened in 2012 it has become the city's default room for big international pop tours. Capacity runs to roughly 20,000, so this is an arena show, not a stadium one.

## Getting tickets and choosing a night

Two dates means two separate ticket pools, and the second night is not a backup for the first. Pick the night that suits your travel plans and buy for that one.

- **Where to buy:** use only the seller linked from the band's official site or the arena's own channels. Resale posts on social media are where fake tickets turn up.
- **Seating:** the arena has a standing floor plus tiered lower and upper boxes wrapped around it. Upper sections look steeply down on the stage.
- **Prices:** the official seller posts the price for each section. Check there rather than trusting figures quoted in fan groups.

Confirm timing, seat maps and tickets on the official site. Check any entry rules printed on your ticket too.

## Reaching the Mall of Asia complex

Pasay's bayside sits west of EDSA, Manila's main ring road. On a weeknight, traffic is the real opening act, so budget far more time than the map suggests.

- **From the airport:** NAIA is a short taxi or Grab ride away. It's close enough that flying in on the day is realistic if your flight lands by early afternoon.
- **EDSA Carousel:** the dedicated busway along EDSA has a stop at the Mall of Asia. It's usually the most predictable route from Makati or Quezon City at rush hour.
- **Rail:** LRT-1 (EDSA station) and MRT-3 (Taft Avenue station) both stop near the EDSA–Taft junction. From there, short jeepney and bus rides head down to the mall.
- **Grab or taxi:** convenient going in, but pricey and slow coming out.

## How fans do arena nights in Manila

Filipino crowds are known for singing every word, loudly, from the first chord. Go expecting a full-voice singalong rather than polite listening. Fan projects such as coordinated signs or phone-light moments are common at Manila shows. Fan accounts usually share instructions on social media beforehand.

A few habits make the night smoother:

- **Arrive early and eat in the mall.** The food courts and restaurants around the arena feed the crowd before doors open. Eating there beats queuing at the arena concessions.
- **Pack light.** Expect bag checks at the entrance, and check the venue's list of banned items before you go. Professional camera gear is commonly refused at arena concerts.
- **Pay how locals pay.** Cards and e-wallets such as GCash are widely accepted across the mall. Small cash still helps with jeepneys and street vendors.
- **Don't fight the exit rush.** When the lights come up, thousands of people order rides at once. Walk back into the mall, sit down for food, and book your ride once the crowd thins.

## Around the arena before the show

The complex is big enough to fill an afternoon. Behind the main mall, the seaside promenade faces west across Manila Bay. The sunsets there are among the most photographed in the city.

The MOA Eye ferris wheel stands by the bay. The SMX Convention Center sits within the same complex, so check whether another big event shares your dates and will add to the crowds. For a longer stay, CCP Complex and the Roxas Boulevard bayfront are a short drive north.

## Weather and timing in November

November is the tail of Manila's rainy season. Typhoons can still reach Luzon, and a storm can mess up flights and roads. Heavy weather can also affect large events, so watch PAGASA's official forecasts in the days before.

Evenings stay hot and humid, and the queue outside can be sticky even when the arena itself is air-conditioned. Carry water and a compact umbrella; it works for rain and for sun on the walk from the bus. Build in buffer time in case a downpour slows EDSA.
