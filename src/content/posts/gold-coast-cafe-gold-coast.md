## Breakfast Before the Broadwater Wakes Up

At 5:30am on a weekday, Marine Parade in Labrador is mostly dog walkers and early joggers. The door at Shop 12 is already open. Cafe Gold Coast runs on the hours of people who start early: first service before sunrise, last orders done by mid-afternoon.

Inside, timber lines the walls and gives the room a rustic, cabin-like feel. That sets it apart from the white-tile look of many coastal cafés. There are outdoor tables too. On a mild Queensland morning, those are the seats people take first.

## What's on the Plate

The menu keeps to three things: breakfasts, sandwiches and burgers. You won't find a tasting menu here. It's somewhere to eat properly before a long day, or to grab a filling lunch without sitting through a formal service.

What the menu covers:

- **Breakfasts**: the reason to arrive early, served from opening.
- **Sandwiches**: good for taking along the foreshore.
- **Burgers**: the heavier lunch option once the morning rush is over.
- **Coffee**: this is Australia, so a flat white is the default order and nobody will blink at it.

Prices are mid-range, so it's a regular café stop rather than a treat. The menu board has the current figures. Reviews are consistently strong for a small neighbourhood café, and it's still a quieter find than the big-name spots in Surfers Paradise or Burleigh.

## Finding Labrador's Stretch of Marine Parade

Labrador is on the Broadwater, the sheltered lagoon behind The Spit. The ocean beaches are further east. Marine Parade follows the Broadwater's western shore, so this is flat-water, pelican-and-paddleboard country rather than surf.

Getting there:

- **By car**: Labrador is just north of Southport, a short drive from the Gold Coast Highway. Look for street parking along Marine Parade and read the signs for time limits.
- **By public transport**: the G:link light rail doesn't reach Labrador. The nearest stops are in Southport, around Broadwater Parklands, a few kilometres south. Translink buses cover Marine Parade, and the Translink journey planner shows current routes.
- **On foot or by bike**: the foreshore path along the Broadwater makes walking or cycling in from Southport a pleasant option.

## Timing Your Table

The hours shape the whole visit. Weekdays run 5:30am to 2:30pm. Saturdays and Sundays start a little later at 6am and close at the same time.

On weekends, try not to arrive between 8am and 12pm. That's the peak, when the outdoor tables fill and you'll be waiting for a seat. If the weekend is your only option, come right at 6am and you'll get ahead of it.

On weekdays, the calmest hour is 1pm to 2pm. It suits a late lunch, but go early in that window. The café closes at 2:30pm, and turning up at 2:20 for a full breakfast is asking a lot of the kitchen.

## Ordering Without the Awkward Pause

Australian cafés usually settle the bill with a card or a phone tap, and cash is rarely needed. Tipping isn't expected. A tip jar or a spare coin is appreciated but never assumed, so don't worry about working out a percentage.

When you walk in, look around for a moment. Many cafés here take your order and payment at the counter and then bring the food to your table, often with a number to put out. If staff aren't coming to seat people, that's probably the setup. Pick a table, note its number if there is one, and head to the counter.

For coffee, order the way Australians do. A "flat white", "long black" or "magic" (a double ristretto flat white, found in some cafés) is understood right away. If you ask for "a regular coffee", expect a question back.

## Making a Morning of It

Breakfast here pairs well with the Broadwater foreshore. After eating, walk south along the water towards Southport and Broadwater Parklands. It's a flat, shaded route with views across to The Spit and South Stradbroke Island.

If you're on the Gold Coast for a few days, this is a good fix for the jet-lag problem. It's open well before 6am on weekdays, so it's one of the few places to get a proper cooked breakfast while the rest of the coast is still shut.

Budget around 45 minutes to an hour for a sit-down meal, more if you take the outdoor seats and a second coffee.
