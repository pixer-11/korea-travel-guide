## The building on York Boulevard

TD Coliseum is the new name for the arena long known as Copps Coliseum, later FirstOntario Centre. It sits at the corner of York Boulevard and Bay Street North in downtown Hamilton. Oak View Group led a major renovation that rebuilt the bowl, concourses and hospitality spaces, and the arena reopened under the TD name in late 2025.

The arena connects to the Jackson Square shopping complex next door. FirstOntario Concert Hall stands a short walk away on Main Street West. That cluster makes the block the city's main entertainment district on show nights.

For a seven-member group with big floor formations, the arena's raised end-stage setup suits this kind of show. Seats along the sides give a clean view of the choreography. Floor spots are closer but lower, so they show faces rather than patterns.

## Getting to downtown Hamilton

GO Transit is the main rail link. Two stations serve the area:

- **Hamilton GO Centre** on Hunter Street East, about a 15-minute walk south and east of the arena
- **West Harbour GO**, on the Lakeshore West line, a walk of roughly 15–20 minutes downhill toward the bay

From Toronto, the Lakeshore West line runs along the lake through Oakville and Burlington. Concertgoers from Union Station usually take the train rather than face the QEW traffic. On weeknights the late return trains run less often, so people tend to note the last departure before the encore.

Hamilton Street Railway (HSR) buses run along King Street and Main Street, both a few blocks from the arena. Drivers use the downtown parkades around Jackson Square and York Boulevard. Those lots fill on concert nights, and the exits clog for a while after a show.
