## One Sunday night in Senayan

Jakarta gets a single date. On Sunday, December 6, 2026, 5 Seconds of Summer play Indonesia Arena as part of their Asia tour. The venue is the indoor arena inside the Gelora Bung Karno sports complex in Senayan, Central Jakarta, the city's main ground for big concerts and sport.

The band formed in Sydney in 2011 and still tours as the original four: Luke Hemmings, Michael Clifford, Calum Hood and Ashton Irwin. The crowd will know every word of "She Looks So Perfect", "Amnesia" and "Youngblood". Our facts don't include the setlist, support act or door times, so this guide doesn't guess at them.

Ticket links for the Jakarta show sit with the band's official tour channels and the local promoter's authorised seller. Resales on social media are where fake wristbands usually turn up.

## Into GBK without a car

Traffic on Jalan Jenderal Sudirman is the main obstacle. Rail beats it easily. Most concertgoers use one of these:

- **MRT Jakarta, North–South Line:** Istora Mandiri and Senayan stations both sit on Sudirman along the complex's eastern edge. From either one, it's a walk through the GBK grounds to the arena.
- **TransJakarta Corridor 1:** the Blok M–Kota busway runs down Sudirman and has stops beside the complex.
- **Gojek or Grab:** motorbike taxis get through jams that stop cars. The pickup points around GBK clog up badly after a show.

Because the show is on a Sunday, many fans are back at work on Monday. That's one reason the trains fill up quickly after the encore.

## Rain, malls and the waiting hours

December falls in Jakarta's rainy season, and heavy evening downpours are normal. The show is indoors, but the walk across the complex is open-air. Fans usually carry a compact umbrella or a poncho.

Three malls on the complex's edge serve as waiting rooms for most fans:

- **FX Sudirman**, on Sudirman at the complex's corner
- **Plaza Senayan**, across Jalan Asia Afrika
- **Senayan City**, next to Plaza Senayan

All three have air-conditioned food courts and toilets that aren't packed with concert queues. Fans meeting friends usually pick one by name, because the arena forecourt is too crowded for that.

## Wristbands, QR codes and the exit crush

Big arena shows in Indonesia usually use e-tickets that are swapped for wristbands at a redemption point. The tickets are often linked to the buyer's ID. Fans normally bring the same ID they bought with: a KTP for locals, a passport for overseas visitors.

Paying for things works the way it does across Jakarta:

- **QRIS codes** are on most vendor counters and work with GoPay, OVO, ShopeePay and DANA.
- **Rupiah in cash** is the backup when the mobile signal drops in a crowd of thousands.
- **Cards** are taken at mall restaurants, but not reliably at the stalls outside.

Merchandise lines usually form well before doors, and popular sizes sell out first. When the show ends, experienced fans walk out to Sudirman or take the MRT one stop before ordering a ride. Waiting at the arena gate for a Grab can take longer than the encore did.

Bahasa Indonesia basics help. Try *permisi* ("excuse me") when you squeeze through a queue and *terima kasih* ("thank you") at the stalls. Tipping isn't expected at food vendors.
