## France's first LE SSERAFIM night

This was a debut on French soil. On October 21, 2026, LE SSERAFIM brought their PUREFLOW world tour to Accor Arena, the group's first concert ever in France. Paris was one of five European dates on what is the biggest tour of their career.

The five members are Kim Chaewon, Sakura, Huh Yunjin, Kazuha and Hong Eunchae. They had been visiting Paris for fashion weeks and promotions for years before this show. For French FEARNOTs, as the fandom is called, this was the first full stage production at home rather than a trip to London or Amsterdam.

## Inside the grass-walled arena

Bercy's arena is easy to spot. It is a squat pyramid with steep lawns running up its sides, opened in 1984 as the Palais Omnisports de Paris-Bercy. Parisians still just call it "Bercy". The bowl holds roughly 20,000 for concerts, and the seating rises steeply, so even upper rows look straight down at the stage.

Tickets split into two broad zones:

- **Fosse**: the standing floor in front of the stage, unnumbered
- **Gradins**: the numbered tiered seating that wraps around the bowl
- **Carré Or** or similar premium zones, where an event offers them

Tickets for Accor Arena shows are sold through the arena's own ticketing and its authorised resellers. Prices are shown there, not here.

## Reaching Bercy and the streets around it

The metro drops you at the doors. **Bercy** station (Lines 6 and 14) sits on the arena's north side. Line 14 is driverless and links Bercy directly to Gare de Lyon, Châtelet and Saint-Lazare in minutes. **Gare de Lyon** (Lines 1 and 14, RER A and D) is a ten-minute walk west, useful when Bercy station is packed after the encore.

Around the arena:

- **Parc de Bercy**: gardens and lawns directly east, where queues often spill
- **Bercy Village**: the old wine warehouses on Cour Saint-Émilion, now restaurants and shops (Cour Saint-Émilion station, Line 14)
- **Cinémathèque française**: the Frank Gehry building on the park's edge
- **Passerelle Simone-de-Beauvoir**: footbridge across the Seine to the Bibliothèque nationale

Many international fans stay around Gare de Lyon or Bastille, both a short Line 1 or 14 ride away.

## How FEARNOTs do a Bercy show

K-pop nights at Bercy follow a familiar pattern. Merchandise lines form outside long before doors open, and fans holding fosse tickets usually queue for hours to get near the barrier. Seated ticket holders tend to arrive far later, since their place is fixed.

Inside, the routine is predictable:

- **Lightsticks**: most fans carry the official LE SSERAFIM lightstick, which often syncs to the show's lighting
- **Fan chants**: the crowd shouts members' names in set gaps in each song, and fans learn them from official chant guides
- **Slogans and banners**: handheld slogans are standard; large signs blocking views are not welcome
- **Bags**: Accor Arena runs security screening at entry, so fans tend to carry small bags

After the show, the platforms at Bercy station fill in seconds. Many attendees walk to Gare de Lyon or cross into Bercy Village for a late meal, letting the first crush clear.
