## Getting there and finding your seat

The centre sits directly on top of Seoul's subway grid, which makes it one of the easier major venues in the city to reach without a car.

- **Bus:** Numerous city buses stop along Sejong-daero directly in front of the complex.
- **Landmarks:** Gwanghwamun Gate and the King Sejong statue are both visible from the plaza outside; if you can see the statue, you're at the right building.

Korean concert-hall audiences for seated jazz and classical bookings tend to be formal by international standards. A few habits will smooth the night:

- Photography during the performance is generally restricted; check signage at your seat, but assume no flash and likely no photos at all once the lights go down.
