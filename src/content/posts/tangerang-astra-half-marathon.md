## Before Sunrise in BSD City

Race day here starts in the dark. BSD City is a planned town southwest of Jakarta, all wide boulevards, landscaped medians and office blocks. On a Sunday before dawn those roads belong to runners in bibs, not to commuter traffic.

The Astra Half Marathon is an annual road race, and in 2026 it takes place on November 29. Start and finish are at Astra Biz Center, Astra's business complex inside BSD City. Three distances share the morning: 5K, 10K and the half marathon.

## Picking Your Distance

Each distance suits a different kind of runner. The wide, mostly flat streets of a planned township work for every level, so the real question is how long you want to be out in the tropical heat.

- **5K:** suits first-timers, families and anyone who wants the race atmosphere without the training block.
- **10K:** the usual middle ground for regular weekend runners in Greater Jakarta.
- **Half marathon (21.1K):** the headline event, and the longest stretch of time you spend in the climbing morning heat.

Course maps, cut-off times and flag-off times for each category belong to the organiser. This guide gives none of them, because none were supplied to us.

## Getting to Astra Biz Center

Most runners come by car or ride-hail. From central Jakarta, the Jakarta–Serpong toll road is the direct route into BSD City. Before dawn on a Sunday the toll road is usually clear. The bottleneck comes later, at the drop-off point near the start.

Rail is possible too. The KRL Commuter Line to Rangkasbitung stops at Rawa Buntu, Serpong and Cisauk, which all serve BSD City. The catch is the hour. Pre-dawn starts often come before the first trains arrive, so runners from Jakarta usually drive, take a Grab or Gojek, or stay in BSD the night before.

Hotels around BSD City and neighbouring Alam Sutera are the usual base for runners from out of town.

## Heat, Humidity and Late November

Late November falls in the wet season around Jakarta. Heavy afternoon and overnight downpours are common, and the air stays humid even at dawn. That is why Indonesian road races usually flag off very early: the cooler hour before the sun clears the buildings is what everyone is racing for.

Experienced local runners treat hydration as part of the race plan, not an afterthought. They also carry a light layer or poncho for the wait in the start pen if it has rained overnight.

## How Race Morning Works in BSD

Indonesian road races follow a familiar rhythm. Bibs, timing chips and race shirts are usually handed out at a race pack collection, which runners call "RPC", in the days before the event. Runners usually bring their registration confirmation and ID to collect.

On the morning itself:

- **Getting dropped off:** ride-hail drivers stop wherever road closures allow. Many runners walk the last stretch rather than sit in the queue of cars.
- **Bag drop:** most runners travel light, with a phone, a small amount of cash and an e-wallet.
- **Paying for things:** QRIS codes, GoPay and OVO are standard at food stalls and cafés across BSD City. Small notes of rupiah cover the rest.
- **Etiquette:** runners line up in their category pens by bib. Walkers keep left on narrow sections so faster runners can pass on the right.

Crowds of supporters gather near the finish at Astra Biz Center rather than out on the course. Family members usually wait there with towels and cold drinks.

## After the Finish Line

BSD City wakes up as the race ends. The township is built around malls and food streets, and post-race breakfast is a ritual in itself.

- **AEON Mall BSD City:** a large Japanese-run mall with a big food floor, handy for a sit-down meal.
- **The Breeze BSD:** an open-air lifestyle centre around a lake, with cafés and plenty of shade.
- **ICE BSD (Indonesia Convention Exhibition):** the large convention centre whose expos and events often share the area's weekend traffic.

Street vendors selling bubur ayam (chicken rice porridge) and nasi uduk are the classic Indonesian post-run breakfast. Some runners stay into the afternoon. Others head straight back to Jakarta before the Sunday traffic builds.
