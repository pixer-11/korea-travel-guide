## Le Trabendo, inside the Parc de la Villette

The venue is at 211 Avenue Jean Jaurès, inside the Parc de la Villette in the 19th arrondissement. It's a low building with a bar, and the main hall is mostly standing room.

Getting there:
- **Metro:** Porte de Pantin (Line 5) is the closest station, a short walk across the park.
- **Tram:** Line T3b stops at Porte de Pantin too.
- **Neighbours:** the Philharmonie de Paris, the Zénith Paris and the Cité des sciences et de l'industrie are all in the same park.

On a Friday the metro runs later than on weeknights, so most fans can get home by Line 5 after the encore.

Fans coming from outside Paris often stay near the Gare de l'Est or Gare du Nord. From there, Line 5 runs straight to Porte de Pantin with no changes. The tour organiser holds the ticket and entry details for this date. Ticket prices are on the official sales page, and this guide gives none.
