## Three nights in Cotai

Tomorrow X Together took STEAL THE WIND to Macau for three nights. The dates were November 20, 21 and 22, 2026, all at Galaxy Arena. For a single city on a world tour, that is a long run. It reflects how many fans from Hong Kong, mainland China and across Southeast Asia can reach Macau in an afternoon.

All five members are on this tour: Soobin, Yeonjun, Beomgyu, Taehyun and Hueningkai. TXT's label, BIGHIT MUSIC, posts tour notices through the group's Weverse community. That is where fans usually look for show-day rules and set details. This page does not invent a setlist or start times.

## The arena itself

Galaxy Arena opened in 2023. It is a purpose-built indoor concert hall attached to the Galaxy Macau resort, so you never have to queue on an open street. Seating rises steeply on several tiers around the floor. That makes the upper sections feel closer to the stage than they would in an older, flatter stadium.

Inside, the arena connects to the resort's air-conditioned corridors. Fans usually spend the hours before a show indoors, in the shopping and dining levels. Late November in Macau is mild and dry, so the walk between buildings is easy.

## Reaching Galaxy Macau

Most international fans arrive through Hong Kong. You have two main crossings:

- **Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge:** shuttle buses run from the Hong Kong port to the Macau port. Galaxy Macau sits a short ride from the Macau side.
- **Ferry:** high-speed ferries run from Hong Kong to Taipa Ferry Terminal. Taipa is the closer of Macau's two ferry terminals for Cotai.
- **Air:** Macau International Airport is in Taipa, minutes from the Cotai Strip.

From any arrival point, Galaxy Macau's free resort shuttles and the Macau LRT Taipa Line both reach the Cotai area. Taxis are available but often scarce after a big show lets out. Many attendees walk across to The Venetian Macao or Broadway Macau to find a ride or a later meal.

## How MOA do a Macau show night

The crowd is mostly MOA, TXT's fandom, and a lot of the ritual is shared. Fans typically bring the official lightstick, which syncs to the show's lighting. Many make small freebies like photocards, stickers or keychains and hand them out in the resort corridors before doors. Trading happens politely, with a smile and an exchange of items rather than any money.

A few practical habits for a Macau show day:

- **Money:** the Macau pataca is the local currency, and Hong Kong dollars are widely accepted at close to par. Change usually comes back in patacas, which are hard to spend outside Macau. Cards and mobile payments work across the resort.
- **Language:** signs are in Chinese, Portuguese and English. Cantonese is the street language, and resort staff generally speak English and Mandarin.
- **Fan chants:** the Korean chants for TXT songs circulate in the fandom. The arena is noticeably louder in the sections where people learned them.
- **Leaving:** with three nights back to back, many fans who come for more than one show stay in Cotai. That way the walk back to the hotel replaces the late border crossing.

Remember that the arena sits inside a casino resort. The gaming floors are restricted to adults, and staff enforce that. Younger fans route around them on the shopping levels.

## Around the resort before and after

Galaxy Macau has a lot to fill the hours. Broadway Macau, next door, has a pedestrian street lined with casual Macanese and Hong Kong-style food stalls. It suits a cheaper dinner before a show.

If you have a free afternoon, Taipa Village is a short ride away. Rua do Cunha is a narrow lane of bakeries selling almond cookies and pork-chop buns. Further south in Coloane, Lord Stow's Bakery is the place most associated with Macau's Portuguese-style egg tart.

For a full day, the Historic Centre of Macau is a UNESCO World Heritage Site across the bridges on the peninsula. It includes Senado Square and the Ruins of St. Paul's. Fans with tickets for several nights often fit it in on one of the free mornings.

## How long to give it

A single show night in Macau works as a day trip from Hong Kong, but it is a long one. You need the crossing in each direction, plus the queue at the end of the night.

Most visitors flying in from further away give Macau two or three nights. One evening goes to the concert, and a day goes to Taipa and the old town.
