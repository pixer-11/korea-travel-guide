## Getting there and home after the encore

Barcelona's public transport handles concert nights well, but the timetable matters. The Metro runs until about midnight Sunday to Thursday and until around 2am on Friday. On Saturday night it runs straight through. On other nights the NitBus network takes over once the Metro stops.

Taxis in Barcelona are black and yellow. A green light on the roof means the cab is free and can be waved down. After a big show the ranks closest to the venue fill fast, so many people walk a few blocks before trying to hail one.

For a stay of several days, the multi-trip transport cards sold in every Metro station work out simpler than single tickets for each journey.
