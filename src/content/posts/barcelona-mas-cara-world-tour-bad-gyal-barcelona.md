## Who is on that stage

Bad Gyal is the stage name of Alba Farelo. She grew up in Vilassar de Mar, a small town on the Maresme coast just north of Barcelona. She broke through in 2016 with "Pai", a Catalan-language version of Rihanna's "Work" sung through heavy Auto-Tune. It started as an internet curiosity and turned into a career.

Since then she has become one of the best-known names in Spanish urban music. Her sound pulls from dancehall, reggaeton and club pop. "Fiebre" is the kind of track that turns a floor into one big bouncing mass.

Her live shows rely on dance as much as vocals. Most people in the room will be moving, not watching politely.
