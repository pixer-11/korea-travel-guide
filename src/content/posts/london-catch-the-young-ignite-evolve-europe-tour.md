## The Steelyard before the steel yard

The venue's name is older than the nightclub. Its history is worth five minutes on the walk in.

For centuries this patch of riverbank was the Steelyard, the London base of the Hanseatic League, the trading network of north German and Baltic merchant towns. It had its own walled compound, warehouses, quays and privileges, and it ran from the Middle Ages until Elizabeth I ended those privileges in the late 16th century. Cannon Street station was later built over the site in the 1860s.

A plaque on the riverside walk marks the spot. It means the K-pop crowd queuing on Allhallows Lane is standing where foreign merchants once unloaded cloth, wax and timber. That is a fair setting for a tour that is itself a European circuit.
