## What Well B Festival is

Well B Festival is a wellness and lifestyle event, not a music or food festival. Expect movement classes, talks and lifestyle brand activations rather than headline acts. The 2026 edition runs November 14-15 at Meydan Racecourse.

Meydan is best known as the home of the Dubai World Cup horse race. For this event the grandstand grounds and surrounding open space host a wellness festival instead, with the racecourse's scale giving room for multiple activity zones running side by side.

## Meydan Racecourse: getting there and around

Meydan Racecourse sits in the Meydan district, east of Downtown Dubai and a short drive from Business Bay. It's built for large-scale events, so expect designated parking areas and clear signage once you're on site.

There's no metro station directly at Meydan; most visitors arrive by car, taxi or ride-hailing app. Given the scale of the venue, budget extra time to walk from parking to the festival zone itself, especially on a two-day event with likely staggered entry points.

- Arrive by car or taxi; Meydan is not on the metro line
- Expect a walk from parking to the main activity areas
- November in Dubai means daytime heat but comfortable evenings, so a mix of indoor and outdoor programming is likely to be scheduled accordingly

## Making the most of a two-day wellness event

Treat November 14-15 as two distinct days rather than one long one. Wellness festivals like this typically run parallel tracks: fitness classes, nutrition talks, and retail or brand pop-ups, often overlapping.

- Check the official program in advance and pick two or three sessions per day rather than trying to see everything
- Wear activewear and comfortable shoes if you plan to join movement classes
- Bring a refillable water bottle; Dubai in November is warmer than a European autumn, even with cooler evenings
