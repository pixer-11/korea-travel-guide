## Getting to My Dinh National Stadium

My Dinh National Stadium sits in Nam Tu Liem District, about 8km west of Hanoi's Old Quarter. It's Vietnam's national stadium, built for the 2003 SEA Games, and it's the default venue for the country's biggest concerts and football fixtures.

Getting there:

- **Taxi/ride-hail (Grab):** the simplest option from anywhere in the city; surge pricing is common right after big shows end.
- **Bus:** several city routes stop near the stadium gates, cheap but slower during event traffic.
- **Motorbike:** common for locals, but parking fills fast and leaving afterward is slow.

On big concert nights, full stadium crowds converge on the same intersections. The roads around My Dinh choke up badly in the hour before doors and for well over an hour after the final encore.

## Tickets and seating

## How to visit like a local

Hanoi concertgoers travel light and move fast once doors open, and bag checks at the gates are standard. Cash is still useful for street food stalls near the stadium, but most taxis and convenience stores now take cards or e-wallets like Momo.

Grab rides requested before the encore ends are far easier to get than those requested after, when everyone has the same idea and surge pricing spikes hard. From the Old Quarter, the ride takes 20-30 minutes each way outside peak traffic, longer on show nights.

Vietnamese fans tend to gather early to soak up the pre-show atmosphere and trade merchandise or light sticks outside the gates. It's part of the experience, not just a queue to endure.

Street vendors set up near the stadium selling food, drinks and unofficial merch. Prices aren't fixed, so a quick, friendly haggle is normal, and small notes are handier than large bills.

## Nearby and what else to do

My Dinh sits near the Vietnam National Convention Center and within reach of West Lake (Ho Tay), a good spot to unwind with lakeside cafés and a slower pace than the Old Quarter. A longer Hanoi trip pairs well with a day exploring the Old Quarter's street food and a visit to the Temple of Literature, both a manageable taxi ride from the stadium.
