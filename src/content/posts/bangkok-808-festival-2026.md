## When and where it happens

The festival runs October 2-3, 2026, at BITEC (Bangkok International Trade & Exhibition Centre) in the Bang Na district, east of central Bangkok. BITEC is a cavernous convention center normally used for trade shows, which means organizers can build out large stage productions indoors, insulated from Bangkok's October rain showers. ## Getting to BITEC without the pre-show scramble

BITEC sits right by Bang Na BTS station on the Sukhumvit Line, one of the more painless festival-venue connections Bangkok offers. From the station it's a short walk, well signed once you're above ground. If you're coming from central Bangkok (Siam, Asoke, Thonglor), the BTS ride runs well under an hour depending on your starting point, and it sidesteps the traffic that can back up along Sukhumvit and Bang Na-Trat Road during event nights.

- Taxis and ride-hailing apps (Grab) work fine but get squeezed in event-exit traffic; budget extra time for the ride home, especially after a late set.
- If driving, BITEC has on-site parking, but arriving early avoids the worst of the queue into the lot.
- The BTS runs until midnight on standard schedule; if a set overruns, plan a taxi or Grab back rather than counting on the last train.

## The two-night rhythm: picking your night

Two-night festivals like this tend to split by taste rather than just headliner name, since one night often leans harder into bass and trap while the other pulls in more mainstream EDM or house-adjacent sets. If NGHTMRE and SLANDER aren't split across separate nights, check which night carries the support lineup that matches your taste before committing to a single-day ticket.

Bangkok's festival crowd for 808 skews young, heavy on both Thai fans and regional visitors flying in from Singapore, Malaysia, Hong Kong, and beyond, so nights can run at full capacity fast once doors open.

## Eating and staying near Bang Na

BITEC's own concourse holds food stalls during events, generally not the reason you're there but fine for a quick refuel between sets. The surrounding Bang Na district is less packed with hotels than central Bangkok, but Sukhumvit Line stations further in (On Nut, Ekkamai, Phrom Phong) put you 15-25 minutes from the venue by BTS with far more choice for accommodation and post-festival food.

Central Bangkok's night market food stalls and 24-hour street vendors along Sukhumvit are a reliable option if you're heading back late and want something more substantial than festival concessions.
