## The Saint Peter team, on an island

This is a Sydney name operating in the Whitsundays. Josh and Julie Niland built their reputation at Saint Peter in Paddington, where Josh's whole-fish cookery, from scales to offal, won the restaurant a national following. His books, including *The Whole Fish Cookbook*, carried that approach well beyond Sydney.

Catseye Pool Club is the couple's Hamilton Island venture. It is currently the island's most talked-about new table. Treat it as a destination meal, not a casual drop-in.

## How the table works

There is no conventional menu to order from. Dishes come in set combinations, and they are designed to be shared across the table. You choose the format and the kitchen sends the plates.

That shapes the evening in practical ways:

- **Come as a group.** Sharing formats suit two or more people better than a solo diner.
- **Flag dietary needs early.** With set combinations, the kitchen has to plan around restrictions, so mention them when you book.
- **Allow time.** Shared courses arrive in sequence, so budget a full sitting rather than a quick bite between activities.

We have not been given the dishes, so expect a menu that shifts. Check the venue's own channels for what is currently served.

## Booking a table at Catseye

Reservations decide whether you eat here at all. Hamilton Island is a resort island with a fixed number of beds, and a new opening from a well-known chef draws most of those guests at once. Lock in a booking when you lock in flights, not on arrival.

A few habits that work on the island:

- **Pay by card or phone.** Contactless payment is standard across Australia.
- **Skip the tip calculation.** Tipping is not expected in Australian restaurants; leave something only if service stood out.
- **Move by buggy or shuttle.** Most visitors get around Hamilton Island by hired golf buggy or the free island shuttle, so plan your ride back after dinner.
- **Check hours before you head over.** Opening times at new venues change often in the first seasons, and none are confirmed here.

## Fitting it into a Hamilton Island trip

The island is easy to reach. Hamilton Island Airport takes direct flights from Sydney, Brisbane and Melbourne. Ferries also run from Airlie Beach on the mainland, crossing the Whitsunday Passage.

Build the dinner around the island's daytime anchors:

- **Whitehaven Beach.** Boat and seaplane trips leave the island for its white silica sand on neighbouring Whitsunday Island.
- **One Tree Hill.** The island's classic sunset lookout, reachable by buggy before an evening booking.
- **Catseye Beach.** The main swimming beach, fronting the resort centre on the island's east side.
- **The Great Barrier Reef.** Day trips run out to the outer reef pontoons and Heart Reef flyovers.

A reef or Whitehaven day followed by a late shared dinner is the obvious pairing. Give the meal its own evening rather than squeezing it after a seaplane landing.
