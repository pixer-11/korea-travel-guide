## A serenade on an open field

*Harana* is the Tagalog word for the old Filipino courtship serenade: a man singing under a window, usually to a guitar. The festival takes that name to an open lawn in Alabang. It's on Saturday, November 28, 2026.

The Filinvest City Events Grounds is a flat, open lot inside Filinvest City, the planned business district on Muntinlupa's eastern edge. It's grass and gravel, with office towers on the skyline around it. Expect to stand and sit on open ground for hours, with little permanent shade.

The weekend runs into Bonifacio Day on Monday, November 30, a national holiday. That's why plenty of attendees from outside Metro Manila make a long weekend of it.

## Getting to Filinvest City

Almost everyone reaches the grounds by road. The main approaches:

- **By car:** South Luzon Expressway (SLEX) southbound, taking the Alabang or Filinvest exits. Drivers from Makati and the airport usually take the Skyway, which feeds onto SLEX.
- **By ride-hailing:** Grab is the default in Metro Manila. Drop-offs on Filinvest City's internal roads are easy, but pickups after a show are slow while the crowd leaves.
- **By bus:** City and point-to-point buses run from central Manila to Alabang. The terminals near Alabang Town Center and Festival Mall are a short ride from the grounds.

Saturday evening traffic on SLEX and Alabang–Zapote Road builds through December's shopping season. Fans from Quezon City or northern Manila typically leave much more time than the distance suggests.

## Weather, stamina and timing

Late November comes after the peak of the typhoon season, as the northeast monsoon (*amihan*) arrives. Evenings are usually drier and a little cooler than the middle of the year, though a late storm is never ruled out. On an open field, that difference counts.

What regulars usually bring to an outdoor Metro Manila festival:

- A light rain jacket or poncho; umbrellas block views and are often restricted at gates
- A small fan or towel for the afternoon heat
- A charged power bank, because ride-hailing apps are how most people get home
- Valid ID for any age-restricted bar areas

The organiser decides the bag rules, food policy and re-entry terms, and they vary from festival to festival.

## Doing an Alabang festival night properly

Locals treat Filinvest City as a whole evening, not just a gate to walk through. Festival Mall sits right beside the district and is where many attendees eat a proper meal, use clean restrooms and meet friends beforehand. Alabang Town Center, slightly further west, plays the same role for people coming from the Ayala Alabang side.

Payments around Alabang are mostly by GCash or card, and malls accept both everywhere. Small vendors outside the gates often prefer cash or GCash. Tipping isn't required, but leaving the change at sit-down restaurants is normal.

Leaving is the hard part. Experienced festival-goers often walk to Festival Mall or a nearby hotel lobby to book their Grab, instead of waiting in the surge at the main exit. Visitors from outside the capital usually stay in Alabang's hotels, a short drive from the grounds, and skip the late-night trip back across Manila. Ticket sales, lineup and set times are handled by the festival organiser, separately from the venue.
