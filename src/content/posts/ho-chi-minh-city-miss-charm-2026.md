That final lands in Ho Chi Minh City on December 5, 2026. Outgoing titleholder Anna Blanco will crown her successor on stage, closing out a reign that began with the previous edition.

## Landing in Ho Chi Minh City for pageant week

Most international arrivals come through Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN), about 8km from District 1, the city's central hotel and nightlife district. A metered taxi or ride-hailing app (Grab is dominant here) into District 1 typically takes 20 to 30 minutes depending on traffic.

Book accommodation in District 1 or neighboring District 3 if you want walkable access to restaurants and the riverfront, since pageant-related events and press days often cluster around the city's larger hotels and convention spaces in this stretch. December falls in the dry season, with lower humidity and minimal rain compared to the monsoon months, making it one of the more comfortable times of year to be outdoors in the city.

- Carry small Vietnamese dong notes for street food and market stalls near District 1, since cards aren't universal at smaller vendors
- Keep your hotel's name and address written in Vietnamese to show drivers, since not all speak English
