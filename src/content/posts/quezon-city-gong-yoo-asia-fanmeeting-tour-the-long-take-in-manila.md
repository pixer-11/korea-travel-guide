## One night, one dome

This is a fan meeting, not a concert. The format usually mixes talk segments, audience games and Q&A with the actor himself. Most of the crowd knows him from *Coffee Prince*, *Train to Busan*, *Goblin* and his recruiter role in *Squid Game*.

## Inside the Big Dome

The Coliseum opened in 1960 and is one of the largest indoor arenas in Asia. Its wide dome roof covers seating that rises in steep tiers around a central floor. That layout keeps even upper sections looking down on the stage rather than across a long hall.

It has a long record of big nights. Muhammad Ali and Joe Frazier fought the "Thrilla in Manila" here in 1975. Since then the arena has hosted basketball finals, beauty pageants and a long run of K-pop and K-drama star events.

## Getting to Cubao

Two rail lines meet a short walk from the arena:

- **MRT-3, Araneta Center–Cubao station**: runs along EDSA, linking Cubao with Makati and North Avenue.
- **LRT-2, Araneta Center–Cubao station**: runs east–west, connecting to Recto in Manila and to Antipolo.
- **Gateway Mall**: connected walkways run between the stations and the Araneta City complex.

Many attendees take Grab or a taxi home rather than queuing with the post-show crowd. EDSA traffic around Cubao on a Saturday evening tends to be slow. Visitors from abroad often stay in Araneta City's own hotels or in Ortigas, a short ride south.
