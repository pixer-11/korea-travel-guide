## Six nights, one dress code

AmaXon is the name of this year's festival. It marks ten years of White Party Bangkok, a New Year circuit festival that brings an international crowd into the city over the last days of December.

It runs from **December 29, 2026 to January 3, 2027**, and each night has its own job:

- **Opening party:** on the riverside, along the Chao Phraya side of the city
- **Four main nights:** at Paragon Hall, the convention hall inside Siam Paragon
- **Closing party:** the Beef Ball, at NEX Hall

The whole run straddles New Year's Eve. That puts one of the Paragon Hall nights against the countdown that Bangkok's malls and riverbanks mark with fireworks.

## Getting to Paragon Hall and beyond

Paragon Hall sits on an upper floor of Siam Paragon on Rama I Road, in the Siam shopping district. A covered skywalk links the mall to **BTS Siam Station**. That is the interchange between the Sukhumvit and Silom lines, so most central hotels are only a few stops away.

Siam Center and the MBK end of the skywalk network are next door. Pre-party dinners in the mall's food halls are an easy walk from the hall entrance.

The riverside opening and NEX Hall are separate venues. Their exact addresses appear on the organiser's ticket pages for those nights. Many visitors stay around Silom or Sukhumvit, where the BTS connects straight to Siam.

## How the White Party crowd does it

These are long nights, and the regulars pace themselves across six of them. Here is what attendees usually do:

- **Wear white.** It is the festival's founding tradition. Most people stick to white or near-white for the main nights and save themed outfits for the Beef Ball.
- **Carry a passport.** Thai venues routinely ask for photo ID at the door, and many festival-goers carry the physical passport rather than a photo of it.
- **Plan the ride home around the BTS.** Trains stop around midnight, well before these parties end. Most people leave by Grab or Bolt, and the Rama I Road kerb outside Siam Paragon gets crowded once the doors let out.
- **Pick their nights.** Some buy a pass covering the whole run, others choose single nights. The festival's ticket options are listed on its official channels.

Thailand's laws on drugs are severe and enforced. Venue security at large Bangkok events is thorough, including bag checks at the door.

## Making a week of it

The festival dates line up with Bangkok's cool season. December and early January bring the driest, least humid weather of the year, which makes daytime sightseeing between parties more bearable.

Recovery days usually mean the river. The Chao Phraya Express Boat links Sathorn Pier, near BTS Saphan Taksin, with Wat Arun and the Grand Palace. Silom Soi 4 and the Silom Soi 2 area are the city's long-standing LGBTQ+ nightlife strip. They are a short taxi ride from Siam and the place many attendees end up on the off-hours.

Hotels around Siam and Silom fill up over New Year in any year. With a festival this size on the same dates, rooms near the BTS are in steady demand for the whole week.
