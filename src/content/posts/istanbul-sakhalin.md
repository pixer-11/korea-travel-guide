## What Sakhalin actually is

Sakhalin is a seafood restaurant from White Rabbit Family, the group built around Michelin-starred chef Vladimir Mukhin. This Istanbul branch is the concept's second location in Turkey, planted inside Zorlu Center, one of the city's major shopping and culture complexes on the European side. The kitchen leans Mediterranean-Asian fusion, built around seafood rather than the standard Bosphorus grill-house formula. Mukhin made his name in Moscow with White Rabbit, a fixture on world's-best-restaurant lists, so Sakhalin arrives with real culinary weight behind it rather than being a generic import.

## Why Zorlu Center matters

Zorlu Center isn't a random mall pick. It houses a performing arts center, luxury retail, and a cluster of ambitious restaurant openings, making it a deliberate landing spot for a brand chasing an international, design-conscious crowd. The location sits on the European side, reachable by metro, and functions as a destination in itself: expect valet and mall parking rather than street parking. Because it's inside a shopping complex, Sakhalin keeps company with high-end fashion houses and a design-forward interior scene, not the fish-market stalls of Kumkapı or Karaköy.

Follow the lead of the room rather than importing habits from a casual lokanta.
