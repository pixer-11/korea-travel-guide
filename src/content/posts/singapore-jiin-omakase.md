Shaw Centre isn't the address you'd guess for a serious omakase counter. It's a 1970s office-and-retail block on Scotts Road at the top of Orchard Road, better known for cinema-goers and shoppers cutting through to Scotts Square. Jiin sits inside that ordinariness deliberately, another Les Amis Group project tucked among the group's cluster of fine-dining rooms rather than announced from the street.

## What Les Amis Group built here

Les Amis Group has spent three decades running some of Singapore's more disciplined dining rooms, and Jiin reads as their answer to the omakase counters that have multiplied across the city over the past decade. The name draws on the idea of a "second self," and the room is built around a single counter where a chef works directly in front of diners, course by course.

That format only works if the kitchen commits to omotenashi, the Japanese idea of hospitality anticipated rather than requested. Here it shows up as a menu that isn't fixed: it shifts with Japan's micro-seasons, the traditional 72 divisions of the year that track small changes in fish, shellfish, and produce far more closely than the four seasons Western menus usually follow.

## Why the seafood is the point

Premium seafood is the spine of the menu, sourced to match whatever micro-season the calendar says you're in. That means a spring visit and an autumn visit are built around genuinely different fish, not a reshuffled version of the same core dozen items.

If you've eaten at other high-end Japanese counters in Singapore, this is the same logic taken further: less "what's good today" and more "what this exact week of the Japanese calendar calls for." It rewards repeat visits across the year rather than a single one-off booking.

## Getting to Shaw Centre

Shaw Centre sits at 1 Scotts Road, right where Scotts Road meets Orchard Road, close to Orchard MRT (North-South and Thomson-East Coast lines). From most exits it's a short walk up past Shaw House and Scotts Square.

- Nearest MRT: Orchard Station, several exits lead onto Orchard/Scotts Road
- Driving in: paid parking is available within Shaw Centre itself
- Landmarks nearby: Scotts Square, Royal Plaza on Scotts, ION Orchard a few minutes further down Orchard Road

Because Jiin only opened in May 2026, signage and exact unit numbers within Shaw Centre may still be settling in. Call ahead or check the Les Amis Group's website for the precise floor before you arrive.

## Reserving a seat at the counter

Omakase counters this size run on a fixed number of stools per seating, so a walk-in almost never works. Book through the Les Amis Group's reservations channel as far ahead as you can, especially for weekend evenings.

Because it's new, opening hours and seating times weren't fully settled at launch. Confirm the current schedule and whether lunch service exists before you plan around it.

## Reading the room at the counter

Omotenashi service means the chef and staff set the pace, not the diner. Expect courses to arrive in a fixed order built around that week's ingredients, so requests to swap items or over-order can work against the format the chef has built.

- Arrive on time; a counter seating usually starts together and runs as one continuous service
- Dress smart-casual, in keeping with Les Amis Group's other dining rooms
- Ask questions about the fish and the micro-season it represents; that's part of the anticipated hospitality, not an interruption
- Save any dietary restrictions for when you book, not at the counter

## Before you go

Treat any menu structure, pricing, or course count you read online, including here, as provisional until you've confirmed it directly with the restaurant. A concept this recent will settle into its rhythm over its first year, and details like seating count, lunch availability, and exact hours are the ones most likely to shift.
