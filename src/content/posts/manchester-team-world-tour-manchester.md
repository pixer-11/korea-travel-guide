## Who &Team are

&Team came out of HYBE's 2021 survival show *I-LAND* and debuted in Japan in December 2022. They're a nine-member group: EJ, Fuma, K, Nicholas, Yuma, Jo, Harua, Taki and Maki. The line-up mixes Japanese, Korean and other backgrounds, which is why they're usually called a Japanese-Korean act.

Their fandom is called LUNÉ, a name you'll see on banners, on homemade merch and in the chants that roll through the queue. The group's concept leans on wolf and moon imagery, and that tends to show up in what fans wear on the night.

## Getting to Oxford Road on a school night

Oxford Road is one of the busiest bus corridors in Europe, and that's the easiest way in. Buses run south from Piccadilly Gardens and the city centre past the university buildings. Most stop within a few minutes' walk of the Students' Union.

On foot or by train:

- **Manchester Oxford Road station**: roughly a 15–20 minute walk south along Oxford Road, past the university buildings.
- **Manchester Piccadilly station**: about 25 minutes on foot, or a short bus ride. This is the main arrival point for anyone coming from London, Birmingham or Manchester Airport.
- **Taxis and ride-hailing**: these jam up around the Students' Union at kick-out, so many fans walk a few streets north before getting picked up.

Driving is the least practical choice. Parking around the campus is limited and Oxford Road has bus-only stretches.

## Before and after the show

The streets around the Academy are good for filling a long wait. A few minutes' walk away:

- **Manchester Museum**: the university's natural history and archaeology museum, on Oxford Road itself.
- **The Whitworth**: an art gallery set in Whitworth Park, just south of the Students' Union.
- **The Curry Mile**: Wilmslow Road in Rusholme, a straight continuation south. It's lined with South Asian and Middle Eastern restaurants that stay busy late.

Closer in, the takeaways and cafés along Oxford Road do most of the pre-show trade, since the line itself is right there. After the encore, the crowd heads north towards the city centre or south towards Rusholme. The buses either way fill quickly for the first half hour.
