## A Monday night of key changes

The Bangkok date falls on Monday, January 25, 2027. The show celebrates 25 years of Westlife, the Irish vocal group that formed in Sligo in the late 1990s.

The numbers explain the scale: over 55 million records sold worldwide and 14 UK No. 1 singles. Expect a setlist built on the ballads that made them, sung by a crowd that knows every word. Those songs include:

- "Swear It Again", their 1999 debut single
- "Flying Without Wings"
- "My Love"
- "Uptown Girl"
- "You Raise Me Up"

Southeast Asia has been loyal Westlife territory for decades. Thailand is a regular stop on their Asian legs, and audiences here span the original teenage fans and their children.

## Doing a Bangkok arena show properly

A Thai concert crowd runs on its own rhythm. Fans typically arrive well before doors to queue at merchandise stalls, take photos at fan-made banners and buy food from the vendors. Those stalls set up around almost every big Bangkok venue.

- **Bag checks:** security usually screens bags at the gate. Professional cameras and detachable lenses are commonly refused at Thai concerts.
- **Getting there on a weeknight:** Monday evening rush hour clogs Bangkok's expressways. Concertgoers usually ride the BTS Skytrain or MRT as far as possible and finish by taxi or motorbike taxi.
- **Getting home:** Grab and Bolt fares surge the moment an arena empties. Many attendees walk a few blocks and order a ride from there, or wait out the first rush at a nearby street-food stall.
- **Singing along:** Thai audiences sing loudly and in English. Phone torches go up for the slow songs, and "Flying Without Wings" gets the full chorus treatment.
- **Respect for the royal anthem:** before some events in Thailand the royal anthem plays, and everyone stands.

## January in Bangkok

Late January sits inside Bangkok's cool, dry season. That is the most comfortable stretch of the year for walking between a station and a venue, though evenings stay warm and indoor halls run cold with air-conditioning. Fans typically carry a light layer for inside.

Visitors flying in for the show usually stay along the BTS Sukhumvit Line or near the MRT Blue Line. Both connect fast with the rest of the city when traffic stalls. The weekend before is the usual time for a day at Chatuchak Weekend Market, or for the Grand Palace and Wat Pho by the river.
