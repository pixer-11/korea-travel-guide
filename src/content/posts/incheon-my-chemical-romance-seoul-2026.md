Paradise City sits a few minutes' drive from Incheon International Airport, on Yeongjongdo island, which makes this one of the easier international concerts to combine with a flight in or out of Korea. My Chemical Romance's November 7, 2026 date there puts a mid-2000s American rock band in a venue better known for its casino, art collection, and spa than for guitar music, which is part of why the show has drawn attention beyond the usual K-pop touring circuit.

## Getting to Paradise City

Paradise City is inside the Incheon airport free economic zone, connected to Terminal 1 by a free shuttle bus that runs frequently and takes about 10 minutes.

- From Terminal 1: shuttle bus, roughly 10 minutes
- From Terminal 2: shuttle bus, slightly longer, plus an inter-terminal transfer if you're flying into T2
- From central Seoul: AREX airport express train to Incheon Airport, then the shuttle, or a taxi that can run over an hour depending on traffic

If you're staying in Seoul proper, budget at least 70-90 minutes each way and don't cut it close on the return, since late trains and shuttles thin out after midnight.

## What kind of venue this is

Paradise City isn't a purpose-built rock arena. It's a resort complex with multiple halls and event spaces, so the actual room used for this show will be sized and configured specifically for the concert rather than being a fixed, year-round music venue.

That matters for what to expect: sightlines, standing versus seated sections, and bag policies can all differ from what you'd find at a dedicated arena like Incheon's own indoor stadiums. Check the official ticket page for the specific hall name and floor plan once you've bought a ticket, since that's where seating charts and entry gates get posted.

## Buying tickets

Tickets for touring international acts in Korea are typically sold through Korean ticketing platforms such as Interpark or Melon Ticket, sometimes with an international resale or verified-fan step for high-demand shows. Prices and on-sale mechanics for this specific date aren't something to guess at here.

- Confirm timing and tickets on the official site or the band's official channels before purchasing
- Watch for whether tickets are seated, standing, or a mix, since that changes both price and what time you'd want to arrive
- Be wary of third-party resale sites charging above face value; Korean ticketing platforms usually require a local phone number or ID verification, which catches some overseas fans off guard

## Making a day (or two) of it

Because Paradise City sits right by the airport rather than in downtown Incheon or Seoul, most fans either fly in specifically for the show or fold it into the start or end of a longer Korea trip. The resort itself has an art wing (Paradise Art Space), an indoor beach club-style pool complex, and several restaurants, so arriving a few hours early isn't dead time.

If you'd rather stay in the city, Incheon's Songdo district and central Seoul are both under two hours away, giving you a same-day return option without booking an airport-adjacent hotel.

## Fitting the show around flights

Because the venue is essentially on airport grounds, this is one of the few concerts where "catch a red-eye out after the encore" is a genuinely workable plan, provided your flight isn't scheduled too tightly against the show's likely finish time. Build in a buffer of at least three hours between the concert ending and your check-in cutoff, since crowds leaving the venue will hit the shuttle and AREX at the same time.

Conversely, if you're flying in the same day, keep in mind immigration and baggage claim at a major international airport can eat 45 minutes to an hour before you've even reached the shuttle stop.

## Before you lock in plans

Treat the date itself as the one fixed point and verify everything else. Set times, door times, support acts, and any age restrictions are the kind of details that belong on the official ticket listing rather than in a general travel guide, so check there directly before finalizing flights, hotels, or shuttle timing.
