## A hospital-ward album in an 80,000-seat bowl

The Black Parade runs from start to finish here. That means a single flatline tone, then "The End.", then the marching drum that opens "Welcome to the Black Parade". It ends with "Famous Last Words" and the hidden "Blood". At Jakarta International Stadium, the album plays to a crowd that fills a steel-and-glass bowl built for tens of thousands.

The date is November 22, 2026. It is the closing night of the band's Southeast Asia run, so expect fans who have followed the tour from Singapore, Manila or Bangkok. The show sits under the Hammersonic banner. Hammersonic is Indonesia's long-running heavy music festival, and its crowd knows how to hold a pit for hours.

## Where the stadium actually is

Jakarta International Stadium (JIS) is in Papanggo, Tanjung Priok, in North Jakarta. That puts it near the port and well north of the Sudirman and Thamrin hotel strip. It opened in 2022 as the home ground of Persija Jakarta. Its retractable roof and stacked tiers are visible from the toll roads that loop past it.

The distance matters more than the map suggests. From central Jakarta, the trip north can be quick on a clear evening and painfully slow at rush hour. Leave yourself a wide buffer.

- **Ride-hailing:** Grab and Gojek both serve the stadium. Gojek's motorbike option (GoRide) cuts through traffic much faster than a car.
- **TransJakarta:** the city bus network has run services to JIS on event days. Check the TransJakarta app for routes on the day.
- **Stay nearby:** hotels around Kelapa Gading or Ancol put you much closer than a base in South Jakarta.

## Tickets and the paperwork around them

Buy only through the official ticketing partner linked from the Hammersonic and My Chemical Romance sites. Ticket categories and seating maps are listed there. Resale screenshots posted on social media are a common scam around big Jakarta concerts.

Indonesian promoters often use named e-tickets. Some ask you to swap them for a wristband, and some check your ID at entry. Read the terms on your ticket before the day. If the name has to match, bring the passport or KTP the ticket was booked under. Confirm timing and tickets on the official site.

## Getting through the gates on the night

Most of the evening comes down to how you handle the crowd and the heat. These habits are what experienced Jakarta concertgoers stick to.

- **Pay cashless.** QRIS, Indonesia's standard QR payment, is everywhere, and most venue vendors take it along with e-wallets like GoPay and OVO. Load your app before you arrive.
- **Check the bag rules.** Stadium shows usually limit bag size and ban outside food, drinks and umbrellas. A small clear bag or none at all gets you through security faster.
- **Plan the exit, not just the entry.** After the show, ride-hailing pickup outside JIS turns into gridlock. Walk ten or fifteen minutes away from the stadium before you book, and set the pin on a named street.
- **Pick a meeting point.** Mobile data can slow down when a full stadium is on one cell network. Agree on a landmark with your group in advance.

A few words of Bahasa Indonesia go a long way with staff and drivers. *Terima kasih* means thank you, and *permisi* means excuse me when you are squeezing through a row.

## Dressing for a November night in Jakarta

November falls in Jakarta's rainy season. Heavy downpours in the afternoon and evening are normal, and the humidity stays high after dark. The roof is retractable, but don't count on it being closed. Bring a packable poncho if umbrellas are banned.

Plenty of fans will wear marching-band jackets and white face paint. Heavy costume layers in tropical humidity need water breaks, so drink before you queue. Comfortable closed shoes also make sense, because the floor gets crowded once the opening tone drops.

## Turning the trip into more than one night

North Jakarta has enough nearby to fill a day before the show. Ancol Dreamland is on the waterfront to the west. Just past it is Kota Tua, the old Dutch colonial quarter around Fatahillah Square, home to the Jakarta History Museum. Further west, the old Sunda Kelapa harbour still has wooden pinisi schooners tied up along the quay.

Kelapa Gading, east of the stadium, is a dining district known for its malls and Chinese-Indonesian restaurants. It works well for a late meal while the traffic around JIS clears. Give the stadium area at least an hour after the encore before you try to head south.
