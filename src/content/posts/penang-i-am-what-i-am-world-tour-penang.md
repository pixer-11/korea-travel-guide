## One night, one arena

This is Penang's date on a touring Mandopop show. The I AM WHAT I AM World Tour plays SPICE Arena on November 28, 2026, a Saturday. The organiser publishes ticket links, seating plans and door times through its own channels. This guide covers the venue, the trip there and the night itself.

Penang suits a Mandopop show. Hokkien and Mandarin are everyday languages on the island, so the audience is mostly local, along with fans from Kedah, Perak and across the causeway in Seberang Perai.

## Inside SPICE Arena

SPICE Arena belongs to the Setia SPICE complex, Penang's main convention and exhibition centre. It is an enclosed, air-conditioned indoor hall, so tropical rain doesn't affect the show. The convention halls and the landscaped grounds sit around the arena. Those grounds fill with fans before and after concerts.

Seating is arranged around the arena floor. The specific layout for this tour comes from the organiser's seating plan, not from the building. Large merchandise and fan-club tables usually go up along the concourse outside the hall.

## Getting to Relau

The arena is in Relau, in the island's south. Bayan Lepas and Penang International Airport are a short drive south of it, and the island end of the first Penang Bridge is close by.

- **From George Town:** roughly 30–40 minutes by car, longer in Saturday evening traffic along the Jelutong Expressway.
- **From the airport:** one of the shortest transfers to any Penang venue, so fans flying in from Kuala Lumpur or Singapore often stay near Bayan Lepas.
- **By ride-hailing:** Grab is how most concert-goers arrive. Pickups after the show are slow while the whole arena empties at once.
- **Mainland visitors:** usually drive across the Penang Bridge, which runs to the island end near the venue.

Queensbay Mall is a short drive away and is a common meet-up point for dinner before the show. Hotels around Bayan Lepas and Bayan Baru are the closest places to sleep. Visitors who want the heritage core usually stay in George Town and accept the longer ride.

## How concert night at SPICE works

Malaysian arena concerts follow a predictable pattern. Fans usually arrive well before doors to buy merchandise, because popular sizes run out at the stalls. Bag checks happen at the entrance. Professional cameras, tripods and outside food are commonly refused at Malaysian arena shows, and the organiser's terms list what this tour allows.

- **Tickets:** they're digital, scanned from a phone at the gate. Fans charge their phones beforehand rather than rely on venue Wi-Fi.
- **Payment at stalls:** card and e-wallets such as Touch 'n Go eWallet and DuitNow QR are widely accepted, though cash still helps at smaller vendors.
- **Fan culture:** light sticks, banners and fan-club chants are normal at Mandopop shows. Singing along to the hits is expected, not frowned on.
- **Leaving:** many people wait 20–30 minutes inside the grounds, or walk to a quieter road before calling a Grab, rather than join the pickup crush at the main drop-off.

Penang's late-night hawker scene makes a good last stop after the encore. Fans heading back to George Town often stop at the hawker centres along the way for char koay teow or a bowl of Hokkien mee.
