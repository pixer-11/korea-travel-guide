## Inside the old Putra stadium

Locals of a certain age still call the venue by an older name. Unifi Arena opened as Putra Indoor Stadium, built for the 1998 Commonwealth Games, and was later known as Axiata Arena before its current naming deal. It's one of the largest indoor arenas in Malaysia. It has hosted badminton finals, K-pop tours and Western pop acts.

The building sits on its own within the sports complex. The open plazas around it are where the merch lines and photo spots usually form. The bowl is fully enclosed and air-conditioned.
