## Two shopfronts, one long browse

The address is 14-15 John Street, which tells you something already: this bookshop spans two numbers on a narrow Georgian side street. Inside, the shelving runs between mismatched armchairs and odd pieces of furniture you are meant to sit in. Free hot drinks are offered to browsers, so you can settle in with a mug.

It is a general bookshop with a strong fiction streak and a habit of championing titles the chains overlook. Handwritten staff recommendations sit beside the books they are vouching for. It has picked up national awards for independent bookselling, and reviewers rate it consistently and unusually highly.

Plan on 45 minutes if you only browse. Allow longer if you start a conversation at the counter, because that is where the shop really earns its reputation.

## Asking the booksellers properly

At Mr B's, the conversation with staff is the point of the visit. Here's how to get the most from it:

- **Lead with a book you loved**, not a genre. "Something like *Stoner*" gets you further than "literary fiction".
- **Say what you didn't like too.** Staff use it to narrow things down.
- **Ask about the reading booth.** The shop rents out a private booth, and its paid "Reading Spa" consultations are booked ahead as gifts. Details and current prices are on the shop's official website.
- **Buy what you're handed.** Booksellers here live off sales, not footfall, so leaving with a book is how you thank them.

Cards are taken at the till as normal. Gift subscriptions, where the shop picks books for someone over a year, are popular presents. Ask in store if you're shopping for a reader back home.

## Fitting it into a Bath day

Currently the shop opens 9:30am–5:30pm Monday to Saturday. Sunday hours are shorter, 11am–5pm, so don't leave it until late on a Sunday afternoon. Hours can change around holidays, so check before a special trip.

A rainy morning is the obvious time to come, and the free drinks and armchairs are made for it. On a dry day, slot it between the Roman Baths and the uphill walk to the Royal Crescent, since John Street sits roughly halfway. If you arrive soon after opening, you are more likely to get a bookseller's full attention before the town centre gets busy.

## Around John Street

The shop is in the upper town's grid of honey-coloured Bath stone. Most of Bath's Georgian highlights are within ten minutes on foot:

- **Milsom Street**: the main shopping street, one block east.
- **Queen Square**: a minute or two west, with its obelisk and plane trees.
- **Jane Austen Centre**: on Gay Street, running up from Queen Square. It pairs well with a bookshop visit.
- **The Circus and Royal Crescent**: continue up Gay Street for the city's two famous Georgian set pieces.
- **Assembly Rooms**: a short walk north on Bennett Street.

From Bath Spa railway station it is about a 15-minute walk north through the centre, past the Abbey. There's no parking at the shop. Use one of the city's park-and-ride sites or a central car park, then walk.
