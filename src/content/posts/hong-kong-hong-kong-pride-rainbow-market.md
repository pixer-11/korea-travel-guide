You are in the middle of Hong Kong Island's business district, and the transport links show it.

On Saturdays, Central's office towers are mostly quiet, but the footbridges and the Queen's Road shopping strip stay busy all afternoon.

## Browsing the stalls with Hong Kong manners

Small stall holders in Hong Kong commonly take cash. Many also accept FPS or PayMe transfers by QR code. Visitors who carry some Hong Kong dollar notes and coins avoid getting stuck at a cash-only table. An Octopus card covers MTR and tram fares but won't work at every pop-up stall.

Photography calls for care. Not everyone at a Pride event in Hong Kong is out at work or at home, so regulars ask before photographing faces. When filming a performance, they frame the stage rather than the crowd. Hong Kong takes personal privacy seriously, and a quick "OK to take a photo?" is normal.
