## Why this bar made the 2026 list

Bar Mood has a clear idea behind it. It takes the spirits a global cocktail bar uses and runs them through Taiwanese flavours, so a gin or whisky you already know comes back tasting of somewhere else. That approach earned it a place among Asia's 50 Best Bars in 2026.

Two infusions come up again and again when people describe the bar:

- **Butter-washed gin.** This is fat-washing. Melted butter is stirred into the spirit, chilled until it sets, then lifted off. The flavour and a rounder texture stay behind, and the fat doesn't.
- **Chrysanthemum whisky.** Chrysanthemum is a familiar Taiwanese tea flower. Its floral, faintly bitter note is pushed into a brown spirit.

## Getting there and getting home

Plan the ride home before the first drink. The Taipei Metro (MRT) stops running around midnight. If the bar is close to a station, check the time of the last train for your line on the Taipei Metro app or the station boards.

After that, taxis are easy to find. You can flag the yellow cabs on the street or book one through an app. One rule catches visitors out: eating and drinking are banned in the paid areas of MRT stations and on trains. That includes a takeaway coffee for the morning after. The fines are enforced.

Allow about two hours for two or three drinks and a conversation with the bartender. That's how long a menu like this takes to explore properly.
