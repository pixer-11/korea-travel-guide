## Concert-night manners, Malaysian style

A few habits make concert nights smoother.

**Paying.** Malaysia is close to cashless in the city. Cards work almost everywhere, and QR payments through DuitNow or the Touch 'n Go eWallet are standard at food stalls and kiosks. The same Touch 'n Go card pays rail fares. Tipping is not expected, and restaurant bills often include a service charge already.

**Queueing.** Malaysian concert crowds queue in orderly lines for merchandise and entry. Merch lines at K-pop and K-band shows often form hours before doors. Fans who want a particular item usually join the line soon after it starts forming, because popular sizes go first.

## Late-October weather and what to carry

October falls in Kuala Lumpur's inter-monsoon season. Heavy afternoon and evening thunderstorms are routine, and they usually blow over within an hour or two. Heat and humidity stay high even after dark.
