## The venue: Smart Araneta Coliseum

The concert is slated for **Smart Araneta Coliseum**, often nicknamed the "Big Dome," inside the Araneta City complex in Cubao, Quezon City. ## Getting there

Araneta City sits directly above **Cubao Station**, served by both the **MRT-3** (Cubao Station) and **LRT-2** (Araneta Center-Cubao Station), making rail the most reliable way to avoid Manila traffic on event night. From MRT-3 Cubao, it's a short covered walkway into the Araneta City complex; LRT-2's Araneta Center-Cubao station connects via a pedestrian bridge as well. If arriving by car or ride-hailing app, build in significant extra time — traffic around Cubao intensifies dramatically before big-arena events, and street parking is scarce, though the Araneta City complex has multi-level parking that fills up fast on concert nights.

## What to do around the venue

Araneta City is a mini-district in itself, anchored by Gateway Mall right next to the Coliseum, so there's no need to rush straight home after the show. Gateway has a wide range of Filipino and international food options for a pre- or post-concert meal, plus a supermarket and pharmacy for last-minute needs. Fans queuing for merchandise or general-admission entry often gather hours early along the Coliseum's outer walkways, so if you want a specific spot near the stage, arriving well ahead of doors is standard practice for shows here.
