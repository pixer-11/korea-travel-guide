## One Saturday on Cotai

Macau gets exactly one night of this tour. VERIVERY play G Box at Galaxy Macau on Saturday, December 19, 2026, a stop on the <GIVE ME FIVE, KEEP THAT VIBE> tour they share with VERRER, their fan community.

Galaxy Macau is easy to spot: the gold-faced towers with domed crowns standing on Cotai, the reclaimed strip between Taipa and Coloane. December is Macau's cool, dry season. Evenings call for a light jacket rather than an umbrella, which suits a queue that snakes along a resort walkway.

## Who's on stage

VERIVERY are a South Korean boy group from Jellyfish Entertainment. They debuted in January 2019 with "Ring Ring Ring." From the start, their pitch was a group that has a hand in its own videos and visual content, and fans followed them for that as much as the music.

The tour title leans into a playful, high-five energy. The setlist and running order are not part of what's known here, so this guide doesn't guess at either. Expect a K-pop concert format: choreographed sets, talk segments with the crowd, and an encore built around fan chants.

## Getting to G Box at Galaxy Macau

Most fans arrive from Hong Kong or mainland China. The usual routes:

- **From Hong Kong by sea:** ferries run to Taipa Ferry Terminal, the closest terminal to Cotai.
- **From Hong Kong by road:** buses cross the Hong Kong–Zhuhai–Macao Bridge to the Macau port, followed by a bus or taxi to Cotai.
- **From Zhuhai and Hengqin:** cross at the Border Gate (Portas do Cerco) or at the Lotus Bridge checkpoint, which lands right on Cotai.
- **Within Macau:** the Taipa Line of the Macau Light Rapid Transit runs through Cotai. Public buses and taxis serve the resort strip too.

Galaxy runs complimentary resort shuttles from the ferry terminals and border crossings. Routes and timetables are posted at each pick-up bay. After the encore, the taxi rank fills fast, so many concertgoers walk to a neighbouring resort's rank or take a shuttle back to the ferry.

Cameras with detachable lenses are typically banned at concert venues in Macau, and staff enforce filming rules on the night. Macau's currency is the pataca, though Hong Kong dollars are widely accepted. Inside the resort, cards and mobile payments work at most counters, and tipping is not expected.

## Before doors and after the encore

Galaxy Macau's Promenade is a covered walk of shops and restaurants inside the resort. Next door, Broadway Macau's food street serves Macanese and Cantonese snacks at casual counters, and it's where many fans eat before the show.

Taipa Village sits a short ride north. Its lanes around Rua do Cunha sell pork chop buns, almond cookies and egg tarts. If you stay over for Sunday, the Macau peninsula has the Ruins of St. Paul's and the black-and-white wave paving of Senado Square, about a twenty-minute drive from Cotai. Many travelling fans book a room on Cotai itself, which turns the late-night trip home into a short walk.
