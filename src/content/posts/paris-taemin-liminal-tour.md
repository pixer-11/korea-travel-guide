## A Monday night at Porte de la Chapelle

The date is Monday, November 16, 2026. On that night Taemin brings his LiMiNaL solo tour to the Adidas Arena on the northern edge of Paris. He is the SHINee member who joined the group as its youngest at fourteen. Since his 2014 solo debut with "Danger" he has built a separate career on stage choreography and concept-heavy staging.

His solo catalogue gives you a sense of the register: "Move", "Criminal", "Advice", "Guilty". These are slow-burn, physical performances where the dancing carries as much weight as the vocal. ## The arena built for 2024

The Adidas Arena is the newest large indoor hall in the city. It was built for the Paris 2024 Olympics, where it hosted badminton and rhythmic gymnastics. Paris Basketball uses it as a home court. For concerts it holds far fewer people than the Accor Arena at Bercy, so even the upper tiers sit fairly close to the stage.

It stands at Porte de la Chapelle, where the 18th arrondissement meets the Périphérique ring road. The neighbourhood is mid-regeneration: new housing, a university campus nearby and plenty of traffic. Nobody walks out here for the scenery. People come for the arena, and the arena is the reason the whole corner has been rebuilt.

## Getting to Porte de la Chapelle

Public transport is the easy option. Parking near the ring road is limited, and traffic backs up badly after a show.

- **Métro Line 12**: Porte de la Chapelle station is right next to the arena. Line 12 runs south through Pigalle, Saint-Lazare, Madeleine and Concorde.
- **Tram T3b**: it stops at Porte de la Chapelle and runs along the edge of the city, which is useful if you are staying in the east or west of Paris.
- **From Gare du Nord**: take Line 4 north to Marcadet-Poissonniers, then change to Line 12 northbound.
- **From Charles de Gaulle airport**: the RER B goes to Gare du Nord, and from there you follow the same change.

The show falls on a weeknight, and the Métro runs past midnight. Most of the crowd pours straight into Porte de la Chapelle station at the end. Many fans walk one stop south to Marx Dormoy to get onto a less packed platform.

## Fosse or gradins: how the night works

At French concerts the standing floor is called the **fosse**. The tiered seats are the **gradins**, and some tickets also carry a block and row number. The fosse is first come, first served, so fans who want the barrier usually queue outside for hours. Seated ticket holders tend to arrive closer to doors and walk straight to their block.

French venues run bag searches at the entrance. Large bags and professional cameras are commonly refused at arena shows. Attendees usually travel light and keep their ID with their ticket.

K-pop crowds in Paris are loud, organised and bilingual. Expect Korean fan chants shouted with a French accent, and fan-made banners or slogans handed out in the queue. SHINee's fandom colour is pearl aqua, and plenty of fans bring the official SHINee lightstick. Under French law, reselling a ticket above face value without the organiser's permission is illegal. Fans therefore stick to authorised resale and avoid strangers on social media.

## Before and after the show

The streets around the arena have little worth stopping for, so most fans eat further south first. Two areas suit a concert night:

- **Rue du Faubourg Saint-Denis near La Chapelle (Line 2)**: this is the city's Sri Lankan and South Indian strip, sometimes called Little Jaffna. It has dosas, kothu and sweet shops, and it's one short Métro hop from the arena.
- **Rue Sainte-Anne and the streets around Pyramides**: Paris's Japanese and Korean restaurant cluster in the 1st and 2nd arrondissements. It's where many K-pop fans go for bibimbap or fried chicken before heading north.

For accommodation, visitors usually stay around Gare du Nord, Gare de l'Est or the lower slopes of Montmartre. All three are a short ride from Porte de la Chapelle and well linked to the airports and the Eurostar. Anyone making a weekend of it can climb Sacré-Cœur on the Sunday, as it's only a few stops down Line 12 from the arena.
