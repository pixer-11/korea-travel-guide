## A bookstore you need a map for

Pick up a store map at the door before you do anything else. Powell's takes up a whole block on West Burnside between NW 10th and NW 11th Avenues. Inside, the floors are split into colour-coded rooms, and each room covers its own set of subjects. Signs hang over the stairwells, and pillars carry numbers so staff can send you to a particular aisle.

What sets it apart is the shelving. Used copies sit right beside new ones, so the same title can show up in several editions in a single row. Walter Powell opened the store in 1971, and it is widely described as the largest independent new-and-used bookstore in the world. Upstairs is a rare book room, which is worth a look even if you only browse the glass cases.

## Getting to West Burnside

The address is 1005 W Burnside St, Portland, OR 97209. The Portland Streetcar's NS Line runs along NW 10th and NW 11th Avenues, which puts you about a block from the doors. From Pioneer Courthouse Square in downtown it's roughly a ten-minute walk north.

Driving here is a hassle. Street parking in the Pearl District is metered and fills up quickly, so take transit or walk if you can. If you're already downtown, put on a rain layer and go on foot. Portland's grey months are exactly when the store is at its most useful.

## When to walk in

At the moment Powell's is open 10am–9pm seven days a week. On weekdays the quietest times are usually 10am–11am and 8pm–9pm. On weekends only 8pm–9pm is reliably calm.

Don't go between 11am and 7pm on a Saturday or Sunday. That's when the aisles fill up, the registers back up and the narrow sections get crowded. Give yourself at least 90 minutes. Serious browsers can easily spend half a day, and on a quiet weekday evening the last hour before close is the easiest time to move around.

## Browsing the Powell's way

This is one of Portland's most-visited stops, so you won't have the place to yourself. These habits help:

- **Ask at the info desks.** Staff can check stock and tell you which room and pillar a title is in. That's quicker than wandering.
- **Use the shelf-talkers.** Handwritten staff recommendation cards are tucked along the shelves. Follow them if you want suggestions you won't find on bestseller lists.
- **Compare editions.** A used copy is often shelved right next to the new one. Look at condition and price on the sticker before you choose.
- **Search online first.** The website shows what's in stock at the store, which saves you combing a whole room for one book.
- **Pay at the nearest register.** There are several around the building, and cards are accepted.

The ground-floor gift section has Powell's-branded totes, mugs and literary goods. That's the easiest place to pick up a souvenir if you're travelling carry-on only. If you're selling used books, check the website for how buying currently works before you carry a heavy box across town.

Once you're done, the Pearl District's galleries and Jamison Square are a few blocks north. Lan Su Chinese Garden in Old Town Chinatown is about a fifteen-minute walk east.
