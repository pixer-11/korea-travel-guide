## Why this show matters

The Weeknd hasn't played Singapore in nearly eight years. This time he's landing at the 55,000-capacity National Stadium, his biggest local production yet, over two nights — October 2 and 3, 2026.

The After Hours Til Dawn Stadium Tour has already broken box-office records across North America and Europe. It threads together his After Hours and Dawn FM eras into one sprawling, cinematic set — moody red lighting, towering stage architecture, a voice that still cuts through a stadium without visible strain.

## Getting to National Stadium

National Stadium sits inside the Singapore Sports Hub in Kallang, on the eastern edge of the city centre.

- **MRT**: Stadium station (Circle Line), which lets out almost directly into the Sports Hub concourse.
- **Alternative**: Mountbatten or Kallang stations, both a short walk away, useful if Stadium station gets congested post-show.
- **Driving**: Parking at the Sports Hub is limited and fills fast on event nights — public transport is the far less stressful option.

Budget extra time getting in. Stadium concerts here routinely see queues at bag-check and ticket scanning that stretch back toward the MRT concourse an hour before doors.

## Tickets and lineup

Creepy Nuts, the Japanese hip-hop duo behind the global viral hit "Bling-Bang-Bang-Born," open both nights — a genuinely interesting pairing rather than a filler support act.

Ticket categories for stadium tours like this typically span standing pit, lower tier seating, and upper tier, with prices varying widely by tier. Because exact pricing and on-sale windows haven't been locked into this guide, treat any number you see elsewhere with caution.

- Confirm on-sale dates directly through the official tour website and Ticketmaster Singapore.
- Watch for presale windows (fan club, credit card) that often open before general sale.
- Be wary of resale sites quoting inflated prices before tickets have even gone on sale.

## What to expect inside

Expect a long night. Doors typically open well before the headline set, with the support act easing the stadium in as the sky darkens over Kallang.

The production leans heavily on visual storytelling — expansive screens, a stage designed to feel like a city block, and lighting states that shift the entire stadium from blood-red to washed-out blue between songs. Sound at National Stadium is generally strong for a roofed stadium, though upper-tier seats can feel a step removed from the visual detail lower down.

Bring layers. Air-conditioning in the Sports Hub concourses can be aggressive even when the bowl itself feels warm from body heat and stage lighting.

## When to go and how long to stay

Two nights means you can choose based on your schedule, though touring artists sometimes tweak setlists slightly between shows, which is part of the appeal for fans catching both.

Plan for the whole evening — doors, support set, changeover, and the headline show can easily add up to four hours or more. Public transport around Kallang runs late on event nights, but it's still worth checking last-train timings for the Circle Line before you commit to staying till the very end.

## How to visit like a local

Singaporean concertgoers tend to arrive early and camp near the pit barriers if they've got standing tickets — doors-open time is treated as a hard deadline, not a suggestion.

Payment inside the venue is almost entirely cashless: expect tap-to-pay or mobile wallets at food and merchandise stalls, so have a card or phone ready rather than hunting for an ATM.

- Don't expect to buy official merch on the night without a queue — lines form immediately at doors and can take over an hour.
- Locals usually exit via Mountbatten or Kallang MRT rather than fighting the crush at Stadium station.
- Tipping isn't expected anywhere in Singapore, including at the venue's food stalls.

The biggest mistake overseas fans make is assuming they can buy tickets at the door. Stadium shows here are almost always sold entirely in advance — confirm your tickets, gate, and seating tier well before October.
