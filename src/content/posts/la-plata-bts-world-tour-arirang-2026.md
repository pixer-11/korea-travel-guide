## Three nights in the provincial capital

The dates are October 21, 23 and 24, 2026. Thursday the 22nd is a gap night, so the run covers four calendar days but only three concerts. BTS has never played Argentina before. The ARIRANG tour's arrival makes La Plata the country's first stop for the group.

Each night is a separate ticketed show. Fans who hold tickets for more than one date often stay in the region for the whole stretch. Prices, seating sections and resale rules belong to the official ticket seller, so this page doesn't quote any of them.

## Estadio Único, on the edge of the grid

The venue's full name is Estadio Único Diego Armando Maradona; it took the footballer's name in 2020. Locals still call it simply "el Único". A white tensile roof shades the stands while the pitch stays open to the sky, and that silhouette is visible from well outside the gates. The ground holds around 50,000 people and has hosted major international stadium concerts for more than a decade.

La Plata sits roughly 60 km southeast of central Buenos Aires. Most international visitors fly into Ezeiza (EZE) and base themselves in the capital. From there the routes are:

- **Train:** the Roca line runs from Constitución station to La Plata station, a little over an hour.
- **Road:** the Autopista Buenos Aires–La Plata connects the two cities directly; coaches and ride-hailing use it.
- **Locally:** taxis and remises cover the last stretch from the city centre to the stadium.

## Getting through the gates and the crush afterwards

For an event this size, fans usually reach the stadium hours before showtime. Exits are slow after a sold-out stadium show, and late trains back to Buenos Aires fill fast. Many travellers hire a remis for the round trip instead, or they stay overnight in La Plata itself. Some book with fan groups that run chartered buses from the capital.

Inside, ARMY in Argentina follows the habits seen at every tour stop. People carry the official ARMY Bomb lightstick, organise fan projects with printed banners or coloured slogans, and practise the fanchants before the show. Spanish is the working language at the gates and kiosks. A few words help: *entrada* (ticket), *puerta* (gate), *salida* (exit).

Payment is mixed. Cards and Mercado Pago QR codes are widely accepted in La Plata, and pesos in cash still smooth taxis and street vendors. Argentines eat dinner late, so restaurants stay open after the show. Parrillas near the centre stay busy well past 11pm.

## The city around the concert

La Plata was founded in 1882 as a planned capital. Numbered streets cross on a square grid, cut by diagonal avenues meeting at plazas. That layout makes the centre easy to navigate on foot during the gap day.

- **Catedral de La Plata:** a huge neo-Gothic cathedral facing Plaza Moreno, among the largest in Latin America.
- **Paseo del Bosque:** the city's big park, with lakes and eucalyptus.
- **Museo de La Plata:** inside the park, famous for its fossil and natural history collections.

October is mid-spring in Argentina. The rest day on the 22nd gives fans who hold tickets on both sides of it a full day between shows for the city.
