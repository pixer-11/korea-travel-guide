## Why go

Tiffany & Co.'s Blue Box Café is one of the more unusual crossovers in Hong Kong's dining scene: a jewelry house running a full café concept, wrapped in the brand's signature robin's-egg blue, inside one of Causeway Bay's most polished shopping addresses. What sets this outpost apart from Tiffany's other Blue Box Cafés worldwide (New York, Tokyo, Shanghai) is the kitchen talent behind it — Agustin Balbi, chef of the Michelin-starred Argentine restaurant Andō in Wan Chai, now oversees the menu. The pastry program has become the headline act, with a Classic Egg Tart and a Pistachio Tart both circulating widely on social media and turning into the café's calling cards. For visitors, it's less about a quiet meal and more about experiencing a design-forward, photogenic space with genuinely credentialed cooking behind the counter.

## Where it is and how to get there

The café sits inside Lee Gardens Three, part of the Lee Gardens cluster of towers that anchors Causeway Bay's upscale retail district, alongside Lee Gardens One and Two and the nearby Hysan Place. This puts it within easy walking distance of Causeway Bay MTR Station (Island Line), typically a five-to-ten-minute walk depending on which exit you use — Exit F1 or F2 tend to be the most direct toward the Lee Gardens complex. Times Square, another Causeway Bay shopping landmark, is a short walk away if you want to combine the visit with a wider shopping loop. Trams running along Yee Wo Street and Hennessy Road are also an option if you're coming from Wan Chai or Sheung Wan and prefer a slower, street-level approach.

## What to see and eat

Expect the interiors to lean fully into Tiffany's aesthetic: the trademark blue used across table settings, upholstery, and packaging, with jewelry-inspired detailing throughout the dining room. The draw for most visitors, though, is the pastry case. The Classic Egg Tart reinterprets a Hong Kong staple — the custard tart found in nearly every neighborhood bakery — through a fine-dining lens, while the Pistachio Tart has become one of the most photographed items on the menu. Beyond the tarts, expect a broader café menu of light bites and drinks, though specific dishes and prices aren't listed here since menus at newly reopened spots shift quickly — check the café's current offerings once you arrive or on Tiffany's official channels beforehand.

## When to go

Weekday late mornings or early afternoons, outside the lunch rush (roughly before noon or after 2:30pm), tend to be calmer than weekends, when Causeway Bay's shopping crowds peak. Because this is a newly reopened, trending location with limited seating, arriving right at opening is often the most reliable way to get a table without a long wait. Avoid Hong Kong public holidays and the run-up to major shopping weekends if you want a more relaxed visit.

## How to visit like a local

Book ahead if reservations are offered — a café this design-driven and freshly reopened, attached to a global luxury brand, is pulling both tourists and locals curious about the Balbi connection, so walk-in waits can stretch long, especially at weekend peak hours. If you can't reserve, put your name down and use the wait to browse Lee Gardens Three or grab a coffee elsewhere in the complex rather than standing in line. Payment across Hong Kong cafés and restaurants at this price point is almost always by card or mobile wallet (Octopus, Apple Pay, or Alipay HK are all widely accepted), so don't worry about carrying cash specifically for this stop. Tipping isn't customary in Hong Kong dining the way it is in the West — a service charge is often already added to the bill, so check before adding extra. The most common visitor mistake is treating this purely as a photo-op and skipping the food: the point of this iteration is that a Michelin-recognized chef is behind the pastries, so it's worth actually ordering rather than just snapping the blue interior and leaving. Dress code isn't strict, but given the setting inside a high-end retail tower, smart-casual fits the room better than beachwear or gym clothes. Above all, confirm current hours, reservation policy, and menu directly with Tiffany & Co. or the café before your visit, since details at newly reopened spots change fast.

## Nearby to pair with your visit

Causeway Bay rewards lingering: Times Square offers dozens of additional dining and shopping options, Victoria Park is a green break a short walk northeast, and the Lee Gardens precinct itself hosts rotating art installations and seasonal displays worth building into the same afternoon.
