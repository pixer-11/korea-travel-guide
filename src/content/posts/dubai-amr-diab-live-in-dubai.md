## Doors, timing and the Dubai concert rhythm

Dubai concert crowds skew toward arriving late. Doors for arena shows here generally open one to two hours before the scheduled start, but a meaningful share of the audience filters in during the opening act or even after the headliner's first few songs. Those who come in at doors get the seats near the front and the clearest photos, while the local habit is to drift in fashionably late.

Arena shows in Dubai usually list the doors time, the start time and any opening act separately, and these vary from show to show rather than following a fixed hour from past concerts.
