Xiangshang Market sits in the middle of Taichung's West District, a neighborhood already dense with boutique cafés and slow-renovated old houses. Spaccie is tucked right beside the market itself, so the walk in is a contrast in textures.

That shift from market chaos to café stillness is part of the charm.

## Getting there

Spaccie sits beside Xiangshang Market in Taichung's West District, one of the city's most walkable neighborhoods for café-hopping. The West District is easily reached by city bus or taxi from central Taichung, and it rewards arriving on foot or by scooter rather than driving, since parking near the market can be tight.

Treat a visit here as part of a wider loop — the West District is dotted with small independent cafés and shops worth wandering between, so budget extra time beyond just this one stop.

## When to go

Because this is a newly opened, buzzy spot, the safest move is to check current hours and whether reservations are needed before you go. New cafés in Taichung often start with limited hours and adjust as they find their rhythm, so don't assume a fixed schedule.

Weekday mornings are typically your best bet at any small, popular new café — before the after-work and weekend crowds discover it fully. Avoid assuming late-night opening; confirm this directly rather than guessing.

## How to visit like a local

Order at the counter, as is standard at Taiwanese cafés of this size — don't wait for table service. Payment is usually a mix of cash and mobile pay (LINE Pay and similar apps are common across Taichung), though it's worth having cash as backup for a newer, smaller operation.

Tipping isn't expected in Taiwanese cafés, so skip that worry entirely. If you want the matcha latte at its freshest pour, arrive not long after opening rather than mid-afternoon when the counter may be busier.

The mistake visitors often make with newly trending cafés like this is showing up without checking if a booking is needed — small spaces fill fast once social media catches on. Call ahead or check their official channels the same day.

Pair your visit with a walk through Xiangshang Market itself before or after your coffee. Seeing the working market right next door gives context to the café's setting, and it's a good way to fold a local food stop into the same short outing.
