## A first star for the valleys

For decades, eating in Cappadocia meant one script. Pottery-kebab jugs get cracked open at the table, Kayseri-style mantı comes under yoghurt and chilli butter, and dinner sits on a hotel terrace above the fairy chimneys. Revithia changes that script. In the 2026 Michelin Guide Türkiye it became the first restaurant in Cappadocia to earn a Michelin Star.

The star matters because of where the cooking starts. The kitchen builds its menu around nearly forgotten regional recipes, the kind of dishes Anatolian home cooks made and stopped making. It then reworks them with modern techniques. You come here for central Anatolia's own food, cooked with care, not for an international tasting menu that happens to be in Turkey.

## What's in the name

*Revithia* is the Greek word for chickpeas. That fits a region whose villages kept Greek-speaking Orthodox communities until the population exchange of 1923. You can still see that past in the carved church façades of Mustafapaşa and the Göreme Open-Air Museum.

We have not been given the menu, and dishes at a recipe-revival kitchen change often. So we won't guess at courses. These are the regional staples it makes sense to ask about:

- **Pastırma and sucuk**, the cured beef that Kayseri is known for
- **Mantı**, the tiny dumplings of the same Kayseri tradition
- **Pekmez**, grape molasses boiled down from Cappadocia's vineyards
- **Local wine** from native grapes such as Emir and Narince, grown in the volcanic soil around Ürgüp

## Securing a table at Revithia

A brand-new star in a region this visited means tables will go fast. Treat booking as part of your trip planning, not something to sort out on the day.

- **Reserve before you fly.** Contact the restaurant directly, or go through the Michelin Guide listing. Ask for the day's hours and whether there is a set menu.
- **Mind the balloon clock.** Most balloon flights launch before sunrise, and hotel pickups come in the dark. A long dinner the night before a flight makes for a short sleep. Book your Revithia evening for a night without an early alarm.
- **Paying and tipping.** Cards are standard at restaurants of this level in Turkey. Leaving around 10 percent is customary when service isn't already on the bill, so check the receipt first.
- **A few words help.** *Rezervasyon* means a booking, and *teşekkürler* means thank you. Staff will wish you *afiyet olsun*, which is roughly "enjoy your meal".

## Fitting it into a Cappadocia stay

Most visitors base themselves in Göreme, Uçhisar or Ürgüp. All three are a short taxi or transfer apart across the valleys. Kayseri Airport and Nevşehir Kapadokya Airport are the two arrival points, roughly an hour's drive or less into the region.

Revithia works as the anchor for a full day. Spend the morning in the Rose Valley or Love Valley, then go underground at the Derinkuyu or Kaymaklı underground cities in the afternoon. Watch sunset from Uçhisar Castle before dinner. If you arrange it ahead, taxis and hotel transfers handle the drive back.

Allow a long, unhurried evening. Starred meals rarely fit into an hour, and this one is a reason to visit the region, not a quick stop on the way through.
