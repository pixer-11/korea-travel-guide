## Two nights under the lotus roof

Zhou Shen plays Century Lotus Stadium on December 5 and 6, 2026. The stadium takes its name from its roof, a ring of white fabric membrane shaped like the petals of an opening lotus. It is open to the sky in the middle, so the weather is part of the show.

Zhou Shen first reached a national audience on The Voice of China in 2014. His unusually high, clear voice has since carried film and drama themes such as "Big Fish" (大鱼), the song most of the crowd can sing along to. Expect long ballads, hushed sections where tens of thousands of people go quiet, and a stadium full of glowing light sticks.

## Getting to Century Lotus Stadium

The stadium sits beside the Dongping River, south of Foshan's old centre. Foshan and Guangzhou effectively form one metro area, and the Guangfo Line runs straight between the two systems. That is why many fans stay in Guangzhou and ride across.

- **From Guangzhou:** take the Guangfo Line into Foshan, then change or take a short taxi ride to the stadium.
- **From Foshan Railway Station or Guangzhou South (high-speed rail):** a taxi or a DiDi ride-hail is the simplest last leg.
- **After the show:** ride-hail pickups crowd the stadium roads, so many attendees walk a few blocks away before calling a car.

December evenings in Foshan are mild by day but cool after dark in an open bowl. Fans usually bring a light jacket.

## Tickets, ID and the gate routine

Ticketing in the Mainland follows its own rules. Big concerts are normally sold through Chinese platforms such as Damai, and each ticket carries the buyer's real name and ID number. Foreign visitors use their passport number, and the same passport is shown at the gate.

- Tickets for real-name shows generally cannot be resold or transferred, which is how the system shuts out scalpers.
- Security screening is strict. Power banks, umbrellas, professional cameras and outside drinks are commonly restricted at Chinese stadium concerts.
- Payment around the venue runs on Alipay and WeChat Pay. Both apps now accept foreign bank cards, and street vendors rarely take cash.
- Fans typically queue at their assigned gate well before doors, and the stadium plaza fills with merchandise and fan-made giveaways.

Unofficial light sticks are sold everywhere outside. Many fans hold the official one because it syncs its colours with the show.

## Around the show: Foshan after the encore

Foshan rewards a spare morning. The Ancestral Temple (Zumiao) in Chancheng is the city's best-known sight, with Lingnan carving and ceramic roof ridges, and it is the place most linked with Wong Fei-hung and Cantonese martial arts. Lingnan Tiandi, the restored grey-brick lanes beside it, has places to eat that stay open late.

- **Shiwan's Nanfeng Ancient Kiln:** a working dragon kiln in the city's historic ceramics district.
- **Shunde:** the district south of the city, recognised by UNESCO as a City of Gastronomy, known for double-skin milk pudding (双皮奶) and fresh river fish.
- **Qinghui Garden:** one of Guangdong's four classical gardens, also in Shunde.

Visitors on the two-night run usually split their base between Chancheng, for the old town, and Guangzhou, for flights and hotel choice.
