## Eleven days at the board

This is India's top women's chess title. The National Women Chess Championship runs from November 18 to 28, 2026, in Telangana. It is a fixed stop on the All India Chess Federation's annual calendar, and the winner holds the national women's crown for the year.

National championships are played at classical time controls. That means long games, often several hours each, with rounds spread across the event's dates. For spectators, the pace is slow: the drama is in the clock times and the final hour of each round, not in rapid exchanges.

Telangana has real chess pedigree. Arjun Erigaisi, one of India's highest-rated grandmasters, comes from Warangal. Hyderabad has hosted national and international events before.

## Venue, tickets and the official source

The AICF is the source for venue, schedule and spectator details. It publishes the host venue, round timings and any ticket or entry arrangements on its official site. This guide names no venue or price, because neither appears in the facts we were given.

What to look for on the federation's event page:

- The host city and hall within Telangana
- The round schedule and the start time of each round
- Spectator rules and any registration for entry
- Links to live game broadcasts, which national events often carry

## Watching inside the playing hall

A chess hall works more like a library than a stadium. Players sit at numbered boards in rows. The top boards are usually set apart near the front, and spectators stand behind a rope or barrier. Arbiters walk the aisles, and the room stays close to silent for hours.

How spectators usually behave:

- Phones are switched off or left outside. Under FIDE anti-cheating rules, devices are tightly restricted near the boards.
- Nobody speaks to a player during a game, and nobody comments on positions within earshot.
- People move between boards slowly and quietly, and there is no flash photography.
- Analysis and conversation happen in the corridor or a separate commentary area, if the venue has one.

Many fans follow most rounds on the live broadcast and come to the hall for the decisive late rounds. The final days usually settle the title, which makes November 27–28 the busiest time for spectators.

## Getting to Telangana and around

Rajiv Gandhi International Airport at Shamshabad, Hyderabad, is the state's main gateway, with direct domestic links across India and international flights. Secunderabad and Hyderabad (Nampally) are the main railway hubs. The Hyderabad Metro runs across much of the city.

November is post-monsoon in Telangana: dry, with warm days and cooler evenings. That suits long hours indoors and evenings out.

Between rounds, visitors in Hyderabad usually head to these:

- Charminar and the Laad Bazaar bangle lanes
- Golconda Fort, on the city's western edge
- Hussain Sagar lake and its central Buddha statue
- Hyderabadi biryani, which is the city's signature meal
