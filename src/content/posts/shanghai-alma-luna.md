## Daiquiris, mojitos and the Havana canon

We weren't given a menu, so we won't guess at Alma Luna's list. It's still worth knowing the drinks a Cuban theme usually draws on. That way you can order with confidence, or ask the bartender how the house handles them.

The classic Cuban drinks, all built on rum:

- **Daiquiri**: rum, lime and sugar, shaken and served straight up. It is closely linked to El Floridita in Havana.
- **Mojito**: rum, lime, sugar, mint and soda. Havana's La Bodeguita del Medio is the bar most tied to it.
- **Cuba Libre**: rum and cola with lime. It's the simplest drink of the group.
- **El Presidente**: rum stirred with vermouth and orange liqueur. It's the spirit-forward choice for people who like a Manhattan.

If you only order one drink, make it a daiquiri. Lime, sugar and rum leave nothing to hide behind, so it shows you quickly how good the bar is.

## Booking, paying and ordering in a Shanghai cocktail bar

Shanghai's bars run on phones. Knowing a few habits makes the night go smoothly.

**Booking.** Popular new bars in the city often take reservations through WeChat or a Dianping listing. Walk-ins may also be possible. Ask ahead rather than assume, especially for a Friday or Saturday. A bar this new and this talked-about is unlikely to have quiet weekends yet. A weeknight is the more reasonable bet if you want a seat at the counter.

**Paying.** Alipay and WeChat Pay are how almost everyone in Shanghai pays. Both apps now let visitors link a foreign Visa or Mastercard. Set this up before you arrive. Many bars do take cards, but don't count on it.

**Tipping.** Tipping isn't a general custom in China. Some higher-end bars add a service charge, so read the bill before you add anything.

**Ordering.** Cocktail bartenders in Shanghai generally work in English as well as Mandarin. You can name a classic, or describe what you like: a spirit, sour or sweet, strong or light. ## Building a night around it

Shanghai's cocktail culture is spread across neighbourhoods, and most of the well-known rooms sit in the Former French Concession and Jing'an. Wherever Alma Luna turns out to be, a short Didi ride will usually get you to a second bar.

- **Start light, finish strong.** A mojito or Cuba Libre early in the night and an El Presidente later follows the order the classics naturally fall into.
