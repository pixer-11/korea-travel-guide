## Why this show matters

Kodaline built their name on widescreen, emotionally direct indie-rock — "All I Want," "High Hopes," "The One" — the kind of songs that turned festival fields and arenas alike into singalongs for over a decade. This farewell tour marks the Dublin quartet's official closing chapter, and Bangkok is one of the stops getting two dates instead of one, a sign of how deep their fanbase runs in Thailand.

For visitors, that's a rare chance to catch a career-spanning setlist in a purpose-built live venue rather than a converted stadium or multi-act festival bill.

## Where and when

The shows are set for **August 28–29, 2026** at **UOB Live**, the live-music arena inside **EmSphere**, the newer retail-and-entertainment complex attached to the EmQuartier/Emporium cluster on Sukhumvit Road. UOB Live is one of Bangkok's larger indoor concert venues, purpose-built for touring international acts, with tiered seating and standing floor sections typically used for shows of this scale. Exact doors/set times, ticket tiers, and whether the two nights share an identical setlist have not been confirmed publicly at the time of writing — treat the dates above as the current official window and double-check before finalizing plans.

## Getting to EmSphere / UOB Live

EmSphere sits directly beside **Phrom Phong BTS Station** (Sukhumvit Line), connected via skywalk — this is by far the easiest way in, especially with concert-night traffic on Sukhumvit. From the station, follow signage toward EmQuartier/EmSphere; the complex links all three "Em" buildings (Emporium, EmQuartier, EmSphere) at upper levels, so you can cut through air-conditioned malls rather than walking the street. Taxis and ride-hailing apps (Grab is standard in Bangkok) are workable but expect slowdowns on show nights; the BTS is the reliable fallback if you're worried about timing.

## What to expect inside

UOB Live hosts touring pop, rock, and K-pop-scale acts, so expect a professional sound and lighting rig sized for a proper arena set rather than a club gig. Bag checks and security screening are standard at Bangkok's larger venues, so arrive with enough buffer before doors — 45 minutes to an hour ahead is a safe habit even if exact doors times aren't yet published.

Merchandise stands and simple food/drink kiosks are typical for this size of venue, though don't expect a full meal — EmQuartier and EmSphere have plenty of restaurants and food courts to eat at beforehand.

## Making a trip of it

Since the run spans two consecutive nights, fans traveling from abroad often base themselves in the Sukhumvit/Phrom Phong/Thong Lo area to minimize commute fatigue — it puts you within a few BTS stops of the venue and inside one of Bangkok's densest restaurant-and-nightlife corridors for the days in between. August falls in Bangkok's rainy season, so pack a light poncho or umbrella for the walk between transit and the venue, even though most of the route is covered via the mall skywalks.

## How to visit like a local

Bangkok concertgoers overwhelmingly arrive by BTS rather than driving — trust the Sukhumvit Line and get off at Phrom Phong Station rather than fighting traffic. Tickets for major touring shows in Thailand are typically sold through official online platforms rather than at the door, and lines for entry can build fast in the hour before doors, so locals who care about floor position show up well ahead rather than assuming they can walk in at showtime.

Cash is still widely used at food stalls and small vendors around EmQuartier/EmSphere, but the malls themselves are fully card- and mobile-payment friendly, so you won't need to carry much Thai baht just for the venue itself. Tipping isn't expected at the concert or in mall food courts; it's more a restaurant-and-service custom, and even then it's modest. The single biggest mistake international fans make is underestimating Sukhumvit traffic and cutting their commute close — build in extra time, especially on the second night when repeat attendees know the venue and crowds move faster.

Because this is a farewell run, expect a heavier-than-usual local and regional fan turnout across both dates, so don't assume the second night will be quieter than the first.

Given how fluid tour details can be this far out, always cross-check the date, city, and any presale information against Kodaline's official website or verified ticketing partner before booking flights, hotels, or tickets.
