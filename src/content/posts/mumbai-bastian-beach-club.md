## Why Bastian Beach Club stands out

Mumbai has plenty of rooftop bars and hotel pool decks, but a dedicated beach club — one built around a central pool, daybeds, and a whitewashed Mediterranean design language — is a genuinely new format for the city. Bastian Beach Club, opened by restaurateur Ranjit Bindra and actor-entrepreneur Shilpa Shetty, fills that gap. It's the beach-club spinoff of the well-known Bastian restaurant brand, and it leans into an Ibiza-Mykonos aesthetic courtesy of designer Minal Chopra: think white cabanas, sea-facing loungers, and a pool that's as much a social stage as a place to swim. For a city that's always been sea-adjacent but rarely sea-immersed in its nightlife, this is a notable addition to Mumbai's trendy dining-and-lounging circuit.

## Where it is and getting there

The club is located in Juhu, one of Mumbai's most recognizable beachfront neighborhoods and long a hub for seafront promenades, film-industry bungalows, and beach-facing eateries. Juhu is roughly 25–30 minutes from Mumbai's domestic and international airports (Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport) depending on traffic, making it an easy add-on for visitors staying near the airport or in the western suburbs like Bandra and Andheri. The area is well served by taxis and app-based cabs; there's no direct metro or suburban rail station right at the door, so most visitors arrive by road. If you're coming from South Mumbai (Colaba, Fort, Marine Drive), budget 45 minutes to over an hour depending on traffic, especially during evening rush hour.

## What to expect: design, pool, and vibe

The signature feature is the central pool, ringed by daybeds positioned for sea views — a layout that borrows directly from the Mediterranean beach-club playbook rather than the usual Mumbai rooftop format. Minal Chopra's design uses a light, resort-style palette that's meant to feel like a holiday rather than a night out in the city. Expect a mix of daytime lounging energy and a livelier after-dark scene, since beach clubs of this style typically shift from relaxed daybed sessions in daylight to music-driven evenings once the sun goes down.

## What to eat and drink

Bastian's restaurants are known for pan-Asian and seafood-forward menus, and the beach club carries that signature through. Two dishes to look for are the Hamachi ceviche — a citrus-forward raw fish preparation typical of Bastian's coastal, Asian-inflected style — and the Arabian seabass, a whole or filleted fish dish that plays to the brand's seafood strength. Expect the drinks menu to lean into coastal, holiday-style cocktails designed to match the beach-club setting. Menus and pricing at new venues like this evolve quickly, so treat any specific dish names as a starting point for what the kitchen is known for rather than a fixed, unchanging menu — confirm current offerings when booking.

## When to go

If your priority is actually using the pool and daybeds without a crowd, aim for a weekday afternoon — beach clubs of this kind tend to peak on weekend evenings when the DJ sets and social crowd arrive. Mumbai's cooler months, roughly November through February, are generally the most comfortable time to spend hours outdoors by a pool; the monsoon season (June–September) and the peak summer heat (April–May) are far less ideal for open-air lounging. Evenings year-round tend to be more temperate than midday.

## How to visit like a local

Book your table or daybed in advance rather than walking in — venues built around limited pool-side seating fill up fast, especially on weekends, and walk-ins risk being turned away or seated far from the water feature that's the whole point of visiting. Dress for a beach-club setting: resort-casual works during the day, while evenings tend to skew slightly dressier once the lounge crowd arrives. Payment is almost universally by card or UPI/mobile payment in Mumbai's upscale hospitality scene, so don't rely on carrying cash for the bill, though keeping some on hand for valet or incidental tips is common practice. A frequent misstep among first-time visitors to any new Mumbai hotspot is assuming hours and reservation rules mirror a typical restaurant — call ahead or check official social channels for the day's operating hours, minimum spend, or entry policy, since these details are still settling at a newly opened venue and can change without much notice.
