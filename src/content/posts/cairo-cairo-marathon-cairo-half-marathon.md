## Race day in Heliopolis

The 14th Cairo Marathon is held on Friday, April 2, 2027. Friday is the start of Egypt's weekend, so on race morning the boulevards of northeast Cairo carry less traffic than on any weekday. Over 8,000 runners gather at Merryland Park, the old green space in Heliopolis, and race over one of three distances:

- **Half marathon (21.1km)**: the headline race and the reason for the event's English name
- **10km**: popular with club runners and first-timers moving up
- **5km**: the family and casual-runner option

Despite the "marathon" in the title, the facts list no full 42.2km distance for this edition. The half is the longest race on the day.

## Why Heliopolis works for running

Heliopolis is the garden suburb that Belgian industrialist Baron Édouard Empain founded in the early 1900s. It was laid out with wide avenues, arcaded streets and roundabouts, a rare stretch of Cairo where the roads have room for a pack of runners. The neo-Moorish arcades around Korba date from this period, and so do the Basilique church and the Hindu-temple outline of the Baron Empain Palace.

Merryland Park is a natural staging area: open lawns, trees and space for bag drops, warm-ups and finish-line crowds. Early April in Cairo usually brings cool mornings and hot middays. Spring is also the season of the khamaseen, dusty desert winds that can haze the sky and dry out your throat.

## Getting to Merryland and race-morning habits

The organiser publishes registration, bib collection and start-time details on its official site. Runners usually treat bib pickup as a separate errand in the days before the race, so race morning is just the run.

- **Metro**: Line 3 of the Cairo Metro runs under Heliopolis, and Al Ahram station is the usual stop for this part of the district.
- **Ride-hailing**: Uber and Careem are how most visiting runners cross the city, especially from downtown or Zamalek before the metro gets busy.
- **Where people stay**: out-of-town runners often book in Heliopolis or near Cairo International Airport, a short drive northeast. That way race morning starts with a short ride rather than a crawl across the city.

Supporters tend to gather near the finish inside the park. Families with 5km runners usually stay through the half-marathon finish.

## Running it the Cairo way

Small cash is the norm. Kiosks, juice stands and taxis around Heliopolis work mostly in Egyptian pounds, and runners usually carry a few small notes for a post-race drink. Tipping drivers and attendants small amounts is customary, and rounding up a fare is the usual way to do it.

Dress codes in Heliopolis are relaxed by Cairo standards. Still, runners heading out after the race usually pull a T-shirt on over a crop top or singlet. Friday's midday prayer brings a quiet stretch as shops pause, which suits a long, slow post-race breakfast.

Afterwards, many runners walk to the Baron Empain Palace or browse the arcades of Korba. Others head to the Basilique, which is still active as a church. With the Pyramids of Giza on the far side of the city, visitors usually save them for another day rather than squeeze them in after 21km.
