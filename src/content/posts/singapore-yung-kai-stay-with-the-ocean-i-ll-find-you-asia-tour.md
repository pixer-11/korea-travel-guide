## Inside Capitol Theatre

Capitol Theatre opened in 1930 as one of Singapore's grandest cinemas. It sat empty for years before a careful restoration reopened it in 2015 as part of the Capitol Singapore complex. The Capitol Kempinski Hotel and the Capitol Galleria shopping arcade share the block.

Inside, the auditorium keeps its period details:

- A domed ceiling painted with the signs of the zodiac
- Winged Pegasus figures flanking the stage area
- Stalls and a raised circle, so most seats look down onto a fairly close stage
- An Art Deco lobby facing Stamford Road, where queues usually form at the doors

The room seats fewer than a thousand people. Few seats are far from the stage, and a guitar-and-voice set carries clearly without arena-sized speakers.
