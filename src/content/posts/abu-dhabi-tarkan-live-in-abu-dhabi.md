## Etihad Arena, Yas Bay

Etihad Arena sits on Yas Bay Waterfront, Yas Island, next to Yas Bay Marina. It's Abu Dhabi's largest indoor arena, built for concerts, boxing and large-scale events, with a capacity that flexes by staging configuration. Yas Island also hosts Yas Mall, Ferrari World, Warner Bros. World and Yas Marina Circuit, all a short drive away.

- Nearest reference point: Yas Bay Waterfront, walkable from Yas Mall
- Parking: multi-storey and surface lots serve the Yas Bay precinct
- Transport: taxis and ride-hailing are the simplest route from central Abu Dhabi or Dubai; both are roughly 30-45 minutes by car depending on traffic
- Airport: Abu Dhabi International Airport is closer than Dubai's airports, roughly 20-25 minutes by car
