## The last night of COLOR ON

Milan closes the tour. POW's '2026 COLOR ON' is the group's first global tour, and it runs through six European cities before ending here on October 26, 2026.

That date is a Monday. Final shows usually have a different feel from the ones before them. Fans who followed the run across Europe often travel in for the closing night, and groups tend to treat the last stop as the farewell of the whole tour.

## Dates, tickets and where they live

There is one date in Milan: October 26, 2026. The tour's organiser publishes the dates, venue and ticket links for each city on its official channels. Those links are the reference point for which seats are real.

Resale posts on social media and unofficial marketplaces turn up around most K-pop tours in Europe. Fans who stick to the official ticket links avoid duplicate or fake tickets. Ticket prices are on the official sales page, and this guide does not repeat them.

Italian concerts often use name-on-ticket entry. Attendees usually carry photo ID that matches the booking name, because staff at the door may compare the two.

## Inside the hall, Milan style

Most of what fans do here is standard K-pop concert behaviour, plus a few things that are specific to Milan.

- **Lightsticks and banners:** fans bring the group's official lightstick and printed slogan cards, which are usually held up during set songs.
- **Fan chants:** European crowds learn the Korean chant lines ahead of the show. Many fans bring the lyrics on their phones.
- **Merch queues:** merchandise lines often form outside the venue hours before doors open. The most popular items sell out first.
- **Bags:** large Italian venues often limit bag size and turn away bulky items, so fans usually travel light.

Milan's metro accepts contactless bank cards at the turnstiles, so there is no need for a paper ticket. Once the metro stops for the night, the ATM night buses (*linee notturne*) take over.

## Getting to Milan for the show

Most international fans fly into Malpensa (MXP). The Malpensa Express train runs from there to Milano Cadorna and Milano Centrale. Linate (LIN) is closer to the city and has its own stop on metro line M4. Bergamo's Orio al Serio airport is linked to Milano Centrale by coach.

Milan has five metro lines, M1 to M5. Visitors without a strong preference usually stay near Centrale or along M1, M2 or M3 so that the late ride back is simple. Hotels around Centrale and Porta Garibaldi fill quickly with travellers arriving by train from other European cities.

Late October in Milan is cool. Coats and umbrellas are normal for queues outside. Clocks in Italy go back one hour on October 25, 2026, the night before the show. Fans arriving from the UK, Ireland or Portugal will find Milan one hour ahead of home.

On the day, fans who come in a few hours early usually spend them around the Duomo and the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II. Both are a short walk from Duomo station, where lines M1 and M3 meet.
