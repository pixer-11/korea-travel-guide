It sits inside the Bukit Jalil National Sports Complex in southern Kuala Lumpur, alongside Axiata Arena and the Aquatic Centre. ## Getting there

Bukit Jalil is well served by rail, which matters given how badly stadium-night traffic backs up around the complex:

- **LRT Bukit Jalil station** (Kelana Jaya Line) is the closest stop, roughly a 10–15 minute walk to the stadium gates.
- From KL Sentral, the Kelana Jaya Line connects directly, making it a straightforward one-line trip for anyone staying in the city center or near KLCC (via interchange).
- From KLIA, the KLIA Ekspres or KLIA Transit into KL Sentral connects to the Kelana Jaya Line toward Bukit Jalil.
- Ride-hailing (Grab) is widely used in the area, though surge pricing and long pickup queues after shows are common; walking to the LRT station and letting crowds thin before requesting a car is a common workaround.

## Nearby & practical tips

- Staying near LRT stations on the Kelana Jaya Line (KL Sentral, Bangsar, or even KLCC with an interchange) gives the easiest commute in and out.
- Bukit Jalil itself has hotels and serviced apartments within walking distance of the stadium, useful for anyone who prefers to skip the post-concert transit rush entirely.
- Pairing the trip with KL staples nearby (Petaling Street, Bukit Bintang, or the Batu Caves) makes sense, since a Bukit Jalil concert night alone doesn't fill a full Kuala Lumpur itinerary.
- Resale tickets for high-demand stadium shows in KL are a common scam target, which makes the promoter's own ticketing channels the safer route.
