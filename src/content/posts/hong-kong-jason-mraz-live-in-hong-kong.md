## One night, one venue, Wednesday 4 November

The date is Wednesday, November 4, 2026. Jason Mraz plays a single show at AsiaWorld-Expo on his Asia 2026 Tour, and there is no second night to fall back on.

He won Grammys for "Make It Mine" and for "Lucky", his duet with Colbie Caillat. Most of the room will also be waiting for "I'm Yours" and "I Won't Give Up". The facts given name no support act, set length or door time. Confirm timing and tickets on the official site.

## Out by the runways at Chek Lap Kok

AsiaWorld-Expo sits on Chek Lap Kok, the island that holds Hong Kong International Airport. It is a large exhibition and arena complex, and planes on approach and departure are a normal part of the view. Expect a big indoor hall, not a club. Plan on standing in the queue for security before you get anywhere near the floor.

November falls in Hong Kong's dry, mild season. The evening is usually comfortable outdoors, but the hall's air conditioning runs cold, so a light layer earns its space.

## Riding the Airport Express out

The MTR Airport Express is the simplest route. On event days, trains continue one stop past Airport to **AsiaWorld-Expo Station**, which opens almost straight onto the venue.

- **From Central:** board at Hong Kong Station, about half an hour to the venue.
- **From Kowloon:** board at Kowloon Station or Tsing Yi.
- **Cheaper, slower:** take the Tung Chung Line to Tung Chung, then a bus or taxi across to the airport island.
- **Taxi:** both red urban and blue Lantau taxis can make the trip. From Kowloon or the Island, it is a long and expensive ride.

Fares are posted at the station and on the MTR website.

## Getting back from Lantau after the encore

Leaving is the part people get wrong. Thousands of people head for one station at once. The queue for the Airport Express platform is managed by staff in snaking lines, so move steadily and keep your ticket or card ready.

- **Pay with Octopus:** tap an Octopus card or a contactless bank card at the gates rather than buying a single ticket after the show.
- **Check the last train:** the Airport Express does not run all night. Look up the last departure on the MTR site before the show, not after.
- **Leaving early:** if you have to catch that last train, leaving during the final song beats the crush.
- **Taxis:** the rank fills fast after the show, and red taxis heading back to town are most in demand.
- **Stay nearby:** hotels on the airport island and in Tung Chung avoid the trip back entirely. That makes sense if you fly out the next morning.

Buy only from the official ticketing channel the promoter links to. Hong Kong concert resale on social media is a well-known route for scams.
