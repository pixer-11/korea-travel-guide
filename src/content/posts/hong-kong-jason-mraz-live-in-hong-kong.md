He won Grammys for "Make It Mine" and for "Lucky", his duet with Colbie Caillat. He is also widely known for "I'm Yours" and "I Won't Give Up". The announcement named no support act, set length or door time.

## Out by the runways at Chek Lap Kok

AsiaWorld-Expo sits on Chek Lap Kok, the island that holds Hong Kong International Airport. It is a large exhibition and arena complex, and planes on approach and departure are a normal part of the view. It is a big indoor hall, not a club, and concert crowds usually queue for security before reaching the floor.

November falls in Hong Kong's dry, mild season. The evening is usually comfortable outdoors, but the hall's air conditioning runs cold, so a light layer earns its space.

## Riding the Airport Express out

The MTR Airport Express is the simplest route. On event days, trains continue one stop past Airport to **AsiaWorld-Expo Station**, which opens almost straight onto the venue.

- **From Central:** board at Hong Kong Station, about half an hour to the venue.
- **From Kowloon:** board at Kowloon Station or Tsing Yi.
- **Cheaper, slower:** take the Tung Chung Line to Tung Chung, then a bus or taxi across to the airport island.
- **Taxi:** both red urban and blue Lantau taxis can make the trip. From Kowloon or the Island, it is a long and expensive ride.

Fares are posted at the station and on the MTR website.

## Getting back from Lantau after the encore

Leaving is the hard part of a big show here. Thousands of people head for one station at once. Staff manage the queue for the Airport Express platform in snaking lines, and it moves steadily.

- **Pay with Octopus:** an Octopus card or a contactless bank card works at the gates, which is quicker than buying a single ticket after a show.
- **The last train:** the Airport Express does not run all night. The last departure time is on the MTR site.
- **Leaving early:** people who need that last train often leave during the final song to beat the crush.
- **Taxis:** the rank fills fast after a show, and red taxis heading back to town are most in demand.
- **Stay nearby:** hotels on the airport island and in Tung Chung avoid the trip back entirely. They suit anyone flying out the next morning.
