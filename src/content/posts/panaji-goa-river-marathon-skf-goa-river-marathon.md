## Race morning on the river

The race runs in Goa's dry season. On December 13, 2026, the SKF Goa River Marathon sends local club runners and international distance runners along a course that follows the water. December is the coolest stretch of the Goan year, with clear skies and low humidity compared with the monsoon months.

Mornings are still the only comfortable part of the day. Like most Indian road races, this one is run in the early hours, before the sun gets high over the river. The heat builds quickly after sunrise, which shapes pacing for anyone used to colder races.

## Getting to the start from Panaji

Panaji sits on the south bank of the Mandovi, and it works as a base because road links fan out from here. The organiser publishes the exact start and finish venue on its official site. Shuttle and parking arrangements, where they exist, appear there too.

For arrival, the main options are:

- **Dabolim Airport (GOI)**, south of Panaji across the Zuari
- **Manohar International Airport at Mopa**, in North Goa
- **Karmali station** on the Konkan Railway, the closest railhead to Panaji
- **Kadamba bus stand** in Panaji, for interstate coaches

Before dawn, public buses are thin on the ground. Runners usually pre-arrange a taxi the night before, or use Goa Miles, the state-backed taxi app. Hotels in Panaji, Miramar and Dona Paula fill fast in mid-December, so visitors typically secure rooms well in advance of the season.

## Bibs, bhaji-pav and the race-day routine

Indian city marathons usually hand out bibs at an expo in the days before the race. That is why out-of-state runners tend to land a day or two ahead. Collection details sit with the organiser, alongside the registration pages.

Locals run on simple carbs. The Goan breakfast standard is bhaji-pav: a spiced potato or pea curry eaten with soft pav bread. Bakeries sell poi, a wholewheat pocket bread, from early morning. Many runners eat that the day before rather than on race morning.

Plain etiquette goes a long way here:

- Cash and UPI both work at small cafés; card acceptance varies.
- Cover shoulders when walking into churches in Old Goa or Fontainhas.
- Tipping is not expected at bakeries; rounding up a taxi fare is common.

## Staying on after the finish

The best recovery walk is flat and close to the water. The Campal promenade along the Mandovi leads toward Miramar Beach, a short ride from central Panaji. Fontainhas, the old Latin Quarter, has narrow lanes of ochre, blue and yellow Portuguese-era houses with tiled roofs.

Old Goa is about 10 km upriver. Its 16th- and 17th-century churches include the Basilica of Bom Jesus, a UNESCO World Heritage Site that holds the remains of St Francis Xavier. The Our Lady of the Immaculate Conception Church, with its white zigzag staircase, stands above Panaji's main square.

The marathon falls in the run-up to Christmas. Panaji's churches and squares are usually decorated by then, and the beach towns to the north fill up for the holiday.
