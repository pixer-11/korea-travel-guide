## A tenor on the Yas Bay waterfront

This is opera in a sports-and-concert arena. Etihad Arena is an indoor, air-conditioned venue on the Yas Bay waterfront. Its long, curving facade faces the water, with a promenade of restaurants running alongside. On December 2, 2026, Andrea Bocelli brought his 30th Anniversary Romanza World Tour to that stage.

The booking sits in a deliberate slot. Yasalam Classics is part of the programme around Grand Prix week, when the island's attention swings between the racetrack and the concert halls. An evening of Italian tenor repertoire lands among a week built around engines.

## Romanza, thirty years on

The tour is named for the album that made Bocelli a global name. *Romanza*, released in 1996, gathered his early recordings and carried "Con te partirò". That song reached a far larger audience as "Time to Say Goodbye", his duet with Sarah Brightman.

A thirtieth-anniversary tour is built around that catalogue. The full programme, guest performers and orchestra line-up for the Abu Dhabi date are not part of the facts here, so this guide does not list them. What you can expect is the format Bocelli is known for:

- a large orchestral sound filling a big indoor hall
- a mix of operatic arias and his crossover songs
- an audience that tends to save its loudest response for the best-known numbers

## Reaching Etihad Arena on Yas Island

Yas Island sits on Abu Dhabi's northeastern edge, close to Zayed International Airport. That makes the arena unusually easy to reach for anyone flying in. The drive from the airport is short. From the downtown Corniche area it is a longer run along the E10 and E12 highways.

Most concertgoers come by taxi or ride-hailing app. Careem and Uber both operate in the city, alongside the cream-and-gold Abu Dhabi taxis. Drivers usually know the arena by name, and "Yas Bay" is the landmark to give if a driver hesitates. Visitors coming from Dubai usually allow for a drive of over an hour each way.

Parking serves the Yas Bay waterfront. On a Grand Prix week evening, though, the island's roads carry race traffic too. That is the main reason many attendees leave the car behind.

## Grand Prix week changes the island

Tying a concert to race week has practical effects. Hotel rooms on Yas Island go quickly during the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix. The W Abu Dhabi, which straddles the Yas Marina Circuit, is the most famous example. Visitors who find the island full usually stay on Saadiyat Island or in central Abu Dhabi and come in by car.

The upside is atmosphere. The whole island is in event mode that week, with fan zones, after-race concerts and crowds that fill the waterfront late into the evening. A Bocelli ticket can sit alongside a race-day pass in a single trip.

## Concert-night manners at Etihad Arena

The crowd at a Bocelli show behaves more like a concert-hall audience than a pop crowd. People generally hold their applause until a piece ends rather than clapping over quiet passages. Phones go up for the famous songs. During the slower arias, most of the hall keeps its screens down.

Dress tends toward smart evening wear. Shoulders and knees are usually covered, in line with the UAE's general expectation of modest dress in public spaces. December evenings on the coast are mild, but arena air-conditioning runs cool. That is why so many people carry a light layer or shawl.

- **Paying:** cards and contactless payment are standard at the arena and on Yas Bay. Cash is rarely needed.
- **Getting home:** ride-hailing queues build as the hall empties. Some people walk the promenade for a while and order a car once the first wave has gone.
- **Drinks:** alcohol is served only in licensed areas. Public drunkenness is an offence in the UAE, not just a faux pas.

## Before the curtain and after the encore

Yas Bay's waterfront is where most concertgoers eat. Its restaurants and terraces sit within a short walk of the arena doors, and pre-show dinner there is the usual routine. Because it is a single strip, a late table after the encore is easy too.

Visitors on a longer stay can fill the days without leaving the island. Nearby attractions include:

- Ferrari World Abu Dhabi, with its red roof and Formula Rossa coaster
- Yas Waterworld and Warner Bros. World Abu Dhabi
- Yas Mall, for an air-conditioned afternoon
- Yas Marina Circuit itself, the reason the week exists

Further afield, Louvre Abu Dhabi on Saadiyat Island and the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque are both a straightforward drive. Together they make a strong daytime pairing with an evening of Bocelli on the bay.
