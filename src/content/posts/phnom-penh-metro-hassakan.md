Sisowath Quay is Phnom Penh's long riverfront promenade. Traffic runs along one side and the Tonle Sap moves slowly on the other. Metro Hassakan is on this stretch, and it works as two places under one name. In daylight it's a restaurant built around Asian fusion tapas. After dark it becomes a cocktail bar for people who dressed up a little.

## One room, two moods

**The format shapes your visit.** Daytime and evening at Metro Hassakan suit different plans, so pick your hour before you pick your outfit.

**Riverside is easy to reach.** It's also one of the most walkable parts of the city. Metro Hassakan is on Sisowath Quay, the road that follows the Tonle Sap through the historic centre.

- **Tuk-tuk or rickshaw by app:** PassApp and Grab both work in Phnom Penh. Using an app fixes the fare before you set off, which saves haggling.

How a night here usually works:

- **Currency:** Cambodia runs on two currencies. US dollars are used everywhere, and small change often comes back in Cambodian riel. Keep a few riel notes for tuk-tuk drivers afterwards.
- **Cards:** Card payment is common at upscale venues like this, but ask before you assume, especially for small bills.
- **Tipping:** Tips aren't required in Cambodia, but people appreciate them. Leaving the riel change or rounding up is normal.
- **Dress:** Smart casual fits the crowd. Swap beach-town flip-flops and singlets for something you'd wear to dinner.
