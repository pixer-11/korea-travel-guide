## Getting to KSPO Dome

KSPO Dome sits inside the Olympic Park complex in Songpa-gu, on the east side of the city — the same sprawling green space that hosted the 1988 Summer Olympics. The simplest route is the subway: take Line 5 or Line 9 to Olympic Park Station and follow the signs through the park toward the dome, a walk of roughly ten to fifteen minutes past fountains, sculpture lawns, and the old Olympic stadium shell.

On concert nights, that walkway tends to turn into a slow-moving river of fans in merch and light sticks. Taxis can drop you closer to the dome's entrance gates, but road access gets congested fast once doors approach, so the subway is genuinely the more reliable option here.
