## Getting through the gates the Lima way

- **Bring ID.** Peruvians carry their DNI and visitors their passport or a copy, since ID checks are common at entry.
- **Pay by card** for drinks and official merch inside. Street vendors outside the gates selling snacks, bootleg shirts and rain ponchos usually want cash in soles.
- **Speak a little Spanish.** *Entrada* is ticket, *puerta* is gate, *salida* is exit. Staff at the gates may not speak English.

When the encore ends, many attendees walk away from the venue toward a quieter street before calling a car. Surge pricing and gridlock right at the gate push people to do this, and it usually gets them home faster than waiting at the curb.
