## A Ramsay address in BKK1

This is a launch, so treat it as one. Gordon Ramsay Burger is set to open in October 2026 in JS Tower, in Phnom Penh's Boeung Keng Kang 1 district. It is the chef's first project in Cambodia. Locals and expats were already talking about it well before opening.

The burger concept has a history with Planet Hollywood. Its first branch opened in 2012 inside Planet Hollywood Resort & Casino in Las Vegas. The pairing is happening again here: JS Tower is also home to Southeast Asia's first Planet Hollywood Hotel. A celebrity-chef restaurant and a celebrity-themed hotel under one roof is new for the city.

Nothing about the Phnom Penh menu, prices or hours has been published in what we can verify. Don't assume a dish from another branch will be served here. Check the restaurant's official pages once it opens.

## Getting to JS Tower

Phnom Penh has no metro, so you will be getting there by road. Most visitors use ride-hailing apps such as Grab or PassApp. Both show the fare before you book, and they can send a tuk-tuk, a small auto-rickshaw or a car.

BKK1 lies south of the Independence Monument, between Norodom Boulevard and Monivong Boulevard. Tuk-tuk drivers know the district by its initials: say "BK-K-One". A new tower can be hard for drivers to place in its first weeks, so drop a pin in the app rather than relying on the building name.

From the riverside area around Sisowath Quay, the ride usually takes 10 to 20 minutes. Evening traffic on Norodom adds to that.

## Booking a table in the first weeks

With this much attention before opening, expect demand to outrun seats at first. Here is how to plan for it:

- **Book ahead if the restaurant takes reservations.** Look on its official site or social pages instead of turning up on spec in the launch month.
- **Have dollars and riel ready.** Cambodia runs on both currencies. Change is often handed back as a mix, with riel replacing small US coins.
- **Cards and QR payments are common** at international venues in the city, including KHQR bank apps like ABA. Still, ask before you order.
- **Read the bill.** Tipping isn't required in Cambodia. Hotel-linked venues sometimes add a service charge, and if it's on the bill, an extra tip is optional.
- **Dress smart-casual.** It's a hotel tower, not a street stall. Sandals and shorts are usually fine, but beachwear isn't.

If you're aiming for a weekend evening, a weekday lunch in the opening weeks usually gives you more options.

## Making a BKK1 evening of it

BKK1 makes it easy to fill the rest of the evening. The Independence Monument is a short ride north, and the lotus-shaped tower is lit after dark. Next to it, Hun Sen Park and the Sihanouk Boulevard promenade fill with evening exercise groups and families.

Street 278, near Wat Langka, has some of the neighbourhood's best-known cafés and bars. Wat Langka is one of the city's oldest pagodas. In the daytime the Royal Palace and the National Museum are about 10 minutes away by tuk-tuk. Both close in the evening, so see them first and eat afterwards.

Plan on an hour to 90 minutes at the table. Then walk off the burger around the monument.
