## Leisure Valley Ground, Sector 29

The venue is an open-air ground in central Gurugram, next to Leisure Valley Park. You stand on grass and compacted earth, with no roof and no tiered seating. Sector 29, one of the city's main clusters of bars and restaurants, sits right beside it. That makes it the obvious place to eat before the show.

Getting there:
- **Metro:** Yellow Line to Millennium City Centre Gurugram or IFFCO Chowk, then a short auto-rickshaw or cab ride.
- **Cab:** Uber and Ola both cover the area. Set your pickup a few streets from the gate, not at it.
- **From Delhi:** NH-48 is the usual road route. Leave plenty of time, because evening traffic at IFFCO Chowk is notoriously slow.

## December in Gurugram

Early-December evenings in Delhi NCR are cool and dry. They are often hazy too, because air quality across the region is typically poor at this time of year. Here is what helps:

- A light jacket or hoodie for after dark
- A reusable N95 mask for the walk in and out
- Comfortable closed shoes for a long stand on uneven ground
- A charged phone, since you will need it for the e-ticket and the ride home

Check the event's prohibited-items list before you leave. Concerts in India commonly bar outside food, bottles and large bags at security.
