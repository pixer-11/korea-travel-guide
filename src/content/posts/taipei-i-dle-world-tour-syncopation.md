## A first for the dome

On March 7, 2026, a girl group headlines Taipei Dome. That makes i-dle, formerly styled (G)I-DLE, the first K-pop girl group to top the bill at Taiwan's largest indoor venue. The show is the Taipei stop of the Syncopation World Tour.

The Taiwan connection runs deeper than the venue. Member Shuhua was born in Taiwan, so this date is a homecoming for her. That fact shapes the crowd's mood and the signs fans hold up.

Taipei Dome opened at the end of 2023. It has a fixed roof and seats roughly 40,000, so the weather can't stop the show. Its steel shell sits on Zhongxiao East Road, a block from Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall.

## Reaching the Xinyi District stadium

Most attendees take the MRT. The Bannan (Blue) Line's Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall Station is the closest stop, and its Exit 5 opens toward the dome complex. Taipei City Hall Station, one stop east, is a 10-minute walk away and handy when the closer platform is packed.

- **EasyCard (悠遊卡):** a tap-on card for the MRT and buses, also accepted at 7-Eleven and FamilyMart.
- **Taxis:** easy to hail before the show. After a 40,000-seat event lets out, though, Zhongxiao East Road backs up.
- **YouBike:** docks cluster around Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall, useful for escaping the post-show crush.

## How the Taipei Dome crowd moves

Taiwanese concert crowds are orderly, and this one follows a set pattern. Fans usually gather on the plaza outside the dome well before doors. They trade photocards and hand out freebies such as stickers and printed fan cards. Bring a few of your own if you want to join in.

- **Lightsticks:** the official i-dle lightstick is the standard. Fans usually pair it to the venue system on site, so it flashes in sync with the stage.
- **Fan chants:** the fandom, Neverland, learns the Korean chants. Taiwanese fans usually add Mandarin cheers for Shuhua.
- **Food:** the convenience stores near the station exits fill up with fans buying onigiri and drinks before the show.
- **Leaving:** many attendees walk to Taipei City Hall Station or toward Xinyi's malls rather than queue at the nearest gate.

Bags and outside items are subject to venue rules. The dome lists its entry policy on its own website.

## Tickets, dates and before or after

The tour organiser handles dates, ticketing and seat maps, and its official tour channels list the Taipei date. The group's label, Cube Entertainment, runs i-dle's official accounts, where tour posters list every city. The facts here name no ticket price and no showtime, so none is given.

The neighbourhood fills a day around the show:

- **Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall:** next door. Its changing of the guard and wide lawn draw fans in tour merch.
- **Songshan Cultural and Creative Park:** a former tobacco factory a short walk north, with design shops and cafés.
- **Taipei 101 and Xinyi's malls:** a direct walk south-east, with late dining after the encore.
- **Raohe Street Night Market:** a short taxi ride north-east, for pepper buns and grilled skewers.

Out-of-town fans usually stay in Xinyi or around Taipei Main Station. Both are on the Bannan Line, so there's no transfer back to the dome.
