## A Manuka shop that predates the lattes

Paperchain has been selling books on Franklin Street since 1982. That's long enough to have watched Manuka turn into one of Canberra's main café and dining strips. The address says Griffith, but you're in Manuka village, the compact grid of shops a short walk from Manuka Oval.

The stock is mostly new titles, not secondhand. The shop also hosts author events, which is a big part of why Canberra readers know the name. It's an easy place to fill 30–45 minutes, longer if you're the type who reads the first chapter before buying.

## Getting to Franklin Street

Manuka sits in the inner south, across Lake Burley Griffin from Civic. Canberra's light rail doesn't come down here, so your options are a bus, a car or a bike.

- **Bus:** Transport Canberra routes stop around Manuka. Check the current timetable on their site or app, because route numbers change.
- **Car:** there are public car parks around the village. Read the signs for paid times and limits.
- **On foot:** from Kingston's foreshore or the Parliamentary Triangle, it's a pleasant walk through leafy inner-south streets.

## Browsing without the crowd

It stays small and under the radar, but weekend afternoons still get busy. On weekdays the quietest stretch is usually 12pm–3pm, which suits a lunch-break browse. Try not to arrive between 2pm and 3pm on weekends. That's typically the peak, when the aisles fill with people killing time before or after a Manuka lunch.

The calmest weekend window is 4pm–6pm. That works fully only on Saturday, when the shop currently stays open until 7pm. On Sunday it closes at 5pm, so a 4pm Sunday visit gives you just an hour. Saturday evening is the better choice.

Opening hours can shift around public holidays. Check the shop's own listing before you make a special trip.

## Buying books the Canberra way

An independent shop like this works differently from a chain. Use that:

- **Ask the staff.** Recommendations from the person behind the counter are the main reason to come. Tell them the last book you loved rather than a genre.
- **Order what isn't on the shelf.** Independent bookshops will usually order titles in for you, which helps if you're staying in Canberra a few days.
- **Book author events early.** These are the shop's big drawcard. Check Paperchain's website or ask in store for what's coming up and how to reserve a place.
- **Pay by card.** Tap-and-go is normal across Australia, and nobody tips in a shop.

Book prices are printed on the covers or stickers, so there are no surprises at the till.

## Making an afternoon of Manuka

Paperchain fits easily into a wider inner-south loop. Bring your new book to one of the cafés along Franklin Street or Bougainville Street and start reading.

Nearby, Manuka Oval hosts cricket and AFL, so check the fixtures if you're in town on a match day: the village gets much busier then. Manuka Pool, the open-air Art Deco pool from the 1930s, is a few minutes' walk away in summer. Kingston and its foreshore are a short drive or a longer stroll east.
