## Why this show matters

ITZY landing at Taipei Arena is a big deal for Taiwanese K-pop fans. The five-member group — one of JYP Entertainment's flagship acts — built this tour around "Tunnel Vision," and Taipei is confirmed as the second Taiwan stop.

That "second stop" detail matters. It suggests strong regional demand, which means tickets for this date could move fast once sales open.

## Getting to Taipei Arena

Taipei Arena sits right above Taipei Metro's Zhongxiao Xinsheng area, easily reached via the Nangang-facing lines that converge downtown. Locals usually take the MRT rather than drive, since parking near the arena on a concert night is scarce and expensive.

- Nearest station: Taipei Arena Station (Wenhu Line and Bannan Line interchange)
- Walk time from station exit to arena: a few minutes, well signed
- Alternative: taxi or rideshare drop-off on side streets, not directly out front

Give yourself extra buffer time. Concert nights at this venue routinely see crowd bottlenecks at the main exits as tens of thousands of fans converge at once.

## What to expect on show day

Taipei Arena is the city's main indoor arena for major international touring acts, seating in the range of 10,000-plus depending on stage configuration. Expect the full stadium-tour treatment: a merchandise line that starts hours before doors, light-stick sales near the entrance, and a plaza outside that fills with fans trading photocards and posing for pre-show selfies.

Merch queues for major K-pop tours are notorious for forming well before any announced doors time. If a specific piece of merchandise matters to you, plan to arrive early and expect that line to be its own commitment.

Inside, expect the standard tour production: choreography-heavy sets, a mix of stage b-side moments and title tracks, and heavy use of light-stick synced visuals — but treat any specific setlist details as unconfirmed until closer to the date.

## Tickets and dates — confirm before booking

The date given is September 5, 2026, but tour schedules can shift, add extra dates, or adjust venue details as the on-sale period approaches. Before you commit to flights or hotel bookings:

- Check ITZY's official social channels and JYP Entertainment's tour announcements for confirmed on-sale dates
- Verify ticket tiers, seating chart, and pricing directly through the official ticketing partner for Taipei Arena shows
- Confirm venue doors and set times only once they're posted — these are not yet part of the public announcement

Do not rely on secondary resale sites for your first purchase attempt. Official on-sales are the safest route, and resale markup for a high-demand K-pop stop like this can be steep.

## How to visit like a local

Taiwanese fans typically queue by numbered wristband or app-based entry system rather than a first-come free-for-all, so check whether this tour uses a randomized entry queue before you show up expecting to just line up early.

Payment for merch and concessions is overwhelmingly card or mobile pay (Line Pay, EasyCard-linked apps) — cash still works but isn't necessary to carry in large amounts.

- Buy tickets the moment official on-sale opens; resale prices spike within hours for major K-pop dates
- Wear or bring your group's official light stick (a "Ridgy" for ITZY) — it's part of the in-arena visual experience, not just a light show, and rentals are limited
- Eat before you arrive — food options directly inside the arena are limited and lines are long
- The biggest local mistake tourists make is underestimating MRT crowding after the show; if you're catching a late flight or high-speed rail, build in an extra hour post-encore

## Nearby, before or after

Taipei Arena sits close to the Dunhua/Zhongxiao shopping corridor, so a pre-show dinner or post-show night market run is easy to fold in. Yongkang Street and the Da'an district's café scene are both a short ride away if you want to make a full day of it rather than just the concert.
