## A girl group in a 1930s picture palace

The Singapore stop is unusually intimate. izna, the K-pop girl group formed through Mnet's survival show *I-LAND 2* and launched in late 2024, brings its WHO DAT GIRL? concert to Capitol Theatre on November 8, 2026. The show starts at 7PM.

Capitol Theatre opened as a cinema around 1930 and reopened in 2015 after a long restoration. Inside, steep tiers of seating face a proscenium stage. That means the back rows sit far closer to the performers than they would at an arena, and a whole room of lightsticks fills your view of the auditorium.

## Getting to Stamford Road

The theatre sits within the Capitol Singapore complex, at the corner of Stamford Road and North Bridge Road. Your options:

- **City Hall MRT** (North-South and East-West Lines): an underground link runs towards Capitol Piazza, so you stay out of the rain.
- **Bras Basah MRT** (Circle Line): a few minutes' walk along Stamford Road.
- **Taxi or Grab**: drop-offs usually go to the North Bridge Road side, which backs up after the show.

The Civic District is compact. National Gallery Singapore, St Andrew's Cathedral and Raffles City are all within a short walk, so getting there on foot is easy.

## Before the lights go down

Fans usually come in time for dinner nearby. Raffles City's basement food hall and the restaurants in the CHIJMES courtyard are the usual picks, and Capitol Piazza has its own food outlets downstairs. Funan, across North Bridge Road, adds more casual options.

The 7PM start leaves plenty of time after the encore for MRT trains home from City Hall. The station is also an interchange, which spreads the crowd across two lines.
