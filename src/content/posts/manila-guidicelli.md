## What Guidicelli actually is

This is not a celebrity restaurant coasting on a famous surname. Matteo Guidicelli, the actor, opened it with his father Gianluca, an Italian, and the food does the talking. It's their most ambitious project together so far, aimed squarely at diners who want a proper Italian tasting menu, not a casual trattoria.

The father-son pairing matters here. Gianluca brings the technique and the Italian pedigree; Matteo brings the platform that got the place noticed fast. Together the menu reads more like a European fine-dining list than a Filipino take on Italian comfort food.

## The dishes that define the menu

Skip the idea of red-sauce pasta and garlic bread. The standout plates push classic Italian ingredients into unexpected pairings.

- Escargot with guanciale: snails paired with cured pork cheek, a rustic-meets-refined combination
- Blue shrimp carpaccio: raw, delicate, meant to be eaten early in the meal
- Bistecca alla Fiorentina, made with Wagyu instead of the traditional Chianina beef
- Optional upgrades on the steak: caviar or shaved truffle for those who want to push the bill further

The Fiorentina is the anchor dish. Traditionally a thick, bone-in T-bone cut grilled rare and sliced tableside, this version swaps in Wagyu for extra marbling and richness, a deliberate break from the purist Tuscan version.

## Who this meal is for

Order the escargot or the carpaccio first if you're sharing, since both are built as starters meant to wake up the palate before the steak arrives. The Fiorentina is a cut for the table, not a solo plate; Florentine tradition serves it for two or more, and the portion here likely follows suit.

If you're the kind of diner who upgrades everything, the truffle and caviar add-ons exist for exactly that impulse. Go in expecting a bill that reflects the ingredients on the table, not a neighborhood pasta joint's prices.

This isn't a walk-in, order-fast kind of place. Treat it like you would any serious tasting-style Italian restaurant: book your table, expect courses to be paced, and budget more time than a quick lunch.

## Booking and getting there without a wasted trip

Because Guidicelli is newly opened and trending, table availability and hours are the two things most likely to shift week to week. Confirm both directly with the restaurant before you leave your hotel.

- Reserve ahead, especially for dinner and weekends; new, well-publicized openings in Manila fill fast once word spreads
- Call or check official channels for current opening hours rather than assuming a standard dinner service window
- Ask about the Fiorentina's serving size and price when booking, since Wagyu pricing and caviar/truffle add-ons can shift the total bill significantly
- If escargot or carpaccio are seasonal or limited, confirm availability when you reserve so you're not disappointed table-side

Given the celebrity attachment, expect interest from both food press and fans; arriving with a confirmed reservation avoids any door confusion on a busy night.
