## Three nights at Galaxy Arena

The run lasts three nights, October 16, 17 and 18, 2026. ENHYPEN, the seven-member BELIFT LAB group formed through the I-LAND survival show, bring their World Tour BLOOD SAGA to Galaxy Arena. The tour name continues the vampire storyline running through their albums, so the staging leans toward that theme.

Galaxy Arena opened in 2023 as the largest indoor venue in Macau. It is a seated, bowl-shaped arena of roughly 16,000 capacity, enclosed inside the Galaxy Macau complex. You enter through the resort itself, past shopping arcades and casino floors, and not from a standalone street entrance.

## Reaching Cotai

Galaxy Macau sits on the Cotai Strip, the reclaimed land between Taipa and Coloane. The Venetian, Londoner and City of Dreams stand across the road. Most visitors come in by one of these routes:

- **From Hong Kong:** TurboJET or Cotai Water Jet ferries to Taipa Ferry Terminal, or the bus across the Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge.
- **From Zhuhai:** on foot through the Gongbei/Border Gate crossing, or via Hengqin Port, which is a short ride from Cotai.
- **Inside Macau:** the free Galaxy shuttle buses connect the resort with the ferry terminals, Border Gate and the bridge port. The Macau LRT Taipa line also serves Cotai.

Taxis queue at the resort's taxi stand. After the encore that line grows long, which is why many attendees walk to a neighbouring resort's taxi rank or take the shuttle out.

## Merch, lightsticks and the crowd after the show

ENHYPEN fans, known as ENGENE, treat the hours before doors as part of the night. Here is how a concert day at a Macau arena usually runs:

- **Merch:** official tour merchandise sells at booths near the arena. On a three-night run, people often shop on a non-show day or before their own night.
- **Lightsticks:** the official ENHYPEN lightstick usually pairs with the venue's seat-sync system, so the arena lights up in coordinated colours. Fans register the lightstick to their seat number via the app.
- **Freebies and photo cards:** small trade circles form in the resort corridors. Exchanges stay informal, and hallway etiquette means keeping walkways clear for resort guests.
- **Payment:** the Macau pataca is the local currency, but Hong Kong dollars are accepted almost everywhere in Cotai. Cards and mobile wallets work at resort outlets.

Galaxy Macau's casino floors are off-limits to under-21s, and security there enforces it. Fans heading to the arena follow the signed concourse routes around the gaming areas.

## Where tickets and stays come from

Galaxy Arena events are ticketed through Galaxy Macau's own ticketing platform, and fan-club presale access for ENGENE members normally comes before general sale. Prices vary by seat zone and are listed on the ticketing page, not here.

Hotels in Cotai fill fast on concert weekends. Visitors usually stay at Galaxy Macau's own hotels (Banyan Tree, Andaz, Raffles, Galaxy Hotel and others) or in Taipa Village, a ten-minute ride away. Budget travellers often stay on the Macau peninsula or across the border in Zhuhai. Taipa Village's Rua do Cunha is where many fans go for pork chop buns and egg tarts before the show.

Most international visitors spend two nights: one for the concert, one for Senado Square, the Ruins of St. Paul's and the peninsula's old streets.
