## A Punjabi countdown on Yas Island

Diljit Dosanjh plays Etihad Live, Yas Island, on December 31, 2026, so his set carries the crowd into the new year. He is the Punjabi singer and actor who played Coachella in 2023, the first Punjabi artist on that bill. His Dil-Luminati Tour then filled stadiums from North America to India.

The UAE's large Punjabi and wider South Asian community makes Abu Dhabi a natural home crowd for him. Expect turbans, phulkari dupattas and Indian flags in the audience, and expect thousands of people singing every Punjabi lyric back at him. Older bhangra-pop hits like "Proper Patola" and newer tracks like "G.O.A.T." and "Born to Shine" are the songs fans know best. The setlist itself belongs to the artist.

## Getting to Etihad Live

Yas Island is about 25–30 minutes by car from central Abu Dhabi. It is roughly an hour or more from Dubai via the E11 or E311. Abu Dhabi's international airport is a short drive away, so fans flying in for the night can land the same day.

- **Taxi and ride-hail:** Abu Dhabi's metered taxis, Uber and Careem all serve Yas Island. This is how most attendees arrive.
- **Driving:** Yas has large car parks, but New Year's Eve traffic builds across the island's bridges in the evening.
- **Staying on the island:** the hotel cluster around Yas Marina Circuit and Yas Mall puts you a short ride from the venue.

The same district also holds Ferrari World, Warner Bros. World and Yas Waterworld. Many visitors make a full day of it before the show.

## Tickets and the venue

Etihad Live is a dedicated live-entertainment venue on Yas Island. It is separate from Etihad Arena on Yas Bay, which is a common mix-up when people book taxis. Tickets come from the official channels of the venue and the concert promoter. Seating tiers, door times and prices are listed on those channels, not here.

Resale listings outside the official chain carry the usual risk of duplicate or invalid barcodes. That risk is higher on a holiday night that sells this strongly. Tickets are typically scanned on a phone at the gate, so attendees keep the ticket app or email ready.

## How the night works in Abu Dhabi

New Year's Eve is the busiest night of the year on Yas Island. Fans typically arrive an hour or more before doors so they clear security and reach their section before the opening act. Large bags, outside food and professional cameras are usually turned away at UAE concert gates.

- **Dress:** smart casual is the norm. Many fans wear traditional Punjabi outfits, and the venue's air conditioning makes a light layer useful.
- **Alcohol:** the legal drinking age in Abu Dhabi is 21. Licensed bars inside venues ask for ID, and public drunkenness is an offence.
- **Leaving:** ride-hail surge pricing and long taxi queues follow the midnight finish. Attendees often walk to a nearby hotel pickup point or wait out the first rush.
- **Payment:** cards and Apple Pay or Google Pay work almost everywhere on Yas.

Bhangra in the aisles is part of the event. Filming a whole song with your phone held high blocks the view of the people behind you, though.
