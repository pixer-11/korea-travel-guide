## One Night on Flatbush Avenue

The New York stop of tripleS's 2026 ANDLESS world tour takes place on November 15, 2026. Instead of a Manhattan arena, the K-pop group plays Kings Theatre in Flatbush, Brooklyn. That makes this a seated, single-room show.

tripleS is the large girl group from the Seoul agency Modhaus. It is known for letting fans vote on units and releases through its COSMO app. Fans who collect the group's digital "objekts" usually treat a tour stop as the in-person payoff of months of voting.

## Inside Kings Theatre

Kings Theatre opened in 1929 as one of Loew's "Wonder Theatres". It sat closed for decades and reopened in 2015 after a full restoration. Before you even find your seat, you pass through:

- a gilded French-Baroque lobby with tall mirrors and chandeliers
- grand staircases climbing to the mezzanine
- a vaulted, ornate auditorium of roughly 3,000 seats across orchestra and balcony levels

The room is steeply raked and compact for its capacity. Even upper-level seats face the stage head-on, so formations and choreography read clearly from most of the house. The orchestra floor is close enough for fan banners to be seen from the stage.

## Lightsticks, Chants and the Merch Line

A K-pop show in a historic theatre runs on its own customs. These are the ones that shape the night here:

- **Lightsticks:** most fans carry the official tripleS lightstick, and the sea of lights from the balconies is part of the show.
- **Fan chants:** WAV, the fandom, shouts member names and set calls during songs. Fans usually learn the chants from the group's official chant guides.
- **Merch:** the merchandise queue on the sidewalk and in the lobby tends to start forming hours before the show. Many attendees buy their goods before the performance and not after it.
- **Freebies:** fan-made slogans, photocards and small gifts are often handed out by fans near the entrance on concert days.
- **Seats:** Kings Theatre is fully seated, so there is no general-admission pit to camp for. Your ticket decides where you watch from.

## Getting to the Theatre

Kings Theatre stands at 1027 Flatbush Avenue, between Beverley Road and Tilden Avenue. The subway is the simplest way in from Manhattan:

- **2 or 5 train** to Beverley Road: the closest stop, about a block away
- **2 or 5 train** to Church Avenue: a short walk north of the theatre
- **B or Q train** to Church Avenue: about ten minutes' walk east along Church Avenue to Flatbush

The 2 train runs from Times Square to Flatbush in roughly 40 minutes. Street parking around Flatbush Avenue is limited on show nights, so most attendees ride the subway. Late trains back to Manhattan are crowded right after the encore. Some fans wait in the area for the first rush to pass.

Flatbush Avenue near the theatre is lined with Caribbean bakeries, roti shops and casual takeout counters. Concertgoers usually eat there before the show instead of in Manhattan. Prospect Park and the Brooklyn Botanic Garden lie a short ride north on the same 2/5 line, which suits anyone making a full day of it in Brooklyn.
