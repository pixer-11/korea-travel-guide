## November in Vancouver, after the show

November is the start of Vancouver's wettest stretch. The sun sets around 4:30pm, so the night is fully dark long before any evening show begins. Locals wear a waterproof shell rather than carry an umbrella, because wind off the water turns umbrellas inside out on exposed blocks.

Most of Metro Vancouver's Korean restaurants and noraebang rooms sit outside downtown. The main areas are:

- **North Road, Coquitlam/Burnaby border:** the region's main Koreatown strip, near Lougheed Town Centre Station on the Millennium and Expo SkyTrain lines
