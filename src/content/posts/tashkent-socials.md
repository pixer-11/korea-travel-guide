## Getting a table without the wait

Socials draws both a local and a visiting crowd, so timing your visit matters more here than at a quiet neighbourhood cafe.

- Weekday lunch (before the after-work rush) is your best shot at a table without a wait.
- Weekend afternoons, when the dessert crowd peaks, are the busiest stretch; expect a queue or a wait for seating.
- Call ahead or check if Socials takes reservations, especially for groups, since demand for photogenic tables can outstrip walk-in seating.
- Confirm current opening hours before heading over; all-day cafes in Tashkent sometimes trim hours on quieter weekdays.

## Ordering and settling the bill

Treat Socials like you would any modern Tashkent cafe: card payment is standard at newer venues like this, though carrying some cash (Uzbekistani som) is a sensible backup. Tipping isn't obligatory but rounding up or leaving a small tip for table service is appreciated and increasingly normal at this kind of spot.

Don't rush the dessert order to the end of the meal out of habit; regulars often order it alongside mains so it arrives while the table is still full and the plates are still being photographed. If you're travelling solo, sitting at a shared or counter-style seat (if the layout offers one) gets you in faster than waiting for a full table to free up.
