## The city laid out in one sweep

The whole region shows up at once. From the pullout on Cypress Bowl Road you look south and east over West Vancouver's hillside houses to the water. Beyond that are the downtown towers, the green block of Stanley Park and the Lions Gate Bridge spanning First Narrows.

On a clear day the view reaches much further. Mount Baker's white cone rises across the border in Washington State. To the west, the Strait of Georgia opens toward the Gulf Islands and Vancouver Island. This is a place you look out from, not a hike: you park, walk a few steps to the edge, and the view is right there.

## Up the switchbacks from Highway 1

You'll need a car or a tour. Take Highway 1 (the Upper Levels Highway) to exit 8, then follow Cypress Bowl Road as it zigzags up the mountain. The lookout is a signed pullout partway up, well before the ski area at the top. From downtown, plan on roughly half an hour of driving when traffic is normal.

No city bus goes up this road. Keep these in mind:

- **Winter driving:** this is a mountain road, and BC's winter tire rules apply in the colder months. Read the signs at the bottom of the road.
- **Direction:** the pullout is easiest to reach on the way up, because you don't have to cross traffic.
- **Combine it:** keep going to Cypress Provincial Park for trails, snowshoeing or skiing, depending on the season.

## Reading the sky before you go

The weather decides your visit. Vancouver's cloud often sits low, and the lookout can be socked in while the city below is clear, or the reverse. Check a webcam or the forecast before you drive up. A grey day can leave you looking at nothing but white.

Sunset is the popular time, and it's easy to see why. The light falls across the inlet and the towers start lighting up. After dark, the grid of streets and the bridge lights make a second, completely different view. Because the lookout is so well known, clear evenings bring everyone out at once, so arriving well before sunset is how you get a parking spot.

## Pullout manners on Cypress Bowl Road

This is a working road, not a park plaza. People treat the lookout like a quick scenic stop: they park, look, take photos and leave so the next car can get in. Follow the same rhythm.

- **Park only in marked spaces.** Don't stop on the road shoulder or block the lanes going up the mountain.
- **Leave nothing visible in the car.** Trailhead and viewpoint lots around Vancouver are known for break-ins.
- **Bring a layer.** It's colder up here than in the city, and the wind can be sharp after dark.
- **Tripods:** set up along the edge without blocking the people behind you, and give others a turn at the best spot.
- **Pack out everything.** There's nowhere convenient to throw rubbish on the mountain.

If the lot is full, don't circle in the traffic lanes. Drive further up toward the park and stop on the way back down, when spaces have often opened up.
