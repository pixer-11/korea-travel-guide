## Why an SM Supermalls debut matters here

Cebu's SM malls function less like shopping centers and more like the city's default social venue: air-conditioned, central, and where most new food concepts choose to test the market first. A brand launching inside SM here is effectively announcing itself to the whole city at once.

For a Manila import, that's also the fastest way to reach both mall regulars and the newer wave of Cebuano diners chasing whatever's trending on social feeds. If a churro stand can generate a queue in an SM atrium, it's doing something right.

## The churro, and why it's the thing to order

Churros are the reason Twist & Buckle has a Michelin Guide track record at all, so treat them as the non-negotiable order rather than an afterthought. The brand's whole identity is built around getting the fry and the dip right, not around a long menu.

Comfort-food sides exist to round out the visit, but they're supporting acts. If you're only ordering once, order the churros first and decide from there whether you have room for anything else.

## Finding the counter inside SM

SM Supermalls in Cebu run large, and a newly opened stall can sit in a different wing than you'd expect, sometimes in a food hall, sometimes as its own storefront. Because this is a fresh opening, exact hours, the specific SM branch, and whether they're taking reservations are all details worth confirming the same day you plan to go, either through the mall directory or Twist & Buckle's own social channels.

Don't assume mall-wide hours apply to the stall itself. New tenants sometimes run shorter hours in their first weeks while they staff up.
