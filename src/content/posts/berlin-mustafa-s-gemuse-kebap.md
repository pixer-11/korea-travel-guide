## A kiosk with a queue

Look for the line before you look for the shop. Mustafa's Gemüse Kebap is a small kiosk on the pavement of Mehringdamm, one of Kreuzberg's main roads. There's no dining room to walk into. You order at a hatch, take the kebab and eat standing up or walk off with it.

It is among the most-reviewed kebab stands in Berlin, and reviewers have rated it well over a very large number of visits. That popularity shapes the whole visit. Don't come expecting a quiet discovery. Expect a well-organised queue of travellers, students and Kreuzbergers, all waiting to eat the same thing.

## What goes in the bread

The name tells you the point of the place. *Gemüse* means vegetables, and the summary sums it up as hearty chicken kebabs with grilled veggies. The grilled vegetables are what set it apart from the usual döner stuffing of raw salad and sauce.

The kebab is chicken in toasted flatbread with the hot vegetables piled in. The fillings that made it famous are well documented, though you should check the board at the hatch for today's options:

- Chicken off the spit, sliced to order
- Grilled vegetables, the house signature
- Crumbled cheese and a squeeze of lemon
- A choice of sauces, usually including a spicy one

It's a budget-friendly meal and a filling one. One kebab is plenty for lunch. If you're travelling with someone vegetarian, ask at the hatch whether there's a version without meat before you reach the front.

## Working the Mehringdamm line

Measured foot traffic makes timing easy. **On weekdays the kiosk is at its quietest from 11am to 11pm**, so a Monday to Thursday visit at almost any hour beats the weekend. **On weekends, come between 11am and 1pm.** Try not to turn up on a Saturday or Sunday between 1pm and 11pm; that's when the line stretches furthest down the pavement.

How the queue works:

- Join the back of the single line. People notice and object to anyone who drifts toward the hatch from the side.
- Decide on your order and sauce before you reach the front. The staff work fast and the exchange is short.
- Keep cash on you. Street kiosks in Berlin have long been cash-first, so don't rely on card payment.
- A few basic German phrases help: *einmal Hähnchen* (one chicken) and *scharf* (spicy).

Have napkins ready and don't wear your best shirt. With vegetables, sauce and cheese inside, the kebab drips. Plenty of people lean against the railings by the station entrance to eat.

## Hours that run late

The schedule runs late into the night, and the kiosk is built for after-dark appetites. Monday to Wednesday it opens from 11am to 11:30pm. Thursday and Sunday it keeps going until 12:30am, both opening at 11am.

Friday is the odd one out. The kiosk opens at **2pm** rather than 11am, so you can't make it a Friday lunch stop, but it stays open until 2:30am. Saturday runs the longest, from 11am through to 2:30am. That makes it an easy late stop after a night out in Kreuzberg.

## Getting to Mehringdamm 33

Take the U-Bahn. **Mehringdamm station** is served by the U6 and U7 lines, and the kiosk at Mehringdamm 33 is a short walk from the station exits on Mehringdamm itself. From Friedrichstraße or Hallesches Tor on the U6, you're only a few stops away.

From Hermannplatz or Neukölln, the U7 runs straight there. Buses also run along Mehringdamm, but the U-Bahn is easier to navigate if you don't know the city. Plan on 30 to 60 minutes in total, depending on the line and how long you take over eating.

## Making a Kreuzberg afternoon of it

The surrounding streets fill a few hours easily. A few doors along Mehringdamm is **Curry 36**, one of Berlin's best-known currywurst stands. Plenty of visitors pair the two on a single street-food crawl, so split portions if you plan to try both.

Walk south and west and you reach the **Bergmannkiez**, with its cafés, secondhand shops and the Marheineke Markthalle. A little further on is **Viktoriapark**. Its hill has the Kreuzberg monument and views north over the city, and it's a good place to sit down with a kebab you carried from the kiosk. On a warm day the walk to the top takes about 15 minutes.
