## What SANA is and why it's suddenly everywhere

SANA opened in 2026 as the signature restaurant of NUJU, a brand-new hotel in Bingin, Uluwatu. It serves Australian-Asian food all day, from breakfast through late dinner. Within months it became one of Bali's most-discussed new tables, largely on the strength of its setting and its desserts.

Uluwatu's dining scene has clustered around clifftop bars and beach clubs for years. SANA breaks that pattern by sitting roadside rather than ocean-front, with a grand entrance built to be a destination in itself. That entrance, plus the hotel pedigree behind it, is a big part of the draw.

## The setting: roadside, not clifftop

Don't expect a sunset-over-the-ocean view here; SANA's position is Bingin's main road, not the cliff edge. The payoff is the arrival experience: a designed, statement entrance that signals you're walking into a hotel restaurant with serious backing, not a casual warung.

- Location: inside NUJU hotel, Bingin, Uluwatu
- Service style: all-day dining, breakfast to dinner
- Cuisine: Australian-Asian fusion
- Standout element: the entrance sequence and dessert menu

## The food: Australian-Asian, dessert-led

SANA's kitchen leans on an Australian-Asian menu, blending brunch-friendly Western dishes with Asian flavors and technique across the day's service. Specific dishes and prices weren't confirmed for this guide, so check SANA's own listings or the NUJU hotel site before you go.

What consistently comes up in early reports is dessert. If you're choosing one meal to have here, make it one that ends with pudding rather than a quick coffee stop; the sweets are what's driving repeat visits and photos.

## Booking a table without the wait

SANA's newness and buzz mean walk-ins can mean a wait, especially at peak brunch and dinner hours. Treat it like any hot new hotel restaurant in Bali: reserve ahead through NUJU rather than showing up cold.

- Book via NUJU hotel's website or direct contact, not third-party apps, for the most accurate availability
- Confirm current opening hours before setting out, as a new venue's schedule can shift in its first year
- Ask about dress code and whether hotel guests get seating priority over walk-ins
- Since it sits on Bingin's main road, factor in Uluwatu traffic if you're coming from Seminyak or Canggu, journeys that can run well over an hour

Bingin itself is walkable to the area's surf breaks and cliffside warungs, so pair a SANA meal with a stroll down to Bingin Beach before or after. As with any newly opened restaurant, treat exact hours, booking rules and menu details as fluid and verify them directly with NUJU on the day you plan to visit.
