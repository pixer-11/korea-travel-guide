## A Sunday night in Carabanchel

Carabanchel is not where most visitors sleep in Madrid. It is a working residential district south of the Manzanares river, all brick apartment blocks, corner bars and fruit shops. On November 15, 2026, that changes for one night. Till Lindemann brings his Meine Welt Tour to Palacio Vistalegre.

The date falls on a Sunday. Madrid winds down slowly on Sunday evenings, but Monday is a normal working day. That shapes the night: plenty of fans make a weekend of it and stay in the city on Sunday night rather than travelling home after the encore.

## The voice behind Rammstein, on his own terms

You know the voice. Lindemann has fronted Rammstein since the 1990s, and that rolling baritone carries straight over into his solo work. Away from the band, he made two albums with Swedish producer Peter Tägtgren under the name Lindemann: *Skills in Pills* (2015) and *F & M* (2019). He followed them in 2023 with *Zunge*, released under his own name.

His solo shows have a reputation for theatrical, deliberately provocative staging. They are built for a crowd that already knows the Rammstein playbook and wants it stranger. Expect a room full of black T-shirts and German lyrics sung back by people who learned them phonetically. The setlist and support act for Madrid are not part of what is known here, so this guide doesn't guess at them.

## Inside Palacio Vistalegre

The arena is round. It stands on the site of the old Vista Alegre bullring and keeps that circular footprint, with tiers of seating rising around a central floor under a permanent roof. In practice, sound and heat stay inside, which suits a loud, heavy show in mid-November.

Standing fans fill the floor, which works like a bullring sand: everyone close and facing inward. The seated tiers give you a clearer, higher view of any stage effects. The venue is used for concerts, basketball and political rallies, so the layout is designed for crowds moving in and out quickly.

## Getting there on Line 5

The Metro is the easy answer. Two stations sit close to the arena:

- **Vista Alegre** (Line 5): the nearest stop, a short walk from the venue.
- **Oporto** (Lines 5 and 6): slightly further, but useful if you are coming round on the Line 6 circle.

Line 5 runs north through the centre, stopping at Ópera, Callao and Gran Vía. From a hotel near Puerta del Sol, the ride takes roughly 15–20 minutes. Taxis and ride-hailing apps work too, though cars crawl on the streets around the arena after a show.

November nights in Madrid are cool and usually dry. A jacket you can tie round your waist inside the arena is what most fans bring.

## How a Madrid gig night runs

Madrid keeps late hours, and concert-goers plan around that. Dinner before a show is often a quick round of tapas rather than a full meal, and the proper dinner comes afterwards. Fans tend to gather in the bars near the venue first, then walk over together.

A few habits worth knowing:

- **Paying:** card and contactless are accepted almost everywhere, including most bars in Carabanchel.
- **Tipping:** locals usually round up or leave small coins; a large tip isn't expected.
- **Metro tickets:** journeys load onto a rechargeable Multi card, sold from station machines. Locals top it up before the show to skip the post-gig queue at the machines.
- **Ordering:** "una caña" gets you a small draught beer, the default round at any bar counter.

After the encore, the Vista Alegre platform crowds quickly. Many fans walk the extra few minutes to Oporto, where the crowd thins out.

## Before and after: Calle del General Ricardos

General Ricardos is Carabanchel's main street, and it runs past the area around the arena. It is lined with ordinary neighbourhood bars where a drink usually comes with a small free tapa, a Madrid habit that survives most strongly in districts like this one. These are local places, not concert-themed bars, and on a Sunday some keep shorter hours.

If you are making a weekend of it, Madrid Río is just to the north. It is a long riverside park along the Manzanares and an easy walk or short ride from Carabanchel. Spend Saturday in the centre around Plaza Mayor and the Royal Palace, then head south for the show on Sunday.
