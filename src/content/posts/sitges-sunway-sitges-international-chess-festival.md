## Eleven days of rated chess by the sea

Sitges in December is a beach town without its summer crowds. Restaurants on the Passeig Marítim stay open, the promenade belongs to dog walkers, and for eleven days a large share of the visitors carry scoresheets and pocket sets.

The Sunway Sitges International Chess Festival runs from December 12 to 22, 2026. This edition belongs to the FIDE Circuit 2026-2027, the international federation's season-long ranking that serves as one of the routes towards the Candidates Tournament. That status is why strong international titled players come here, even though the setting is a resort town of about 30,000 people.

## What the festival looks like

The main event is a FIDE-rated open, so strong grandmasters and club amateurs take part in the same competition. In a Swiss open, everyone plays every round and pairings depend on the score so far. In the early rounds, a 1600-rated player can find themselves facing a titled professional.

What you'll find in a hall like this:

- Long rows of tables, each with a board, a clock and a scoresheet
- Top boards set apart, often with live broadcast boards
- Arbiters walking the aisles
- Players stepping out between moves to stretch or study other games

The festival's name and timing are the only details given in this guide's verified facts. The organiser publishes the dates, venue, round times and registration or spectator details on its official site. Round schedules and pairings for FIDE events are usually also posted on chess-results.com.

## Watching a round without breaking the silence

A tournament hall has rules, and spectators are expected to follow them as players do.

- **Phones:** players face forfeit if their device sounds, and spectators keep theirs silent as well. Many keep phones out of the playing area altogether.
- **Talking:** analysis happens outside the hall, never beside the boards.
- **Distance:** spectators stand behind the barrier or rope line and avoid hovering over a game in progress.
- **Photos:** flash is frowned on. Event photography is usually allowed only in the opening minutes of a round.

Dinner in Catalonia starts late, around 9pm, so most players eat after the evening round. Bars around Carrer de Sant Pau and the old-town lanes fill with post-game analysis. Cards are accepted almost everywhere. Small tips are customary only for good service, usually by rounding up.

## Getting to Sitges and passing the time between rounds

Sitges sits about 35 km southwest of Barcelona. Rodalies line R2 Sud trains from Barcelona Sants and Passeig de Gràcia reach Sitges station in roughly 35–45 minutes. Some players stay in Barcelona and commute, but most stay in town because evening rounds end late.

From Barcelona–El Prat Airport, the drive along the C-32 coastal motorway takes about 25 minutes. Sitges station is a short walk from the seafront and the old town.

On a rest day or a free morning:

- Walk to the **Església de Sant Bartomeu i Santa Tecla**, the baroque church on the headland above the beach
- Visit the **Cau Ferrat**, Santiago Rusiñol's former home and studio, now a museum of modernista art and ironwork
- Walk the **Passeig Marítim**, which runs for several kilometres along the town's beaches

December brings mild daytime temperatures and short days. A morning walk along the waterfront is the usual way to clear your head before an afternoon round.
