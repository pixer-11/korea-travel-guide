## Getting out to Expo

Singapore EXPO is a cluster of very large exhibition halls on Expo Drive, in the Changi South and Tampines corner of the island. It sits a long way from the Marina Bay skyline, and that changes how the evening works.

The rail link is the easy part. Expo MRT station is an interchange:

- **Downtown Line (DT35):** runs straight in from Bugis, Chinatown and the city fringe without changes.
- **East-West Line, Changi Airport branch (CG1):** connects to Tanah Merah for the main East-West Line, and to Changi Airport two stops the other way.

The station's swooping, metal-clad roof is easy to spot. The EXPO halls are a short covered walk away, past Changi City Point mall. On the train, tap a contactless bank card or an EZ-Link card at the gates. Eating and drinking are banned on trains and inside paid station areas, and the rule is enforced.

## Monsoon-season timing

Late November falls within Singapore's northeast monsoon. This is the wettest stretch of the year, and heavy afternoon and evening thunderstorms are routine.

That matters out here, because a lot of the waiting happens outdoors between the station and the halls. Experienced fans usually carry a compact umbrella or a light poncho. Inside, Singapore's air-conditioning runs cold. After hours of sticky queueing, a thin layer for the show itself is a small comfort that many concertgoers swear by.
