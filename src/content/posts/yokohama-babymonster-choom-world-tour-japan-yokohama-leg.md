## The venue: Pia Arena MM

Pia Arena MM opened in 2020 in Yokohama's Minato Mirai ("Minatomirai," literally "harbor of the future") district, the city's redeveloped waterfront zone known for the Cosmo Clock 21 Ferris wheel, the Landmark Tower, and Yokohama Red Brick Warehouse. The arena was designed with K-pop and J-pop concerts in mind, with tiered seating and a large floor configuration that arena tours like this one typically use. ## How to visit like a local

Japanese concert crowds are famously orderly, and the rhythm is easier to match than to fight. Tickets for big tours are generally sorted well ahead — most sales for shows like this run through pre-registered lottery systems weeks in advance, and last-minute box-office pickups are not reliable.

Cash is still widely used for merchandise stalls at Japanese arena shows, so yen goes further than card or mobile pay at many booths; lines for goods often form hours before doors and move slowly, which makes merch and a calm entry something of a trade-off. Inside the venue, Japanese fan culture favors coordinated chants and light-stick waving over shouting individual requests, and the surrounding crowd's cues are the best guide.

The most common visitor mistake is underestimating transit timing on the return leg — trains from Minatomirai and Sakuragicho get very crowded immediately after a show lets out, and a short walk to a quieter station is the usual workaround for a late connection.
