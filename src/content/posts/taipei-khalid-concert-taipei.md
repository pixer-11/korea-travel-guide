## Khalid plays Xinyi on December 7

On December 7, 2026, Khalid's soft-voiced R&B comes to Taipei. The American singer plays the Taipei International Convention Center, usually shortened to TICC. The building sits in Xinyi, the city's glass-and-steel business district, and Taipei 101 rises directly across the road.

Khalid grew up in El Paso, Texas. He broke through with the 2017 album *American Teen* and songs like "Location" and "Young Dumb & Broke". Those were followed by "Talk", "Better" and the album *Free Spirit*. A Taipei crowd also knows him from collaborations: "Lovely" with Billie Eilish, "Eastside" with Benny Blanco and Halsey, and "Love Lies" with Normani.

## Inside the convention center

TICC is a conference venue first and a concert hall second. That shapes the night. Its main auditorium is a fixed-seat, raked hall with a few thousand seats, so there is no standing pit and no scramble for the rail. Everyone has a numbered seat, and even the back rows sit relatively close to the stage.

For Khalid's music, which leans on a low, warm vocal more than on pyrotechnics, that closeness helps. Ballads like "Better" carry well in a room built for amplified speech. The lobby is wide and plain, with signage in Chinese and English directing people to the correct hall level and door.

## Riding the red line in

The MRT is how most of the audience gets there. The station is **Taipei 101/World Trade Center** on the red Tamsui–Xinyi Line (R03). From there it is a few minutes on foot past the World Trade Center exhibition halls to TICC's entrance on Xinyi Road.

- **Alternative stop:** Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall Station on the blue Bannan Line, about a 10–15 minute walk north-east through the Xinyi malls
- **Payment:** an EasyCard or iPASS covers the MRT, buses and convenience stores
- **Taxis:** they queue along Xinyi Road, though the curb is congested when a large hall empties

After the encore, the red line platform at Taipei 101 fills quickly. Many attendees walk one stop to Xiangshan or Xinyi Anhe to board with less of a crush.

## How tickets and concert nights work in Taipei

Taiwanese concert tickets are normally sold online through platforms such as KKTIX, tixCraft and ibon. ibon also runs ticket kiosks in 7-Eleven stores. The promoter and its chosen platform publish this show's seating plan, ticket tiers and entry details. The convention center's own box office is not where they are sold.

Some customs that local concertgoers follow:

- **Bring ID that matches the ticket.** Real-name ticketing is common at Taiwanese shows, and staff may compare names at the door.
- **Camera rules are the promoter's call.** Ushers at TICC do enforce them, so phones usually stay low during the set.
- **Singing along is expected.** Fans usually stay quiet through the verses of the slower songs, then the room joins in on the choruses.
- **Bags stay small.** The aisles in a seated hall are narrow, and large luggage is awkward at security.

Fans typically arrive about an hour before doors so they have time to buy merchandise. The merch tables are usually set up in the lobby.

## Eating around Taipei 101

Xinyi is full of food, much of it inside the malls. The basement food courts at Taipei 101 and the Shin Kong Mitsukoshi buildings serve noodles, dumplings and bento quickly. They suit a pre-show meal when time is tight. Din Tai Fung's Taipei 101 branch is the famous dumpling stop, and its queue at dinner time is just as famous.

For something more local after the show, Linjiang Street Night Market (also called Tonghua Night Market) is near Xinyi Anhe Station, one MRT stop west. Its stalls sell pepper buns, grilled squid and bubble tea late into the evening. Raohe Night Market in Songshan is a longer trip but one that visitors often pair with the show.

## December in the city

Early December in Taipei is cool and often damp. Evenings are usually mild rather than cold, and a light jacket and a compact umbrella cover most conditions. The convention hall itself is air-conditioned.

Visitors staying a day or two usually take in the Xinyi area around the show. The trail up Elephant Mountain starts near Xiangshan Station and gives the classic view of Taipei 101 at dusk. The Taipei 101 observatory is next to the venue, and Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall is a short walk north.
