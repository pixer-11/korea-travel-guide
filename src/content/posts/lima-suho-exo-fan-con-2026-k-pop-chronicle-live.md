## One night, one EXO leader, one park

Suho brings his fan-con to Lima on Friday, December 18, 2026. The event is billed as **2026 K-POP Chronicle Live**, and it is held at the Explanada Parque de la Exposición.

Suho, born Kim Jun-myeon, has led EXO since the group debuted with SM Entertainment in 2012. His solo career began with the 2020 mini-album *Self-Portrait*. A fan-con mixes performance with talk segments and interaction, so expect a different evening from a straight concert.

## The esplanade at Parque de la Exposición

The venue is open ground, not an arena. Parque de la Exposición dates from the 1870s and holds the white Palacio de la Exposición, now home to MALI (Museo de Arte de Lima). The explanada is the flat open space Lima promoters use for large outdoor shows.

December is the start of Lima's summer. Rain is close to unheard of, and evenings are usually mild with a sea breeze off the Pacific. A light layer covers the night; an umbrella almost never earns its place in the bag.

Within walking distance of the park:

- **MALI**, inside the park itself, for Peruvian art from pre-Columbian textiles onward
- **Parque de la Reserva** and its Circuito Mágico del Agua fountains, south across Av. 28 de Julio
- **Estadio Nacional**, just east across the Vía Expresa
- **Plaza San Martín** and the historic centre, roughly a 15-minute walk north along Paseo Colón

## Getting to Cercado de Lima

The Metropolitano is the most direct way in. This bus rapid transit line runs down the Vía Expresa (Paseo de la República) from Barranco through Miraflores. Its underground **Estación Central** sits beside Paseo de los Héroes Navales, a few minutes on foot from the park's northern edge. **Estación Estadio Nacional** is the other close stop.

Most international visitors base themselves in Miraflores or Barranco, which keeps them on the same Metropolitano line. A Friday evening in central Lima means heavy traffic on Av. Arequipa and the Vía Expresa. Fans coming by car usually budget well over the map estimate.

After dark, Limeños rarely flag taxis on the street. They order through Uber, Cabify, inDrive or DiDi, and set the pickup a block or two from the venue gate, away from the post-show crush.

## Doing the fan-con the Lima way

Peru's K-pop fanbase is large and organised. EXO-Ls in Lima typically run fan projects, such as coordinated banners and printed slogans, and hand out small freebies like photocards and stickers near the entrance queue. Bringing a few of your own to trade is common practice.

Practical habits attendees follow:

- **Payment:** cards work at most stalls; locals also pay with Yape and Plin, Peru's phone-transfer apps, which visitors without a Peruvian bank account cannot use. Small soles notes cover street vendors.
- **Queueing:** "la fila" forms by ticket zone, and staff direct each zone to its own gate.
- **Language:** stage talk is in Korean, with interpretation into Spanish. A few phrases help with staff: *entrada* (ticket), *puerta* (gate), *salida* (exit).
- **Phones:** central Lima crowds attract pickpockets. Fans keep phones in front pockets or zipped bags, not back pockets.

Ticket prices, zones and door times sit with the promoter's ticketing for this date, not with the park or MALI.
