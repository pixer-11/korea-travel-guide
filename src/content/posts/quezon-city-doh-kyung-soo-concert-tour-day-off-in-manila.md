## A Saturday in Cubao for D.O.

EXO's D.O. comes to the Big Dome. Doh Kyung-soo, the group's lead vocalist since its 2012 debut and a film and drama actor in his own right, plays Smart Araneta Coliseum in Quezon City on Saturday, January 23, 2027. The Manila date is part of his 2026–2027 Concert Tour [DAY OFF].

This is a solo show. The draw is his voice: unhurried, warm and close to the microphone, the sound that carried his solo ballads and acoustic work outside EXO. It suits a hall built around a round, steeply raked bowl, where even the upper tiers look down on the stage instead of across a flat field.

Manila's EXO-L fanbase is one of the longest-running K-pop followings in Southeast Asia. On a show night the concourse fills with lightsticks, printed banners and fan-made merchandise well before the music starts.

## Inside the Big Dome

Smart Araneta Coliseum opened in 1960. For decades it has been the default room for big indoor concerts and basketball in Metro Manila. Locals call it the Big Dome, and the name is literal: a wide, low concrete roof spans the whole seating bowl without pillars blocking the view.

Its most famous night belongs to boxing. In 1975 Muhammad Ali and Joe Frazier fought the "Thrilla in Manila" here, and the mall across the plaza is still called Ali Mall. Seating rises in tiers from the floor up to the Upper Box and General Admission levels near the roof.

Seating plans for concerts depend on how each show sets its stage. The organiser publishes the seat map for each date alongside its ticket links.

## Getting to Araneta City

Rail is the easiest way in. Two lines meet at Cubao:

- **MRT-3, Araneta Center-Cubao station** sits on EDSA, with covered walkways into Gateway Mall and on to the Coliseum.
- **LRT-2, Araneta Center-Cubao station** sits on Aurora Boulevard and connects through Gateway Mall too.

Coming from Makati or Ortigas, MRT-3 runs straight up EDSA. From the Santolan or Recto side, LRT-2 is the more direct line. Saturday traffic on EDSA and Aurora Boulevard is heavy, so ride-hail cars often crawl the last kilometre.

Araneta City has several multi-level car parks, but they fill on big event nights. That is why so many concertgoers arrive by train or get dropped off.

## How EXO-L do a show night in Manila

Fan culture here runs on generosity and noise. Attendees usually do some version of the following:

- **Freebies before doors.** Fan clubs hand out photocards, stickers, banners and snacks around the Coliseum plaza. Fans often bring small items of their own to trade.
- **Lightsticks.** The official EXO lightstick is the standard accessory. Many fans also carry printed slogans for planned fan events during the set.
- **Fanchants.** Filipino crowds are loud and know the chants. Ballads get a hush; the call-and-response parts get the whole bowl.
- **Paying.** Mall food courts and most shops take cards and GCash. Small street vendors around Cubao are more comfortable with cash.

Bag rules and banned items follow the venue's security policy. Queues for entry form along the Coliseum's outer ring, and fans usually line up by gate according to their seat section.

## Before the lights go down: eating around Cubao

Araneta City is a full commercial district, so nobody goes hungry. Gateway Mall and Gateway Mall 2 have food courts and sit-down chains, connected to the Coliseum by walkways. Ali Mall is older and cheaper, with fast-food counters that serve long queues quickly.

Farmers Market, the wet market and food hall at the edge of the complex, is the local option. Grilled seafood and Filipino comfort dishes are the reason to go. Its stalls do a steady trade with people heading to Coliseum events.

## Cubao after the encore

The real test comes when the show ends. Thousands of people leave at the same moment, and the platforms at both rail stations fill up fast. On a Saturday the trains stop running around midnight, so a long show can end close to the last services.

Some fans wait out the first rush over a late meal in the malls. Others book a room for the night at a hotel inside Araneta City, such as Novotel Manila Araneta City. Ride-hail fares tend to surge right after a major concert, and pickup points around the Coliseum get crowded.

Cubao has other places to fill a weekend around the show. The Cubao Expo arts strip, with its vintage shops and small bars, is a short ride away. It suits fans making a full Saturday of it.
