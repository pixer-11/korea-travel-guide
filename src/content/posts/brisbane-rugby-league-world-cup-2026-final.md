The ground suits a final. Brisbane locals still call it Lang Park, and it holds about 52,500 people. It is the Queensland home of State of Origin. The stands rise steeply and close to the touchline, so even the upper tiers look almost straight down onto the in-goal areas.

## Getting to Suncorp Stadium

The stadium sits in Milton, on the western edge of the CBD. Driving is impractical because there is no public parking at the venue. Event-day road closures also make nearby streets slow.

- **Train:** Milton station sits just across from the stadium on the Ipswich/Rosewood and Springfield lines. Roma Street station is about a 15-minute walk away.
- **Bus:** event shuttles usually run from the CBD and from suburban hubs.
- **Ticket travel:** Suncorp event tickets have traditionally included Translink public transport on match day. The conditions are printed on each ticket.

Getting away usually takes at least an hour after full-time. Platforms at Milton fill first, so walking to Roma Street is often quicker.

## Matchday on Caxton Street

Brisbane's pre-match ritual happens on one street. Caxton Street runs from Petrie Terrace downhill to the stadium. It is lined with pubs, the Caxton Hotel among them, and they fill with jerseys hours before kick-off. Fans have a drink there, then walk the last few hundred metres with the crowd.

How a big match day usually runs inside:

- **Payment:** the stadium is cashless, so food and drink are paid for by card or phone wallet.
- **Bags:** venue bag rules apply to anything bigger than a small bag.
- **Anthems and war dances:** the Pacific nations traditionally perform the Sipi Tau, the Haka and the Cibi before kick-off. The stadium goes quiet for them, and the crowd is expected to stay silent.
- **Staying for both:** at double-headers, many fans keep their seats between matches instead of going back out. Re-entry rules vary from event to event.

With a free morning, Brisbane's South Bank parklands and the Queensland Museum are a short ride across the river from Roma Street. They are easy to see before an afternoon at the stadium.
