## What to expect once you're inside

Multi-day Indonesian festivals like this one typically run from late afternoon into night, with local and international acts alternating stages so attendees can bounce between a Barasuara set and a JUICY LUICY set without missing much. Jakarta heat and humidity are real factors even after dark in August, and crowds tend to thin out near food stalls and shaded areas between big sets.

Indonesian festival crowds tend to be enthusiastic but good-natured; singing along in Bahasa Indonesia to Pamungkas or Barasuara is part of the experience even for those who don't speak the language, since these are some of the most sung-along-to acts in the country's current scene.
