## One orchestra, one Friday, one giant museum

The Greatest Hits Symphonic takes place on Friday, December 11, 2026, at the Grand Egyptian Museum in Giza. A live orchestra plays songs from films and stage musicals. These are melodies much of the audience can already hum.

The organiser publishes the dates and ticket links on its official site. This guide covers what the facts support: the date, the building and the practicalities of a night on Cairo's western edge.

## The venue at the edge of the plateau

This museum is built on a monumental scale. It sits on the desert shelf roughly 2 km from the Giza pyramids, between the plateau and the Cairo–Alexandria Desert Road. Its front is a vast sloping wall of translucent stone panels set in triangular patterns, an echo of the pyramids beyond.

Inside, the building is just as big:

- **The atrium** holds the colossal granite statue of Ramesses II, the one that once stood in central Cairo's Ramses Square.
- **The Grand Staircase** climbs past rows of pharaonic statuary toward windows framing the pyramids.
- **The Tutankhamun galleries** gather the boy king's burial treasures together.

A concert here means hearing film scores in a space built for 3,000-year-old kings. That contrast is a large part of the appeal.

## Getting out to Giza on a Friday night

Friday is Egypt's main day off. Daytime roads are usually calmer than on weekdays, but the routes west across the Nile still slow down in the evening. Most concertgoers come by car, and there is no metro station at the museum door.

- **Ride-hailing:** Uber and Careem both operate across Cairo, and the fare shows in the app before you ride. Most visitors use one of these.
- **From downtown or Zamalek:** the drive is around 20 km and crosses the Nile. In traffic it often takes an hour.
- **From hotels in the Pyramids area:** it is a short hop, which is why many concert visitors stay on the Giza side.

Small tips, known locally as baksheesh, are normal for drivers, porters and attendants. People usually carry Egyptian pounds in small notes for them. Desert evenings in December turn cool, so attendees typically bring a jacket. Cairo concert crowds tend to dress smart-casual.

## Making a full Giza day of it

Most visitors pair the evening with the pyramids. The Great Pyramid of Khufu, the pyramids of Khafre and Menkaure, and the Great Sphinx are all within a short drive. Morning on the plateau is the usual choice, before the midday sun gets strong.

The Mena House hotel sits right at the foot of the plateau and has hosted visitors since the 19th century. It's a classic place for a drink with a view of the pyramids before heading over to the museum. Saqqara's Step Pyramid of Djoser lies about 25 km south for anyone adding another day. Spending a weekend in Giza lets the concert, the museum's galleries and the plateau each have their own time.
