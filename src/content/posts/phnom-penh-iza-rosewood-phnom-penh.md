## What Iza actually is

Iza sits inside Rosewood Phnom Penh, the hotel occupying the upper floors of Vattanac Capital Tower on Monivong Boulevard. It's a Japanese fine-dining room, not a hotel lobby lounge or a grab-a-bowl izakaya. The draw is technical precision: knife work, dashi, the kind of course-by-course discipline that separates a trained Japanese chef from a fusion menu wearing Japanese words.

That distinction matters in Phnom Penh, where Japanese restaurants range widely in authenticity. Iza has built its name specifically on the chef's command of technique, which is why it gets mentioned alongside the city's best upscale dining rather than its best cheap sushi.

## Why it stands out in Phnom Penh

Phnom Penh's fine-dining scene is small compared to Bangkok or Singapore, so a hotel restaurant with genuine culinary depth stands out fast. Iza's reputation rests less on spectacle and more on craft: the kind of meal where technique is the headline, not the view or the wine list.

Being inside Rosewood also shapes the experience. Expect the service standards, room design and pacing of an international five-star hotel rather than a standalone chef-driven restaurant with its own quirks and hours.

- Location: Rosewood Phnom Penh, Vattanac Capital Tower, Monivong Boulevard
- Cuisine: Japanese fine dining
- Known for: a chef with strong command of Japanese technique
- Context: one of the city's standout upscale restaurants

## Booking a table at Iza

Treat this like any hotel fine-dining room: reservations are the norm, not an afterthought. Rosewood hotels typically take restaurant bookings through their concierge or hotel website, and a Japanese tasting-style room like this will fill its limited seats on weekend evenings first.

- Call or email Rosewood Phnom Penh directly to book
- Ask about dress code, since hotel fine dining in the tower skews smart-casual to formal
- Confirm current opening days and seating times before you go; hotel restaurant schedules change with season and staffing, and none are listed here to avoid passing along stale information
- If you have dietary restrictions, mention them at booking rather than at the table, standard practice for tasting-format Japanese menus

## Getting to Vattanac Capital Tower

The tower is one of Phnom Penh's tallest landmarks, hard to miss on Monivong Boulevard near the Bassac riverfront side of downtown. Arrive by tuk-tuk or ride-hail app rather than trying to self-navigate parking in a high-security office tower.

Give the driver the hotel name directly, since Rosewood Phnom Penh is the address most drivers and security desks will recognize over the tower's own name. Building security in Cambodian office towers can be strict, so allow a few extra minutes to clear the lobby and reach the hotel's dedicated elevators.

Pair a visit with the tower's other draw: Rosewood's rooftop bar sits above it, so some visitors do a pre-dinner drink up top before heading down to Iza, though confirm with the hotel whether that flow works with your reservation time.
