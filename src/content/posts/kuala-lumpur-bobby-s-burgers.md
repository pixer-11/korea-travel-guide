## What Bobby's Burgers actually is

Bobby's Burgers is the Kuala Lumpur outpost of a concept built by Bobby Saputra, an Indonesian social-media personality with a large following across the region. The brand's calling card is the Bobby's Smash Burger, made with Angus beef and flattened hard on the griddle for a crisp, lacy edge. It landed in KL riding heavy online momentum before most diners had tasted a single patty, the kind of pre-launch hype that now regularly outpaces word of mouth.

## Finding it inside The Exchange TRX

The restaurant is located within The Exchange TRX, the mall anchoring the Tun Razak Exchange financial district in central Kuala Lumpur. TRX is served by its own MRT station, TRX, on the Kajang Line, putting the mall a short walk from the platform without needing a taxi or rideshare. The Exchange TRX is a large multi-level retail complex, so once inside, use the mall directory or signage to track down the unit rather than wandering floor to floor.
