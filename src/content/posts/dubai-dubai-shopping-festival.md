Six weeks of discounts, raffles and free shows take over a city that already treats the mall as a public square. The 32nd Dubai Shopping Festival runs from December 4, 2026 to January 17, 2027. It falls in Dubai's cooler months, when daytime temperatures usually sit in the low to mid 20s°C and evenings outdoors are pleasant.

## Opening weekend at Coca-Cola Arena

Two very different headliners open this edition. Rashid Al Majed, the Saudi singer whose career runs back to the 1980s, brings Gulf Arabic pop. Steve Aoki, the American DJ and producer, brings big-room electronic sets and his habit of throwing cakes into the crowd.

Both play Coca-Cola Arena, the indoor venue at City Walk in Al Wasl. These are the festival's ticketed events, sold separately from the free programme. Taxis and ride-hailing cars are how most concertgoers reach City Walk, since no Metro station sits at the door.

## Light shows at JBR and Bluewaters

The DSF Light Show runs nightly at JBR and Bluewaters. It costs nothing to watch from the public waterfront. JBR's beachfront promenade, The Walk, faces Bluewaters Island across a short stretch of water. A pedestrian bridge links the two, so you can watch from either side and walk across afterwards.

Getting there:

- **Dubai Tram:** the JBR 1 and JBR 2 stops sit behind the beach.
- **Metro:** the Red Line connects to the tram at DMCC and Sobha Realty stations.
- **On foot:** Bluewaters, home to the Ain Dubai wheel, is a few minutes over the bridge from The Beach at JBR.

Families usually make an evening of it. They eat along The Walk, then stay for the show.

## Hatta and the wider city

The festival reaches beyond the coast. A candlelight concert is held in Hatta, the mountain enclave in the Hajar range, roughly 90 minutes' drive from central Dubai. The area around Hatta Dam, with its turquoise water and kayaks, makes the trip a full day.

Back in town, retail promotions run across malls and souks. DSF has been held since 1996. Its long-running traditions include raffles for cars and gold, and markdowns at large malls such as The Dubai Mall and Mall of the Emirates. The Gold Souk in Deira also takes part.

## Shopping DSF the way residents do

Residents treat the festival as a season, not a single trip. Most spread their shopping over several visits and save evenings for the outdoor shows. A few habits help:

- **Payment:** cards and phone wallets work almost everywhere. Cash is handy only in older souk stalls.
- **Bargaining:** prices are fixed in malls. In Deira's souks, haggling is expected, and gold is priced by weight plus a making charge.
- **Raffle entries:** these usually come with a qualifying purchase at participating stores, so keep your receipts.
- **Transport:** a Nol card covers the Metro, tram and buses. Each Metro train has a Gold Class carriage and a section for women and children.
- **Dress:** malls post signs asking for covered shoulders and knees. Swimwear belongs on the beach, not The Walk.

Dubai Festivals and Retail Establishment organises DSF. It publishes the full programme, venues and participating retailers on the official festival site, and arena ticket links sit with Coca-Cola Arena.
