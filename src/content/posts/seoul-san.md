## Eating and behaving like you belong there

Korean fine dining rooms at this level are generally quiet, and conversation stays low so the pacing of courses doesn't get lost in noise. Dress a notch above Gangnam's daytime casual, closer to what you'd wear to a serious dinner in any major city.

Payment is by card as standard in Seoul's restaurant scene, and a service charge or its equivalent may already be built into how the meal is billed, so ask rather than assume you need to tip extra. If you arrive early, don't loiter at the stairwell entrance; Korean service culture generally prefers you wait to be seated rather than hovering at the host stand.
