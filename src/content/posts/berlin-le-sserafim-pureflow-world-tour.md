## A first for Europe

This is the night LE SSERAFIM first played Europe. On Monday, October 26, 2026, the five-member group performs at Uber Arena as a stop on the PUREFLOW World Tour. Chaewon, Sakura, Yunjin, Kazuha and Eunchae bring the HYBE/Source Music group's first European show to Berlin.

The venue opened in 2008 as O2 World. It became Mercedes-Benz Arena in 2015 and Uber Arena in 2024. In concert layout it holds roughly 17,000 people, with a standing floor in front of the stage and steep tiered seating curving around three sides.

## Getting to Uber Platz

The arena faces the Spree in Friedrichshain, on the open square now called Uber Platz. The nearest stations are:

- **Warschauer Straße**: S3, S5, S7, S9 and the U1, about a five-minute walk east of the arena
- **Ostbahnhof**: S-Bahn and regional trains, roughly ten minutes' walk west
- **Tram M10**: terminates at Warschauer Straße

The concert falls in fare zone A of the BVG network. Between the station and the arena you pass the East Side Mall, a convenient spot for food. Across Mühlenstraße runs the East Side Gallery, the longest surviving stretch of the Berlin Wall. The red-brick Oberbaumbrücke crosses the river just south of Warschauer Straße.

## Inside Uber Arena: the house rules

German arenas run on clear rules, and Uber Arena is no exception. The arena sets out its bag-size limits and prohibited items on its website. Fans with larger bags usually leave them at their hotel rather than risk the security line.

- **Tickets** are scanned at the gate; mobile tickets are standard for arena shows in Germany.
- **Lightsticks** are the norm in the stands. Most of the crowd holds the official one, and the arena glows with it during ballads.
- **Fan chants** for each song circulate online among fans before tour dates. A large share of the Berlin crowd shouts them in Korean.
- **Exit** traffic funnels toward Warschauer Straße, where platforms crowd heavily after the encore. Many attendees walk west to Ostbahnhof instead, where it is usually calmer.
