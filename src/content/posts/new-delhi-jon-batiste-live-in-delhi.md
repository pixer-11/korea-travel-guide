## A New Orleans pianist in Pragati Maidan

Jon Batiste's Delhi date was November 24, 2026. It was one stop on a two-city tour that marked his first run of shows in India, and Bharat Mandapam was the Delhi venue.

He brings a lot of history to that stage. His album *We Are* won Album of the Year at the 2022 Grammys, and he shared the Oscar for the score of Pixar's *Soul*. For years he led Stay Human, the house band on *The Late Show with Stephen Colbert*.

His live sets usually mix things up. Expect solo piano, New Orleans brass-band energy, gospel and pop, often in the same stretch. This guide names no setlist or support act, because the facts it was given include neither.

## Inside Bharat Mandapam

Bharat Mandapam is the convention centre that replaced the old Pragati Maidan exhibition halls. It opened in 2023, just before it hosted the G20 Leaders' Summit that September. Its curved main building takes its shape from a conch shell, the shankh.

Out front stands a bronze Nataraja, the dancing Shiva. It is a little over 8 metres tall and often described as the tallest Nataraja statue in the world. Concertgoers commonly use it as a meeting point.

The complex is big, with several halls and long covered walkways. Walking from the gate to your seat takes real minutes, not seconds.

## Getting to Pragati Maidan

The venue sits on Mathura Road, east of India Gate and next to Purana Qila.

- **Metro:** Supreme Court station on the Blue Line (it was called Pragati Maidan station until 2024) is the nearest stop. Mandi House, where the Blue and Violet lines meet, is a slightly longer walk.
- **Cabs:** Uber, Ola and auto-rickshaws drop off along Bhairon Marg and Mathura Road. Traffic around Pragati Maidan backs up badly when big events let out.
- **Nearby:** the Crafts Museum on Bhairon Marg, Purana Qila and the National Zoological Park are all within a short drive.

Late November evenings in Delhi are cool and dry. This is also the time of year when smog is usually at its worst, so many visitors carry a mask for the journey.

## How a Bharat Mandapam concert night runs

Large shows here follow the same pattern as other big Delhi venues.

- **Security:** expect airport-style bag checks and frisking at the entry gates. Attendees usually travel light, because power banks, lighters and outside food are commonly refused.
- **Tickets:** Indian concert apps normally deliver e-tickets with a QR code, and fans usually have it on screen with a photo ID before reaching the gate.
- **Paying:** food and bar counters inside generally accept UPI and cards. Locals pay almost everything by scanning a QR code with their phone.
- **Leaving:** the metro closes around midnight. After a show many people walk to Mandi House or book a cab from a quieter road, away from the crush of pickups at the gate.

Most Delhi concert crowds stay in their seats for the first half, then stand for the encore. Batiste often comes off the stage into the crowd with a melodica, so aisle seats get a closer view.
