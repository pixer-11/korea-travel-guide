## What Supernova is

Supernova is the Abu Dhabi transplant of a Soho burger concept that built a cult following in London on one idea done well: the smash burger. It occupies a villa locals call "The House," in the low-rise, leafy Al Bateen district rather than a mall food court. That setting alone sets it apart from Abu Dhabi's usual burger scene, which skews toward glossy chains and hotel coffee shops.

The menu stays tight and specific. Double patties are smashed hard on the flat-top so the edges turn lacy and crisp, a technique that separates smash burgers from the thicker, pub-style patty. Hand-cut fries round out the short list. There's no attempt at a sprawling menu of sides, salads or thirty burger variations; the whole point is doing one thing precisely.

## Why Al Bateen, not the malls

Al Bateen is a quieter, villa-lined neighborhood near the Corniche, known more for embassies and low buildings than for restaurant strips. Supernova's choice to open here, in a converted house rather than a retail unit, mirrors the original Soho branch's ethos: small, unpolished, and deliberately not mall-glossy. The minimalist aesthetic carries through: expect plain surfaces and a stripped interior rather than themed decor.

That location also means it isn't wedged into the tourist circuit around Yas Island or the Corniche hotels. You come here specifically, not on the way to something else. If you're staying near Corniche Road or Al Bateen's marina area, it's a short taxi ride; if you're coming from Yas or Saadiyat, budget proper transit time.

## Getting a table

Given how fast London's original built a following, expect early demand here too, especially on weekend evenings.

- Book ahead where possible; a new, buzzed-about opening like this can fill fast.
- Call or check its official channels for current opening hours before you go, since these can shift in the first months.
- Aim for an off-peak slot, like a weekday lunch, if you want to skip a wait.
- Don't expect an extensive menu on arrival; the appeal is the tightly edited one they've brought over from Soho.

## Ordering like it's Soho, not a food court

Order at the counter or table as directed by staff, but don't expect customization culture here. Smash burger joints of this style build the dish around a fixed method, not add-on choices, so the double patty and crisp edge are the point, not a base to modify.

- Go for the signature double-patty smash burger first; it's the reason the concept crossed continents.
- Pair it with the hand-cut fries rather than skipping to a side salad; there isn't a big alternative menu.
- Treat it as a focused, in-and-out meal rather than a long, lingering dinner, in keeping with the minimalist, no-frills setup.
- Confirm payment methods and any reservation policy directly with Supernova before visiting, since specifics like these change fastest at a new opening.
