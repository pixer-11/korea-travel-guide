## Starship's nine-piece sound comes to Cotai

CRAVITY brings <SONOROUS> to Macau for one night. The South Korean boy group plays The Londoner Theatre on December 19, 2026. That makes Macau a single stop on a tour built around the <SONOROUS> concert.

The group debuted under Starship Entertainment in 2020 with the album *Hideout: Remember Who We Are*. Since then they have built a reputation for sharp group choreography, and their fandom is called LUVITY. A theatre puts those formations closer to the seats than an arena would. Even the upper rows face the stage head-on rather than across a stadium floor.

## Finding The Londoner Theatre

The venue sits inside The Londoner Macao on the Cotai Strip. That is the reclaimed land between Taipa and Coloane, lined with integrated casino resorts. You can't miss the building: its façade copies the Houses of Parliament, and a replica of Elizabeth Tower rises above the entrance.

Ways in:
- **From Hong Kong by sea:** ferries to Taipa Ferry Terminal land closest to Cotai.
- **From Hong Kong airport:** buses across the Hong Kong–Zhuhai–Macau Bridge reach the Macau port, and from there it is a short taxi ride to Cotai.
- **Within Macau:** the Light Rapid Transit Taipa Line runs along the edge of Cotai. Taxis queue at the resort's ground-floor entrances.
- **On foot:** covered walkways and shopping corridors link The Londoner with The Venetian Macao and The Parisian Macao next door.

Inside, follow the signs past the casino floor and the shops to the theatre. Expect a long indoor walk: these resorts are huge and the corridors loop.

## Concert-night habits in a Macau theatre

The crowd mixes fans from Hong Kong, mainland China, Taiwan and Southeast Asia, so you'll hear Cantonese, Mandarin, Korean and English in the lobby. Fans usually bring the official CRAVITY lightstick, and many venues sync these to the show. Printed slogan banners for the fan chant moments are common too.

- **Cameras:** Macau concert venues typically ban professional cameras and detachable lenses, and security bag checks happen at the door.
- **Money:** Macau uses the pataca. Hong Kong dollars are widely accepted at resorts, and card and mobile payment work in most shops and restaurants inside them.
- **Merch:** fans usually buy merchandise at booths outside the hall before the show, and the lines build as doors approach.
- **Leaving:** the taxi rank empties fast after the encore. Many attendees walk through to a neighbouring resort's taxi stand or eat dinner nearby while the crowd thins.

## Around the show: December in Macau

December is Macau's cool, dry season, and evenings on the Strip are comfortable for walking. December 20 is Macau SAR Establishment Day, a public holiday, so the concert falls on a holiday-eve Saturday. Hotel demand around Cotai is usually high that weekend.

Visitors staying more than one night often split their time between Cotai and the Macau Peninsula. On the Peninsula you'll find the Ruins of St. Paul's and the Senado Square cobbles. Closer to the venue, Taipa Village's Rua do Cunha has Macanese snack shops selling pork chop buns and egg tarts, and it is a short taxi ride from The Londoner.
