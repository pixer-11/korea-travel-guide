## Inside Jakarta International Stadium

JIS opened in 2022 and is the home ground of football club Persija Jakarta. It holds around 82,000 people and has a retractable roof, one of the few stadiums in Southeast Asia built with one. The stands rise steeply on all four sides, so even the upper tiers sit closer to the pitch than in an older athletics bowl.

The stadium sits in Papanggo, Tanjung Priok, a long way from the hotel districts of Sudirman, Thamrin and Kemang. That distance shapes the whole evening. Allow at least an hour from central Jakarta in normal traffic, and more on a Saturday evening, which is what October 10, 2026 falls on.

## Getting to Tanjung Priok and back

You have three realistic ways to reach JIS:

- **KRL Commuter Line:** trains run to Tanjung Priok station. From there you continue by bus, ride-hail or on foot.
- **TransJakarta:** the city's bus network runs routes toward JIS. Pay with a tap-on card or app, not cash.
- **Ride-hail:** Gojek and Grab cars and motorbike taxis work well on the way in. The return trip is where it gets difficult.

After a sell-out stadium show, thousands of people request rides from the same few streets at once. Prices surge, drivers cancel, and roads lock up. Walking a few blocks away from the stadium before booking usually gets you picked up faster. Agree a meeting point with your group in advance too, because mobile data slows down when the whole crowd is posting videos at once.

A few local habits make the night smoother.

- **Dress for heat and rain:** October usually marks the start of Jakarta's rainy season. Afternoons are hot and humid, and evening downpours are common. A light poncho is easier to manage in a crowd than an umbrella, and some stadiums ban umbrellas outright.
- **Check the bag policy:** stadium security in Indonesia searches bags at the gate. Read the official list of banned items before you pack.
- **Eat beforehand:** queues for food inside a full stadium are long. A proper meal near your hotel saves you from relying on whatever stays stocked late in the night.

Merch lines tend to be shortest once the opening act is on stage, while most people are still finding their seats.
