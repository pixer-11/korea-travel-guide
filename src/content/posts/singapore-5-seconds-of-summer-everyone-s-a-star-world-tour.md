## Two nights, eight years in the making

5 Seconds of Summer have been away from Singapore for eight years. That gap matters. A lot of the fans who sang along to "She Looks So Perfect" as teenagers are now adults buying their own tickets, and this tour is where those two groups of fans meet.

The Everyone's A Star! World Tour plays The Star Theatre on 16 November and 3 December 2026. Both nights are at the same venue, seventeen days apart. Singapore is getting more than a single stop on a regional run.

## The band arriving on stage

The four-piece formed in Sydney in 2011: Luke Hemmings, Michael Clifford, Calum Hood and Ashton Irwin. They began by posting covers online and broke through opening stadium shows for One Direction. Then they built their own following with guitar-driven pop-rock.

Their catalogue covers a lot of ground. There are the early pop-punk singles, the darker and bigger sound of "Youngblood" and the heavier "Teeth". The tour's name comes from their album *Everyone's a Star!*. Nobody here has seen the setlist, so treat any song prediction as a guess.

## Inside The Star Theatre

The Star Theatre is not a stadium or an open field. It is a purpose-built auditorium with roughly 5,000 seats, set inside The Star Performing Arts Centre at The Star Vista in Buona Vista. The seating rises in steep tiers, so the upper levels are much closer to the stage than they look on a seating map.

It's fully indoors and air-conditioned. That counts in November and December, the northeast monsoon months, when heavy evening downpours are common. People who sit through a whole show often find the auditorium cool, which is why regulars carry a light layer.

The building itself is a curved glass-and-steel shell above a shopping mall. Escalators carry concertgoers up from the retail floors to the theatre foyers. Before doors open, the queue spills into the mall's public spaces and the outdoor amphitheatre steps.

## Getting to Buona Vista

The closest station is **Buona Vista MRT**, an interchange on the East-West Line (EW21) and the Circle Line (CC22). From there it is a short walk along a covered path to The Star Vista. **one-north MRT** (CC23) is the alternative one stop along the Circle Line, and it can be quieter after a show.

Approximate journeys from common bases:

- **City Hall / Raffles Place:** direct on the East-West Line, about 15–20 minutes
- **Orchard:** take the North-South Line to Dhoby Ghaut or Bishan, then change, or catch a short taxi
- **Holland Village:** one stop on the Circle Line, or a walk
- **Changi Airport:** East-West Line with a change at Tanah Merah, roughly an hour

Taxis and ride-hailing cars use the drop-off point at The Star Vista. Expect queues and surge pricing at the end of the night.

## Getting out of Buona Vista after the encore

Singapore's MRT is fully cashless. Most visitors tap a contactless bank card or a phone wallet at the gates through SimplyGo, so nobody has to buy tickets at a machine. This is the quickest way to get 5,000 people onto the platforms, and most of them use it.

On show nights, the crowd tends to head for the Buona Vista escalators all at once. Many locals go the other way and walk ten minutes to one-north station, or they eat first and let the rush pass. Since there are two dates, plenty of fans attend both. They usually know the route by the second night.

Singapore venues are strict about rules. Bag checks at the entrance are normal, and ushers do enforce seat numbers in the tiers. Merchandise lines usually form in the foyer as soon as doors open, and fans who want a particular size tend to go there first.

## Eating before the show

There's no need to leave the building to eat. The Star Vista's lower floors have food courts, casual restaurants and cafés, and they get busy in the hour before doors.

For something wider, these are close by:

- **Rochester Commons:** restaurants and bars in colonial-era bungalows, a short walk away
- **Holland Village:** one MRT stop away, with Holland Village Market & Food Centre and a strip of bars along Lorong Mambong
- **Ghim Moh Market & Food Centre:** a neighbourhood hawker centre near Buona Vista, where locals go for chwee kueh and other breakfast-to-dinner standards

Hawker stalls often accept cash or a local QR payment only. If you plan on a hawker meal, carry some Singapore dollars.

## Where tickets and details live

Dates, seating plans and the official ticket links for both Singapore nights are published through the tour's official channels and the authorised ticketing partner for The Star Theatre. Resale listings on social media are a known source of scams in Singapore. Fans who buy secondhand usually stick to the authorised platform's resale options.

Door times, support acts and the age policy are set by the promoter for each night. They can differ between the November and December shows.

## Staying nearby

Buona Vista is a business and residential district, so it has few hotels within walking distance. Most visitors stay in the city centre or around Orchard and ride the East-West Line out. That journey is easy in both directions.

If you're in town for both dates, Holland Village makes a relaxed base. It has cafés, bars and the Circle Line on the doorstep.
