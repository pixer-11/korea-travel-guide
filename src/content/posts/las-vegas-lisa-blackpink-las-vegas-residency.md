## Two nights at The Colosseum

Both residency dates fall on the same weekend. The shows take place on Friday, November 13 and Saturday, November 14, 2026, at The Colosseum inside Caesars Palace.

## Getting to Caesars Palace

Caesars Palace sits at the corner of Las Vegas Boulevard and Flamingo Road, in the middle of the Strip. The ways in:

- **On foot:** the Bellagio, The LINQ, Paris and The Cromwell are all a short walk away along the Strip.
- **Las Vegas Monorail:** Flamingo/Caesars Palace station is across the boulevard. It connects to the convention-centre end of the Strip and to MGM Grand at the south end.
- **Rideshare and taxi:** Caesars has signposted pickup and drop-off areas. On show nights, demand there peaks the moment the theatre empties.
- **Driving:** the resort has a self-parking garage and valet. Current rates are posted at the garage entrances.

From the airport, the drive to the central Strip is usually short. Many fans flying in for these dates take a taxi or rideshare and skip a rental car altogether.
