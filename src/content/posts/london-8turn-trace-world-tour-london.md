## Eight members, one compact hall

TRACE is 8TURN's second world tour. The group debuted in 2023 under MNH Entertainment, and the London stop takes place in November 2026 at Islington Assembly Hall. On the tour map, that makes London one of the smaller rooms.

The hall holds well under a thousand people. Nobody stands far from the stage, and the back of the floor still gets a clear view of choreography built for eight. For a group touring Europe at this stage of its career, that closeness is the main draw.

## Getting to Upper Street

The venue sits inside Islington Town Hall on Upper Street, N1, with its own entrance beside the town hall's main front. Getting there:

- **Highbury & Islington** (Victoria line, London Overground): about ten minutes' walk south down Upper Street
- **Angel** (Northern line): about fifteen minutes' walk north, past the shops and restaurants of Upper Street
- **Buses**: several routes run the length of Upper Street and stop close to the town hall

Islington has no event car parks and street parking is tightly controlled, so concertgoers mostly arrive by Tube or bus. After the show, the Victoria line from Highbury & Islington gets you back to King's Cross St Pancras and Oxford Circus within minutes.

## Inside Islington Assembly Hall

The hall dates from the 1930s. It keeps its Art Deco detailing: a decorated ceiling, a raised stage at one end and a balcony around the room. Most of the space is a flat standing floor. The balcony gives a raised view, which matters for shorter fans when the floor is packed.

Upper Street is one of north London's busiest eating streets. Pubs, ramen bars and chain restaurants line it from Angel to Highbury Corner, so attendees usually eat there before doors. Visitors from outside London often stay near King's Cross, two stops away on the Victoria line.
