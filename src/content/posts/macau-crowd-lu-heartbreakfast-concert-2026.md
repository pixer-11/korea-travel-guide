## Three nights of Crowd Lu on the Cotai Strip

The tour carries his breakfast-shop personality to Macau. Taiwanese singer-songwriter Crowd Lu (盧廣仲) performs his HeartBreakFast Concert 2026 ver. at The Venetian Arena on December 4, 5 and 6, 2026. Three nights in one city makes this a substantial stop on the run.

He is known for his acoustic guitar, his wide grin and plenty of chatter between songs, and he is a Golden Melody Award winner. He also acted in the TV drama *The Boy Who Lost His Way* (花甲男孩轉大人), whose theme "Fish" (魚仔) became one of his best-loved ballads. He sings and talks in Mandarin.

## Inside The Venetian Arena

The arena sits inside The Venetian Macao, the canal-and-gondola resort on the Cotai Strip in Taipa. It was built for concerts and sport and seats roughly 15,000. The seating is a steep bowl, so the upper tiers still look straight down at the stage.

The way in runs through the resort's casino floor and shopping mall. That walk from the hotel entrances takes longer than most first-timers expect. Concertgoers usually allow 15–20 minutes to get from the lobby to their seat.

Nearby for before or after the show:
- **Grand Canal Shoppes**: painted-sky indoor canals with restaurants on the same level as the arena
- **The Londoner and The Parisian Macao**: next door, with the half-scale Eiffel Tower
- **Taipa Village and Rua do Cunha**: about 15 minutes' walk north, known for pork chop buns and almond cookies

## Getting to Cotai

Most people get there on the Venetian's free shuttles. They have long run from:
- Macau Outer Harbour Ferry Terminal
- Taipa Ferry Terminal
- Hong Kong–Zhuhai–Macau Bridge Macau Port
- Border Gate (Portas do Cerco), coming from Zhuhai
- Macau International Airport

The Macau Light Rail Transit Taipa Line also stops on the Cotai Strip, and Cotai West station is the closest. The taxi queue at the Venetian's main entrance gets very long after a sell-out show. Plenty of fans walk to a neighbouring resort's taxi rank or ride the LRT out instead.

Hong Kong fans often come on a TurboJET or Cotai Water Jet ferry and stay the night, because the last boats sail not long after the encore. Rooms across Cotai fill fast on a three-night concert weekend.

## Show-night habits in a Macau arena

Pay the way locals do. The pataca (MOP) is the official currency, but Hong Kong dollars are accepted almost everywhere at roughly par. You get change in patacas, though. Inside the resort, cards and mobile wallets such as MPay, Alipay and WeChat Pay are widely taken.

Language is mixed. Locals speak Cantonese and Crowd Lu sings in Mandarin, and front-of-house staff use Cantonese, Mandarin and English. Many fans learn the choruses of "I Love You" (我愛你) and "Fish" beforehand, because sing-alongs are a big part of his shows.

The usual arena rules apply. Professional cameras and selfie sticks are generally not allowed past security, and ushers stop people filming on long lenses. Sands venues in Cotai typically sell tickets through Cotai Ticketing, and Crowd Lu's official channels post the tour stops.

Merchandise booths sit in the arena concourse. Fans usually reach them before doors open, since the lines grow at the interval and after the encore.
