## Twenty years from Pinoy Dream Academy

The title says it straight: *biyahe* is "journey" and *bente* is "twenty". Yeng Constantino won the first season of ABS-CBN's Pinoy Dream Academy in 2006, and she is one of the rare reality-show winners who went on to write her own hits. "Salamat", "Hawak Kamay", "Jeepney Love Story" and "Ikaw" are all hers.

That songbook shapes the room. "Hawak Kamay" is a hand-holding anthem, while "Ikaw" became a staple at Filipino weddings. Crowds at her shows tend to sing whole choruses back without being prompted. The setlist itself is not part of the published facts, so expect nothing in particular beyond an anniversary show built on two decades of material.

## Three nights in Santa Rosa, Laguna

The concert runs from December 4 to 6, 2026, which is Friday to Sunday. Santa Rosa is a city in Laguna, roughly 40km south of Metro Manila along the South Luzon Expressway (SLEX). It is home to the Nuvali development and Enchanted Kingdom theme park.

Early December is the start of the dry, cooler months on Luzon. That makes evenings outdoors and the drive down more comfortable than in typhoon season. It is also peak Christmas season, with malls fully decorated and traffic heavier than usual.

## Getting down the SLEX

Most concertgoers come from Manila by road:

- **Car or Grab:** SLEX south to the Santa Rosa or Eton City exits. Friday-evening southbound traffic out of Makati and Alabang is notoriously slow.
- **Bus:** provincial and city buses bound for Laguna leave from terminals in Buendia (Makati) and Alabang, stopping along the Santa Rosa stretch of the national road and expressway.
- **Staying over:** fans from farther afield usually book hotels around Nuvali or Paseo de Santa Rosa, which cuts out the late-night drive back north.

Late on Friday, ride-hailing pickups around Santa Rosa thin out. Groups often arrange their return ride in advance or share a car.

## Concert-night habits of Yeng's crowd

Filipino concert crowds are participatory. A few things fans usually do:

- **Sing, loudly.** Full-voice singalongs are expected, not tolerated. Tagalog choruses carry the night.
- **Bring signs and lights.** Handmade placards and phone flashlights are standard during ballads such as "Ikaw".
- **Pay digitally.** GCash and Maya are widely accepted at food stalls and nearby malls. Small cash notes help with tricycles and vendors outside.
- **Skip the tip worry.** Tipping is not expected at merch tables or food kiosks.

Because there are three nights, the Sunday show draws families and out-of-town fans who make a weekend of it. Fans heading back to Manila tend to leave the Friday show promptly to beat the SLEX crawl. The organiser publishes dates, venue details and ticket links on its official channels, and those are the reference for seating and entry rules.
