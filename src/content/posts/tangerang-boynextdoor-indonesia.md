## KNOCK ON Vol.2 comes to BSD

The show is on December 5, 2026. BOYNEXTDOOR, the six-member group from KOZ Entertainment under HYBE, plays Indonesia Convention Exhibition in Tangerang as part of its 'KNOCK ON Vol.2' tour. The members are Sungho, Riwoo, Jaehyun, Taesan, Leehan and Woonhak.

The group debuted in 2023 and became known for tracks like "But Sometimes", "Earth, Wind & Fire" and "Nice Guy". The first KNOCK ON tour came before this one. Volume 2 brings the format back to a hall built for trade fairs, not a stadium.

## Inside Indonesia Convention Exhibition

ICE is among the largest exhibition centres in Indonesia. It is a long, low complex of column-free halls with a wide pre-function corridor and big glass entrances facing the car parks. Concerts here use one or more halls set up as standing pens and seated blocks on a flat floor. That makes your section matter more than it would in a raked arena.

Around the venue:
- **AEON Mall BSD City** is a short ride away, with food courts and Japanese chains that fans use before and after the show.
- **The Breeze BSD** is an open-air lifestyle strip with cafés and restaurants.
- **Hotels in BSD City**, some walkable to ICE, are where out-of-town fans usually stay. Staying there avoids the late-night trip back into central Jakarta.

## Reaching ICE from Jakarta

You can come by train, by car or from the airport.

- **KRL Commuter Line:** the Rangkasbitung line runs from Tanah Abang to **Cisauk** and **Rawa Buntu**. Cisauk connects to BSD's Intermoda transit hub, and from there feeder buses and short ride-hail trips cover the last stretch to ICE.
- **By car or ride-hail:** the Jakarta–Serpong toll road is the direct route. On a Saturday evening, Jakarta traffic can double the journey time.
- **From Soekarno–Hatta Airport:** a taxi or Grab across the toll network is usually the simplest way. BSD is closer to the airport than central Jakarta is.

After the encore, ride-hail pickup zones get crowded and prices surge. Many fans walk to a nearby mall or hotel lobby and order their car from there, or take the train back if it is still running.

## How ONEDOOR does a show day

Indonesian K-pop crowds are organised, and the habits are predictable.

- **Tickets and ID:** Indonesian promoters often tie tickets to the buyer's identity and swap e-tickets for wristbands at a redemption point. Fans usually carry the ID used at purchase. The organiser publishes the ticketing platform and entry rules on its official channels.
- **Freebies:** fans (ONEDOOR) hand out homemade photocards, stickers and snacks outside the halls, often coordinated through X and Instagram fan accounts. A small stack of your own to trade gets you into the swapping.
- **Paying:** merch booths and food stalls widely take QRIS, so GoPay, OVO, DANA and banking apps all work. Cards are common at the malls nearby.
- **Lightsticks:** the official BOYNEXTDOOR lightstick is standard kit. It often syncs to the show's lighting through an app.
- **Queues:** standing-pen entry is usually called by queue number printed on the ticket, so fans wait in numbered lines rather than one crowd.

December falls in West Java's rainy season, and afternoon downpours are common. A light rain jacket or folding umbrella is useful for the wait outside the halls.
