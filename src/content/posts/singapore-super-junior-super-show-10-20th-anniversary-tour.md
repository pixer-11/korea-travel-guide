## Two nights in Kallang

Super Junior open 2027 in Singapore. The 'Super Show 10' tour marks 20 years since the group debuted under SM Entertainment in 2005. It plays the Singapore Indoor Stadium on 2 and 3 January 2027.

Super Show is the group's own concert series and has run since 2008. Every edition tours Asia as a full production, and fans travel across the region to see it. Two Singapore dates usually means a share of the crowd flies in from Malaysia, Indonesia and further away.

The organiser publishes dates, seating plans and ticket links through its official channels. Prices, set times and the performing line-up are not covered here.

## The stadium and the walk in

The Singapore Indoor Stadium is the silver, pagoda-roofed arena designed by Kenzo Tange. It opened in 1989 and sits inside the Singapore Sports Hub, beside the National Stadium's dome and the Kallang Basin waterfront.

Getting there by MRT:

- **Stadium (CC6, Circle Line):** the closest station, a short covered walk to the arena.
- **Kallang (EW10, East-West Line):** roughly a 15-minute walk across the Sports Hub grounds.
- **Mountbatten (CC7, Circle Line):** one stop on from Stadium. Fans often walk there after the show to skip the platform queue.

Taxis and Grab cars use the Sports Hub drop-off points. After the show, ride-hail surge pricing and waiting times both climb sharply.

## Blue ocean etiquette

Super Junior fans call themselves E.L.F., and the official colour is pearl sapphire blue. On both nights the stands fill with the group's official light stick, so a light stick is the one accessory most attendees bring.

How the room usually works:

- **Fanchants:** Korean fan cheers are shouted in set places within the songs. Regulars learn the chants for the hits beforehand and the whole arena calls them together.
- **Paying for the MRT:** Singapore's MRT gates take contactless Visa and Mastercard cards and mobile wallets, so visitors rarely buy a separate travel card.
- **Merchandise:** official merch booths at stadium concerts draw long lines on show day. Many fans buy on the afternoon of the first night and leave the second night free.
- **Fan sharing:** fans often hand out free photo cards, stickers or banners outside the gates. A small stash of your own to give in return is part of the custom.

Phone filming rules vary by tour. Arena staff enforce what the organiser sets, and the rule is shown at the entrances.

## Eating, staying, and January weather

Kallang Wave Mall connects to the Indoor Stadium and has food courts, fast food and a supermarket. On show nights it doubles as the waiting room. Leisure Park Kallang, a short walk along the water, has more restaurants and is usually calmer.

Visitors from overseas tend to stay one of two ways. Some stay in the city centre around Bugis or City Hall, a few Circle Line or East-West Line stops away. Others book near Kallang and Lavender, then walk home after the show.

January falls in Singapore's northeast monsoon season, when afternoon and evening downpours are common. Fans queuing outdoors usually carry a compact umbrella or a poncho. Inside, the stadium is air-conditioned and can feel cold after a humid wait, so a light layer helps.
