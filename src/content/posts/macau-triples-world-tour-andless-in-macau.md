## Finding Broadway Macau

Broadway Macau is the lower-key resort on the eastern side of the Galaxy Macau complex in Cotai. It sits on the reclaimed strip between Taipa and Coloane. The theatre is indoors, past the resort's lobby.

Typical routes in:
- **From Hong Kong by ferry:** TurboJET or Cotai Water Jet to the Taipa Ferry Terminal, then a taxi or resort shuttle to Cotai.
- **From Hong Kong by road:** cross-border buses over the Hong Kong–Zhuhai–Macao Bridge, then a shuttle or taxi from the Macau port.
- **From mainland China:** Border Gate (Gongbei) or the Lotus Bridge crossing at Hengqin, which is the closest to Cotai.
- **Within Macau:** casino shuttle buses run between the ferry terminals, the border crossings and the big Cotai resorts, and Galaxy's own stop is a short walk from Broadway.

After the show, taxi queues at Cotai resorts get long. Many fans walk through to Galaxy's main transport area instead.

## Before and after on the Broadway side

Broadway Food Street runs along the resort's ground floor. It's an open-fronted strip of Macanese and Hong Kong–style eateries, the cheapest sit-down option in this part of Cotai. Fans waiting for the show usually end up there.

Taipa Village is a short taxi ride north. Its lanes of Portuguese-era houses around Rua do Cunha sell pork chop buns, almond cookies and egg tarts. For something bigger, Galaxy's malls and the rest of the Cotai Strip are next door.

Hotels across Cotai and Taipa fill up on concert weekends. Visitors from Hong Kong often do the show as a day trip and take a late ferry home. Many fans also stay overnight so they aren't racing the last sailings.
