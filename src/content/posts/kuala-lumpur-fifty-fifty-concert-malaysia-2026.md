## Inside a Zepp hall

Zepp is a Japanese chain of live houses. The Kuala Lumpur branch follows the same idea as its sisters in Tokyo and Osaka: a wide, flat main floor in front of a raised stage, with a smaller tiered section behind and above.

That layout matters. On the floor you're standing shoulder to shoulder, close enough to see choreography without leaning on the screens. From the upper level you get a clean, full view of formations, which suits anyone who wants to film the dance breaks for memory rather than fight for space.

The room is compact compared with Axiata Arena or Stadium Merdeka. Even the back rows feel near the stage.
