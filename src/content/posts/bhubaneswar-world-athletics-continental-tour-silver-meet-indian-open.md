## Why this meet matters

Bhubaneswar has spent the last decade building a reputation as India's most credible host city for elite sport, and the Indian Open was its latest proof point. After hosting a Continental Tour bronze-level meet at Kalinga Stadium in 2025, Bhubaneswar was upgraded to Silver status for 2026 — putting it a tier above most regional meets on World Athletics' global circuit and a notch below Diamond League events.

A Silver designation typically draws a stronger international start list, more world-ranking points on the line, and tighter competition standards, meaning athletes chasing qualification marks for global championships alongside strong Indian national talent.

## Getting there

Kalinga Stadium sits in the Kalinga Nagar/Nayapalli area of Bhubaneswar, a few kilometers from the city center and roughly 15–20 minutes by road from Biju Patnaik International Airport (BBI), which has direct connections to Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, and other major Indian hubs. Bhubaneswar Railway Station, on major east-coast rail lines, is a similar distance away. Autorickshaws and app-based cabs (Ola, Uber) were the easiest way to reach the stadium.

## What to expect at Kalinga Stadium

Kalinga Stadium is Odisha's flagship multi-sport venue, previously used for the Hockey World Cup and the Indian Grand Prix athletics meets. Its athletics stretch includes a synthetic track and stands that regularly host domestic and international competitions, so the infrastructure was well-tested for exactly this kind of event. For a Continental Tour meet, the program usually runs across a single day with heats or field events in the afternoon building toward marquee finals — sprints, middle-distance races, and jumps/throws — in the evening session, when temperatures drop and crowds peak.

## When to go

August in Bhubaneswar is deep in the monsoon season — hot, humid, and prone to sudden showers, so light breathable clothing, a compact umbrella or rain shell, and water were sensible things to bring. Evening sessions are more comfortable than afternoon ones. This was the city's first Silver-level meet, and logistics such as gate opening times and security screening could differ slightly from previous bronze-level editions.

## Beyond the track

Bhubaneswar, the "Temple City," rewards a day or two either side of the meet. The 11th-century Lingaraj Temple and the nearby Mukteshwar Temple are architectural highlights of Odisha's distinctive temple style. The Khandagiri and Udayagiri Caves, ancient Jain rock-cut shelters, sit on the city's western edge, and the Odisha State Museum gives useful context on regional history and tribal art. Foodies could seek out Odia thali spreads and local sweets like chhena poda and rasabali, widely available in the city's central Unit markets.

## How to visit like a local

Third-party resellers around venue gates are common at big Indian sporting events and not reliable for authentic seating. Locals attending big meets at Kalinga Stadium typically arrive well before the marquee evening finals to avoid the bottleneck at security checkpoints, then settle in with snacks bought outside rather than expecting extensive concessions inside.

Cash and UPI mobile payments are both widely used in Bhubaneswar, though small cash was useful for auto fares and street food stalls near the stadium, where digital payment isn't always accepted. Approach roads are usually restricted for major events, meaning a short walk from the drop-off point to the gate.
