## Why go

La Vie by Thomas Bühner is the Taipei outpost of German chef Thomas Bühner, whose culinary reputation was built on precise, ingredient-forward cooking that strips dishes down to their essential flavors — what's often described as an "elemental" approach to tasting menus. The restaurant carries one Michelin star and was recently named Best Restaurant in Taiwan 2026 by Savour BlackBookAsia, a signal that it's become one of the city's most talked-about fine-dining tables. For visitors who want to understand where Taipei's high-end dining scene is heading — beyond the beef noodle soup and night market stalls — this is a place to see European technique applied with local ingredients and sensibility.

## Where it is and how to get there

The restaurant sits inside NOKE Mall in Zhongshan District, one of Taipei's more design-forward commercial and dining neighborhoods, known for boutique shopping and a dense cluster of cafes and restaurants. Zhongshan is well served by the Taipei Metro — the Zhongshan Station on the Red (Tamsui-Xinyi) and Green (Songshan-Xindian) lines, and the nearby Shuanglian Station on the Red Line, both put you within a short walk or taxi ride of the mall. Because La Vie is embedded in a shopping complex rather than a standalone shopfront, it's worth building in a few extra minutes to locate the correct entrance and floor once inside — mall directories or ground-floor information desks can point you the right way.

## When to go

Fine dining rooms inside malls tend to sync loosely with the mall's own operating rhythm, but restaurants of La Vie's caliber usually run dinner service and sometimes a lunch seating on select days. Given its recent "Best Restaurant in Taiwan 2026" recognition, demand has likely spiked, so early evening reservations and weekday visits are generally easier to secure than Friday or Saturday night slots. As with any tasting-menu restaurant, plan for the meal to run long — two to three hours is typical for this style of dining — so avoid scheduling anything immediately afterward.
