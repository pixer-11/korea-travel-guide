## The venue: Palacio Vistalegre Arena

Vistalegre started life as a bullring in the 1940s, and the round brick shell still gives the interior its shape. Promoters converted it decades ago into one of Madrid's main indoor arenas, used for concerts, boxing and the occasional circus run.

The bowl seats several thousand and the acoustics favour loud guitar bands more than they do orchestral acts, which suits Evanescence's mix of symphonic rock and metal riffs fine.

Expect a general floor area in front of the stage for standing tickets, with tiered seating rising around it. If you want to see Amy Lee's face rather than a screen, floor or lower-tier seats close to the stage matter more here than in a stadium show.
