## Why this show mattered

Southeast Asia had waited a long time for a Weeknd stadium date, and Jakarta got first crack at it. The AFTER HOURS TIL DAWN tour was built around the neon-lit, cinematic production the singer debuted on the North American and European legs, a show that leaned hard into the After Hours/Dawn FM aesthetic of red suits, mirrored stages, and a set list spanning "Blinding Lights," "Save Your Tears," and deep cuts from across his catalogue.

Two nights at Jakarta International Stadium signalled that the promoters expected serious demand, and it also gave fans a rare option: if tickets for one date were out of reach, there was a second show the following evening.

## Getting to Jakarta International Stadium

JIS sits in Tanjung Priok, North Jakarta, a purpose-built stadium that has become the city's go-to venue for events at this scale. It's not in the central tourist core, so the journey takes real time. The stadium is served by TransJakarta bus corridors running toward Tanjung Priok, and ride-hailing apps (Gojek, Grab) are the most straightforward way in for most visitors.

From South or Central Jakarta (Senayan, SCBD, Menteng), the trip runs 45 minutes to over an hour each way depending on the hour and whether it's a show night.

## What to expect inside

Stadium shows of this size in Jakarta typically split the floor into standing pens closest to the stage, with tiered seating rising around them. VIP packages, early entry, and merchandise stands were part of the tour's usual setup.

## When to arrive and how to pace two nights

The Jakarta booking was two full shows on consecutive nights, September 26–27, rather than one headline date with a spare. Set lists on this tour had varied city to city, and promoters often stagger crowd flow across two-night runs to ease the crush.

For anyone flying in internationally, Jakarta's Soekarno-Hatta Airport (CGK) is roughly 90 minutes from JIS in ordinary traffic, longer during evening rush.

## Where to stay and eat nearby

Tanjung Priok itself is a working port district, light on hotels and dedicated tourist dining, so visitors to shows there tend to base themselves in Central Jakarta or around Kelapa Gading, a mall-and-residential area roughly 20–30 minutes from the stadium with a dense concentration of restaurants, hotels, and 24-hour convenience stores. Kelapa Gading also works as a fallback for pre-show dinner away from stadium-adjacent crowds.

## How to visit like a local

Jakartans navigating a stadium show this size tend to lean almost entirely on Gojek/Grab and TransJakarta rather than driving themselves, since parking near JIS becomes chaotic on event nights. Cashless payment is the norm at concessions and merch tables across Jakarta, with a local e-wallet (GoPay, OVO) or a card working better than cash.

A common mistake international fans make at Jakarta stadium shows is underestimating the exit crush afterward, since tens of thousands of people converge on the same few roads simultaneously; locals typically linger 20–30 minutes inside after the final song, grabbing merch or letting the crowd thin, rather than sprinting for the gate. Indonesian concert crowds are also known for queueing at entry gates hours before doors.
