## Getting to Jakarta International Stadium

JIS sits in Tanjung Priok, North Jakarta, a purpose-built stadium that's become the city's go-to venue for events at this scale. It's not in the central tourist core, so budget real time for the trip. The stadium is served by TransJakarta bus corridors running toward Tanjung Priok, and ride-hailing apps (Gojek, Grab) are the most straightforward way in for most visitors — just expect surge pricing and gridlock in the hours before doors open, especially with two consecutive sold-out-scale nights stacking traffic on top of itself.
