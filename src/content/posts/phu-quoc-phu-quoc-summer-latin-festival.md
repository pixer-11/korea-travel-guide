## What the festival is

Four days of Latin dance on Phu Quoc island: October 29 to November 1, 2026. The centerpiece is the Bachata Champion J&J Competition, a partnered "Jack and Jill" bachata contest where dancers draw random partners on the spot. Around it sit workshops, beach parties and live performances. The whole event is based at Mercury Resort and Villas, a beachfront property on the island rather than in a downtown convention hall.

## Venue and getting there

Mercury Resort and Villas sits on Phu Quoc's coast, so festival days mix indoor studio sessions with outdoor beach parties. Phu Quoc International Airport (PQC) connects to Ho Chi Minh City, Hanoi and a growing list of regional Asian cities, with a taxi or resort transfer needed to reach Mercury from the terminal. Book accommodation on or near the resort early: late October falls in Phu Quoc's rainy season, and rooms near an active festival venue sell out faster than the general island average.

- Airport: Phu Quoc International (PQC)
- Venue: Mercury Resort and Villas
- Dates: October 29 to November 1, 2026
- Format: competition, workshops, beach parties, live shows

## Tickets and schedule

No prices or set times are published here because organizers adjust workshop tracks and party schedules between announcements. Confirm timing and tickets on the official Phu Quoc Summer Latin Festival source, and check whether the J&J competition requires separate registration from general festival admission. Dancers planning to compete should also verify partner-rotation rules and any level restrictions directly with organizers, since Jack and Jill formats often gate entry by skill level.

## Making the most of four days

This is a dance-first crowd, not a passive-spectator event, so pack shoes suited to both a studio floor and sand.

- Bring both indoor dance shoes and something grippy for beach-party sets
- Sign up for workshops early if slots are capped by level (beginner, intermediate, advanced)
- Watch the J&J competition rounds even if not competing; partner-draw formats move fast and reward front-row viewing
- Pace yourself across four days: competitions, workshops and parties often overlap in the evening

Phu Quoc's late-October weather runs warm and humid with a real chance of rain, so pack light, breathable layers and a rain shell for the beach sessions rather than relying on the resort's coverage alone.
