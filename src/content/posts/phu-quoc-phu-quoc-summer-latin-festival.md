## What the festival is

The 2026 festival was planned as four days of Latin dance on Phu Quoc island, from October 29 to November 1. Its centrepiece was the Bachata Champion J&J Competition, a "Jack and Jill" bachata contest, a format in which dancers draw random partners on the spot. Around it, the programme included workshops, beach parties and live performances. The whole event was based at Mercury Resort and Villas, a beachfront property on the island rather than a downtown convention hall.

## Venue and getting there

Mercury Resort and Villas sits on Phu Quoc's coast, so a festival there mixes indoor studio sessions with outdoor beach parties. Phu Quoc International Airport (PQC) connects to Ho Chi Minh City, Hanoi and a growing list of regional Asian cities, and a taxi or resort transfer covers the trip from the terminal to Mercury. Late October falls in Phu Quoc's rainy season, and rooms near an active festival venue tend to fill faster than the general island average.

## Making the most of four days

Latin dance festivals like this draw a dance-first crowd rather than passive spectators, and the mix of studio floor and sand shapes what dancers bring.

- Indoor dance shoes suit studio sessions, while something grippy works better for beach-party sets
- Workshops at such festivals are often capped by level (beginner, intermediate, advanced)
- J&J competition rounds move fast, and partner-draw formats reward front-row viewing
- Over four days, competitions, workshops and parties often overlap in the evening

Phu Quoc's late-October weather is warm and humid with a real chance of rain. Light, breathable layers and a rain shell suit beach sessions better than relying on a resort's coverage alone.
