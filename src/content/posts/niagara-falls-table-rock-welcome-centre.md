## The railing at the edge

The river drops away a few metres from your shoes. Table Rock Welcome Centre stands at 6650 Niagara River Pkwy, where the Canadian shoreline meets the curve of the Horseshoe Falls. The stone wall along the promenade is as close as the public can get to the falls on foot. Spray drifts over it depending on the wind.

The name comes from a flat rock shelf that once stuck out over the gorge. It has since broken away. Today the complex holds a retail mall and an indoor observation area on several levels. Outdoor overlooks run along the front.

## What's inside the building

The centre works as a base for the falls. Inside you'll find:

- **Journey Behind the Falls:** an elevator takes you down through the bedrock to tunnels, viewing portals behind the falling water and a lower deck at the base. Ponchos are handed out.
- **Indoor viewing levels:** you see the falls through glass, so this is where to go in rain or a Niagara winter.
- **Shops and food:** souvenir halls and casual dining, run by Niagara Parks.

Give it 1–2 hours with Journey Behind the Falls. Allow about 30 minutes if you only want the railing and a look around.

## Getting there and moving on

Stay on foot if you can. The riverside promenade runs north from Table Rock through Queen Victoria Park to Clifton Hill and the Rainbow Bridge. It's a flat walk of roughly 1.5 km with the American Falls in view most of the way.

- **From Fallsview hotels:** the Falls Incline Railway runs down the escarpment from Portage Road to the Table Rock area.
- **By bus:** Niagara Parks' WEGO buses stop at Table Rock. Check the current routes before you go.
- **By car:** Niagara Parks runs parking lots along the Parkway. Rates are posted at each lot entrance.

## Working the Table Rock crowd

Table Rock is well known and busy. Nobody gets the railing to themselves. Here's what helps:

- **Go early in the morning or after dark.** Google lists the grounds as open around the clock at the moment. Tour groups arrive mid-morning and stay through the afternoon, so the edges of the day usually give you more room. The outdoor overlook is the part that stays open late. Shop and Journey Behind the Falls hours change through the year, so check Niagara Parks' official site for this month's times.
- **Wait for the evening lights.** The falls are lit in colour every night. In summer there are often fireworks too; dates are on the Niagara Parks calendar.
- **Watch the wind.** On a breezy day the mist from the Horseshoe Falls soaks the railing. Put your phone in a zip bag, or stand further back along the wall.
- **Pay by card.** Canadians pay by tap almost everywhere. If you get table service in the restaurant, a tip of 15–20% is normal.
- **Book Journey Behind the Falls ahead.** Timed tickets can sell out on summer weekends. Prices are on the Niagara Parks website.
