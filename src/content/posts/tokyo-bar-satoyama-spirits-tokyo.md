## What makes this bar different

Bar Satoyama Spirits skips the usual back-bar of imported gin and bourbon entirely. Every pour is built from botanicals sourced through the Japan Botanical Research Institute: things foraged from Japanese woodland rather than shipped from Scotland or Kentucky.

"Satoyama" refers to the traditional buffer zone between mountain forest and farmed lowland, the managed woodland Japanese villages have drawn on for centuries. The name signals the concept directly: this is a bar treating forest ingredients as spirits categories in their own right, not garnish.

The room seats 30, small enough that a handful of walk-ins can fill it on a busy night. It opened in August 2026, so it's still finding its rhythm.

## Where it sits near Ebisu

The bar is close to Ebisu Station, served by the JR Yamanote and Saikyo lines plus the Hibiya Metro line. Ebisu itself is a drinking neighborhood already, dense with small bars, yakitori counters and the elevated Ebisu Garden Place complex nearby.

That means Satoyama Spirits doesn't have to be a destination on its own; it fits naturally into a night of bar-hopping through the backstreets north and west of the station. Expect a mix of curious locals and industry regulars checking out a genuinely new concept rather than another gin bar.
