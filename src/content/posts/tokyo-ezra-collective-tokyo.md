## What's happening

Ezra Collective, the Mercury Prize-winning London jazz quintet, play their first ever Japan headline show on October 13, 2026. The venue is Toyosu Pit, a mid-sized standing hall in Tokyo's Koto ward built for exactly this kind of high-energy live set.

The band's sound mixes Afrobeat, hip-hop and jazz, and their live shows lean loud and physical rather than seated and reserved. Toyosu Pit's flat, open floor suits that: expect a standing crowd, not rows of chairs.

This is billed as a debut Japan date, so there's no back catalogue of past Tokyo shows to compare it to. Treat it as a one-off rather than a stop on a recurring tour.

## Toyosu Pit: getting there and getting in

Toyosu Pit is in Koto ward, on Tokyo Bay's reclaimed waterfront, inside the Toyosu area redeveloped from old gasworks land into offices, apartments and event space.

- Nearest station: Shin-Toyosu, on the Yurikamome automated line
- Walk from station to venue: roughly 5–7 minutes, mostly flat and covered
- Alternative route: Toyosu Station on the Yurakucho line, then a longer walk or short bus/taxi hop
- The hall is a converted warehouse-style shed: concrete floor, high ceiling, no fixed seating for standing shows

Yurikamome trains run from Shimbashi and connect to the JR and Ginza lines there, so most visitors coming from central Tokyo hotels change at Shimbashi.

## Tickets and pricing

Exact ticket tiers and prices for this show aren't listed here because they shift by seller and change over time. Japanese club and arena shows commonly separate standing floor tickets from any elevated or seated area, when one exists.

International fans often buy through official overseas ticket agents or resale-checked platforms rather than Japanese domestic sites that require a local address or phone number. Some Japanese venues also run a numbered-entry system for standing tickets, where your ticket number determines when you're let onto the floor, not first-come-first-served at the door.

Confirm exact dates, ticket tiers, and on-sale details on the official Ezra Collective or Toyosu Pit channels before making plans. Don't rely on secondary resale listings for the base price.

## Making a night of it around Toyosu

Toyosu itself is a modern, low-rise district built around a fish and produce market relocation, waterfront parks and shopping complexes, so it's quieter and more spread out than central Tokyo nightlife zones.

- Toyosu Market: the wholesale fish market that replaced Tsukiji's outer wholesale functions, open mornings, worth an early visit the same day if you're an early riser
- LaLaport Toyosu: a large shopping and dining complex a short walk from the station, useful for a pre-show meal
- Harumi and Odaiba: connected by bridges and short rides, worth a wander if you arrive in Tokyo a day early

Trains on the Yurikamome stop running late at night, well before typical festival curfews elsewhere, so check the last train time before the show rather than after. A taxi back toward central Tokyo is a reliable backup if you cut it close.
