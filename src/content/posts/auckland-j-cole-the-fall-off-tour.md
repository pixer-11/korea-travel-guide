## One Saturday night at Quay Park

J. Cole's Auckland date is a single night. The Fall-Off Tour plays Spark Arena on Saturday, December 5, 2026, with the city in early summer and the evenings stretching long and light.

It sits on reclaimed land at Quay Park, between the port's container cranes and the slope up to Parnell. Most of the country's large touring hip-hop and pop shows come through this room.

## Reaching Spark Arena from the city

The arena's street address is Mahuhu Crescent, and the easiest way in is on foot from downtown.

- **Ferry:** Boats from Devonport, Waiheke and the other harbour routes dock at the Downtown Ferry Terminal on Quay Street. From there it is a slightly longer walk along the waterfront.
- **Bus:** Many routes end at or pass through Britomart and the lower Queen Street area.
- **Car:** There are parking buildings around the CBD and Parnell. Arena-night traffic on Beach Road and the motorway ramps makes driving the slowest option, so most concertgoers leave the car at home.
