## What the Green Star actually means

This isn't a design gimmick or a green paint job. The MICHELIN inspectors who award it look at sourcing, waste, energy use and how a restaurant treats the people and land behind its ingredients. A Green Star sits alongside a restaurant's regular rating rather than replacing it, so Sur's inclusion says something specific about practice, not just plating.

For a visitor, the practical upshot is a menu that leans on what's genuinely in season around Taichung and central Taiwan, likely shifting through the year rather than staying fixed. Expect the kitchen to talk about where things come from if you ask; that's usually the point of a Green Star kitchen, not an afterthought.
