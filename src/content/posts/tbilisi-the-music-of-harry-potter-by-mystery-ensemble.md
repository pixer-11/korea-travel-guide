## The scores behind the programme

Eight films produced four composers' worth of music. That range shapes any evening built on the series:

- **John Williams** scored the first three films and wrote Hedwig's Theme, the series' signature.
- **Patrick Doyle** scored *Goblet of Fire*, with its Yule Ball dance music.
- **Nicholas Hooper** scored *Order of the Phoenix* and *Half-Blood Prince*.
- **Alexandre Desplat** scored both parts of *Deathly Hallows*.

Fans who know the films well tend to notice the shift in tone: the bright, Christmassy feel of the early Williams music gives way to the darker writing of the later films.

## A December concert night in Tbilisi

Tbilisi in mid-December is cold, often close to freezing after dark. Concertgoers usually come in winter coats and leave them at the cloakroom (*garderobi*). Most Tbilisi concert halls run one, and keeping a heavy coat on your lap in the seats is unusual.

Getting there and back is simple:

- **Taxi apps:** Bolt is the usual way locals and visitors get around at night, and it avoids haggling with street taxis.
- **Metro:** Tbilisi's two metro lines are cheap and fast, but it is still worth knowing the nearest station to the hall before you set out.
- **Walking:** central Tbilisi is hilly and its old pavements are uneven, so the walk back takes longer than the map suggests.

Family audiences are common at Harry Potter concerts, and some attendees come in house scarves or robes. Phones usually stay down during the music, though a quick photo during applause is normal at tribute shows. A simple *gmadlobt* (thank you) to cloakroom and door staff goes down well.

Because the concert falls on a Saturday, people often combine it with dinner beforehand. Tbilisi restaurants generally serve late, so eating after the show is just as easy. December also brings New Year decorations to the city centre. Georgian Orthodox Christmas is on January 7, so the holiday season runs well past the concert date.
