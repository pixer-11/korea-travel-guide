## Duman at JJ Arena, 28 November

Duman plays JJ Arena in Istanbul on November 28, 2026. It is a hometown show. The band formed in Istanbul in 1999 around singer and guitarist Kaan Tangöze, and it has spent more than two decades as one of the main names in Turkish rock.

The date falls on a Saturday. Fans from the wider region usually travel in for an arena show like this one. Most of them stay the weekend rather than flying home the same night.

## Moving around Istanbul on a November night

Late November in Istanbul is chilly and often wet. Most concertgoers wear a coat they're happy to carry or check. Arena floors get hot once the crowd fills in, so layers that come off easily make more sense than one heavy jacket.

## How a Duman crowd behaves

Turkish rock audiences sing loudly and in unison. Phones go up for the ballads and come down for the faster songs. Standing fans tend to hold their spot on the floor once the support act, if there is one, finishes.

Some practical habits that make the night easier:

- **Getting in:** large Istanbul arenas usually run bag and body checks at the door. Fans with small bags get through fastest.
- **Paying inside:** bars and merch stands at big venues usually take cards and contactless. Many attendees carry a little Turkish lira as well, for kiosks and taxis outside.
- **Getting home:** after the encore, the queue for taxis builds fast. Locals tend to use the BiTaksi app or walk to the nearest rail line rather than hail a cab at the door.
- **Language:** a "teşekkürler" (thank you) at the bar goes a long way. Most bar staff at arena shows speak basic English.
