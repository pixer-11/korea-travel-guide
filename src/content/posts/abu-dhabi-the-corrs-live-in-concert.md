## The venue: Etihad Arena, Yas Island

The show was set for Etihad Arena, the indoor arena on Yas Island that's become Abu Dhabi's default home for big-name touring acts since it opened in 2023. It's a purpose-built, air-conditioned venue (a relief in late September, when outdoor evening temperatures in Abu Dhabi are still firmly in the 30s°C) with tiered seating that gives most tickets a genuinely clear sightline to the stage, unlike some retrofitted convention-hall shows.

Yas Island itself is Abu Dhabi's entertainment district, so the arena sits within a short drive of Yas Mall, Ferrari World, Warner Bros. World, and a strip of waterfront restaurants and bars that fill up around big shows.

## Getting there

Yas Island is roughly a 20–30 minute drive from central Abu Dhabi and about 45–60 minutes from Dubai depending on traffic, which makes an evening trip realistic even for visitors based there. Taxis and ride-hailing apps (Careem, Uber) are the simplest way in from either city; there's no metro connection to Yas Island, so public transport isn't really an option for anyone not already staying on the island.

Etihad Arena has dedicated event parking for drivers, though arena nights on Yas regularly back up the access roads in the hour before doors.
