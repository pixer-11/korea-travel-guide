## One night, one hall, November 27

Yuuri (優里) plays AsiaWorld-Expo Hall 10 on November 27, 2026. The Japanese singer-songwriter broke through in 2020 with "Dry Flower" (ドライフラワー). It became one of the most-streamed Japanese songs of that period and gave him a big following across Asia.

"Betelgeuse" (ベテルギウス) and "Kakurenbo" (かくれんぼ) are among his best-known tracks. The material is mostly piano- and guitar-driven ballads with big, open choruses. Expect a crowd that knows the Japanese lyrics.

Hall 10 is one of the flat-floored exhibition halls in AsiaWorld-Expo's main building. It is a separate room from AsiaWorld-Arena (Hall 1). Signage inside the venue lists hall numbers in English and Chinese, and the walk from the entrance to Hall 10 runs along long, carpeted concourses.

## Getting out to Chek Lap Kok

AsiaWorld-Expo sits on reclaimed land at Chek Lap Kok, the airport island, a long way from the city's main hotel districts. These are the usual ways in:

- **Airport Express:** on event days, trains continue one stop past Airport to AsiaWorld-Expo Station, which opens onto the venue. From Hong Kong Station in Central the ride takes about half an hour.
- **Tung Chung Line:** cheaper but slower. Take it to Tung Chung, then a bus or taxi across to the airport island.
- **Taxi:** Lantau taxis are blue and urban taxis are red. Both serve the venue, though the fare from Kowloon or Hong Kong Island is high.

Octopus cards and contactless bank cards work at the MTR gates, and that includes the Airport Express.
