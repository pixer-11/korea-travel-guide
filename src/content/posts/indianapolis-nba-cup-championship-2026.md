## A title game in a 1928 college gym

The final is not in an NBA arena. On December 11, 2026, the NBA Cup Championship 2026 is held at Hinkle Fieldhouse, on Butler University's campus about five miles north of downtown Indianapolis. It is a brick barn of a building with a steel-truss roof and tall banks of windows. It has hosted Indiana basketball since 1928.

For a tournament whose first finals were staged on the Las Vegas Strip, the move is a statement. Indiana treats high school and college basketball with near-religious seriousness, and Hinkle is where much of that devotion took shape.

## How the Cup reaches December 11

The NBA launched this tournament as the In-Season Tournament in 2023, and the Los Angeles Lakers won the first title. It became the NBA Cup the following season. Group-stage games are woven into the regular-season schedule through November.

The knockout rounds come next. Quarterfinals and semifinals are played at NBA arenas in late November and early December, and the two survivors then travel to Indianapolis. The championship game sits outside the normal league standings, so the trophy is the whole point of the night.

## The Hoosiers floor

This is the section that makes this year's final different. Hinkle Fieldhouse opened as Butler Fieldhouse in 1928 and was renamed in 1966 for Tony Hinkle, the long-serving Butler coach. When it opened it was the largest basketball arena in the United States.

For decades it staged the Indiana high school state finals. In 1954, tiny Milan High School won the title here, and that game inspired the 1986 film *Hoosiers*, whose climactic scenes were shot inside the building. Oscar Robertson's Crispus Attucks teams won state championships on the same floor in the 1950s. The fieldhouse is a National Historic Landmark.

Inside, the seating rises steeply from the court, and daylight comes in through the big end windows. The roof is a vaulted span of exposed trusses. The building holds far fewer people than a modern NBA arena, so seats sit close to the floor. That scarcity is also why tickets for this final are hard to come by.
