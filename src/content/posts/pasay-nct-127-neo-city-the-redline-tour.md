## One night on the bay

The Manila stop is a single date. NCT 127 brings Neo City: The Redline, its 5th world tour, to SM Mall of Asia Arena on December 12, 2026. It is the first tour since the album BLINGY, so the setlist is built around that record and the older material behind it.

The Neo City name has run through all of the group's world tours, and Manila has hosted earlier ones. For Filipino NCTzens, a December date also means the show falls in the middle of the holiday season. The mall's Christmas displays fill the walkways that lead to the arena doors.

## Inside SM Mall of Asia Arena

The arena is a large indoor bowl. Its tiered seating rises steeply from a floor that is usually laid out as standing or seated sections for concerts. Even the upper levels look straight down at the stage, which makes the venue a regular stop for K-pop tours passing through Metro Manila.

It stands at the northern end of the Mall of Asia Complex in Pasay. SM Mall of Asia, one of the country's largest malls, is next door. A covered walk links the two, so food courts, convenience stores and restrooms are minutes from the gates.

## Reaching the Mall of Asia Complex

Most concertgoers come by car, Grab or bus, and they hit traffic around the complex on show nights. Common options:

- **Bus:** the EDSA Carousel has a Mall of Asia stop at its southern end.
- **Rail:** LRT-1 and MRT-3 meet at EDSA/Taft Avenue, and jeepneys marked "MOA" run from there along the bay.
- **From the airport:** NAIA terminals are a short taxi or Grab ride away.
- **Parking:** the mall's multi-level car parks serve the arena.

After the encore, ride-hailing queues build fast. Many fans walk to the bayfront or into the mall for an hour and book their ride once demand drops.

## Lightsticks, freebies and the fanchant

NCT concerts in Manila run on fan rituals. Here is how a show night usually goes:

- **Lightsticks:** most of the crowd holds the official NCT lightstick. At many K-pop shows these sync wirelessly to the stage lighting once paired at the venue.
- **Freebies:** fans swap self-made photocards, stickers and fans outside the arena before doors open. Fan clubs often post their giveaway points on social media.
- **Fanchants:** fans shout members' names in set sequences during songs. Many attendees learn the chants from fan-made guides beforehand.
- **Payment:** GCash and cards work across the mall, and mall ATMs cover any cash needs.

K-pop shows at the arena typically search bags at the entrance. Professional cameras and detachable-lens gear are commonly refused. Phones are generally allowed, but the fan norm is to keep them low so the people behind you can see the stage.

Many out-of-town fans stay at the hotels inside the Mall of Asia Complex or along Macapagal Boulevard. From there the arena is a walk, so you skip the post-show taxi scramble altogether. Before doors, the seawall behind SM by the Bay looks west over Manila Bay, where the sun sets.
