## The amphitheatre by the Media City lake

Dubai Media City Amphitheatre is a purpose-built outdoor stage on the edge of Dubai Media City. The setting is part of the appeal:

- **Open sky:** there is no roof, so the show happens under the Dubai night.
- **Skyline backdrop:** glass office towers of Media City and Internet City frame the stage.
- **Tiered layout:** the amphitheatre form gives raised sightlines towards the back, with a flat standing area nearer the stage.

Because it is outdoors, the weather is part of the experience. Dubai's big open-air concerts generally run in the cooler months between autumn and spring. Even then, evenings can be warm and humid by the water.

## Getting to Media City and home again

The venue sits in the Al Sufouh area, between Dubai Marina and Barsha Heights. Several routes reach it:

- **Dubai Metro:** the Red Line stops at Dubai Internet City station, a short taxi hop or a longer walk from the amphitheatre.
- **Dubai Tram:** the tram line through Al Sufouh has a Media City stop, and it connects to the Metro near the Marina.
- **Taxi and ride-hailing:** Dubai Taxi, Careem and Uber all operate here, and most attendees use one of them for at least one leg.
- **Driving:** parking around Media City is mainly office-district parking, and roads clog as crowds leave.

Metro and tram fares use a Nol card, which is sold at station machines and counters. After the encore, ride-hailing prices tend to surge and pickup points get crowded. Many fans walk towards the Marina or JBR before calling a car.

## Concert manners in Dubai

K-pop habits meet UAE law at this show. A few local norms shape the night:

- **Dress:** concert outfits are fine, but very revealing clothing in public places can draw attention under local decency rules. Most fans pick something stage-ready but covered enough for the Metro ride.
- **Alcohol:** being drunk in public is an offence in the UAE, even on the way home. Fans who drink usually keep it moderate.
- **Filming:** phones up for a chorus is normal. Filming other people up close and posting them online is a legal risk in the UAE, so most fans keep the camera on the stage.
- **Payment:** cards and phone payments are accepted almost everywhere in Dubai, including taxis and Metro top-up machines.
