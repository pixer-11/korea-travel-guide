## Why Fuga R stands out

Paris has no shortage of rooftop bars, but most cluster around the Seine, the Marais, or the grands boulevards. Fuga R breaks that pattern by planting itself above the Monceau district in the 8th arrondissement, a quieter, moneyed pocket of the city best known for Parc Monceau's classical follies and grand Haussmannian townhouses. Opened in 2026 as part of the Fuga group's expansion, the venue's headline feature is a genuine 360-degree panorama — a rarer commodity than it sounds, since most Parisian rooftops only offer a partial skyline slice. That full-circle view, paired with the group's reputation for design-forward spaces, is why it's already being talked about as one of the city's trendsetting new openings for drinks and dining.

## The neighborhood: Monceau and the 8th

The 8th arrondissement is Paris at its most polished — home to the Champs-Élysées, Parc Monceau, and a cluster of embassies and grand apartment buildings. Unlike the rooftop scene around Beaubourg or Bastille, this part of the city skews residential and refined rather than nightlife-driven, which makes Fuga R something of an outlier: a buzzy elevated bar in an area more associated with quiet gardens and antique dealers. For visitors, that's part of the appeal — you can pair a rooftop cocktail with a stroll through Parc Monceau's rotunda and faux ruins, or a wander past the Belle Époque façades on Avenue Hoche and Avenue Van Dyck beforehand.

The Monceau district is served by Métro Monceau on Line 2, which puts you within a short walk of the area's grand avenues. Sunset is the obvious draw — Paris's skyline takes on a golden cast that flatters both the Haussmannian rooftops to the east and the more modern skyline of La Défense to the west.
