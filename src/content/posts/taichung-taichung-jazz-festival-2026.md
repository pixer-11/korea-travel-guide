## Ten nights on the lawn

The stage faces open grass. Taichung Civic Square is a broad, flat plaza and lawn in Xitun District, ringed by apartment towers and shops. For the Taichung Jazz Festival it becomes an outdoor concert ground, with the audience spread across the grass in front of the stage instead of packed into rows of seats.

The 2026 edition was set for **October 16-25, 2026**. It is one of the longest-running city jazz festivals in Taiwan, organised under Taichung's cultural affairs authorities. Most years it mixes local Taiwanese bands with touring international names.

## Who is playing in 2026

The announced programme named two international trios:

- **Alfredo Rodriguez Trio** (Cuba): a piano-led trio built on Cuban rhythm and jazz improvisation.
- **Alessio Menconi Trio** (Italy): guitarist Menconi's small-group project.

Local acts filled out the bill alongside them, spread across the ten evenings of the festival.

## Getting to Civic Square

Civic Square sits on the west side of the city, close to Taiwan Boulevard. Dozens of city bus routes run along the boulevard, so the bus is the simplest option from Taichung Station or the downtown hotels. Taxis and ride-hailing apps both work well for a late finish.

From the High Speed Rail, the stop is Taichung HSR station in Wuri. From there, a taxi or a connecting bus runs into Xitun. The Calligraphy Greenway runs from the National Museum of Natural Science to the square, lined with shaded lawns and shops.

## Claiming grass the Taichung way

On a festival evening, the lawn fills from the front. Locals typically come early with a picnic mat, put down a small patch of territory, then take turns going for food. A few local habits:

- **Mats and thin sheets.** The square is mostly grass and paving, and sitting is the norm.
- **EasyCard payments.** The card pays Taichung buses and most convenience stores, including the 7-Eleven and FamilyMart branches around Civic Square.
- **Rubbish goes home.** Public bins are scarce in Taiwan. People take their trash home or sort it at official collection points, and leaving litter on the lawn is frowned on.
- **Quiet during sets.** Audiences here are relaxed, but people do listen. Chatting waits for the gaps between songs.

October is usually dry and mild in Taichung, which is why the festival is staged outdoors at this time of year. Evenings cool off, so a light layer is useful, along with a compact umbrella in case a late typhoon-season shower comes through.

Weekend nights usually draw the biggest audiences at a free-flowing city event like this one. Weekday nights tend to be easier for space on the grass. Admission rules, any ticketed or seated sections, and set times change from year to year.
