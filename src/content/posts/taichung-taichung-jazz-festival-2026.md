## Ten nights on the lawn

The stage faces open grass. Taichung Civic Square is a broad, flat plaza and lawn in Xitun District, ringed by apartment towers and shops. For the Taichung Jazz Festival it becomes an outdoor concert ground, with the audience spread across the grass in front of the stage instead of packed into rows of seats.

The 2026 edition runs **October 16-25, 2026**, as announced. It is one of the longest-running city jazz festivals in Taiwan, organised under Taichung's cultural affairs authorities. Most years it mixes local Taiwanese bands with touring international names.

## Who is playing in 2026

Two international trios are confirmed in the announced programme:

- **Alfredo Rodriguez Trio** (Cuba): a piano-led trio built on Cuban rhythm and jazz improvisation.
- **Alessio Menconi Trio** (Italy): guitarist Menconi's small-group project.

Local acts fill out the bill alongside them. Day-by-day set times and the full lineup are on the official festival site. Check there before planning around a particular performer.

## Getting to Civic Square

Civic Square sits on the west side of the city, close to Taiwan Boulevard. Dozens of city bus routes run along the boulevard, so the bus is the simplest option from Taichung Station or the downtown hotels. Taxis and ride-hailing apps both work well for the late finish.

Arriving by High Speed Rail, you get off at Taichung HSR station in Wuri. From there, take a taxi or a connecting bus into Xitun. The Calligraphy Greenway runs from the National Museum of Natural Science to the square, so you can walk the greenway's shaded lawns and shops before the music starts.

## Claiming grass the Taichung way

On a festival evening, the lawn fills from the front. Locals arrive early with a picnic mat, put down a small patch of territory, then take turns going for food. A few habits help:

- **Bring a mat or thin sheet.** The square is mostly grass and paving, and sitting is the norm.
- **Tap with an EasyCard.** It pays Taichung buses and most convenience stores, including the 7-Eleven and FamilyMart branches around Civic Square.
- **Carry your rubbish out.** Public bins are scarce in Taiwan. People take their trash home or sort it at official collection points, and leaving litter on the lawn is frowned on.
- **Keep talk low during sets.** Crowds here are relaxed, but people do listen. Save the chatting for between songs.

October is usually dry and mild in Taichung, which is why the festival takes place outdoors at this time of year. Evenings cool off, so pack a light layer, plus a compact umbrella in case a late typhoon-season shower comes through.

Weekend nights draw the biggest crowds, which is predictable for a free-flowing city event. If the act you want plays on a weekday, that is usually the easier night for space on the grass. Admission rules, any ticketed or seated sections, and set times can change from year to year, so confirm timing and tickets on the official site.
