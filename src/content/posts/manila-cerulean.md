## What Cerulean actually is

Cerulean is chef Francis Tolentino's second restaurant, arriving after his fine-dining debut Taupe. Where Taupe built his name, Cerulean narrows the focus to seafood pulled from Philippine waters. The lens is modern Southeast Asian rather than strictly Filipino, so expect technique and plating that borrow from across the region while the raw ingredient stays local.

It opened in July 2026 as part of a wave of new Manila openings that month. That timing matters: this is a restaurant still finding its rhythm, not an institution with a settled routine. Menus, hours, and service pace can shift in a new kitchen's first months.

## Why it stands out from Taupe

Tolentino built his reputation on Taupe's fine-dining format. Cerulean reads as a deliberate pivot: seafood-led, region-wide in its references, presumably more product-forward than composition-forward.
