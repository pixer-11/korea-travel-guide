## What Tribe Sky Beach Club actually is

Tribe started in Bangkok, where rooftop beach clubs are their own genre: infinity pools, day beds, DJ sets, city views stacked above traffic. Shanghai is the brand's first stop outside Thailand, landing on a mall rooftop in late 2026.

The signature feature is an infinity sky pool that appears to spill into the skyline. Around it: art installations meant to be photographed, plus a sound and lighting rig built for club-level nights, not background music.

This is a vacation-mood import grafted onto a Shanghai rooftop, not a beach club in the literal sense. There's no ocean here; the "beach" is imported atmosphere, sand-colored decking and pool culture layered onto a commercial tower.

## Where it sits and why the location matters

It occupies a rooftop above a shopping mall, the kind of vertical siting that's become standard for Shanghai's nightlife scene as ground-floor real estate gets scarcer. Rooftop venues here depend on the building beneath them for lifts, security and access hours, so the mall's own schedule will shape when you can actually get up top.

Shanghai's mall-rooftop format usually means:

- A dedicated lift or lobby entrance separate from regular mall shoppers
- Access tied to the mall's operating calendar, not a standalone building
- Skyline views over the surrounding district rather than waterfront

Because this is a first-international-market opening, expect the operator to still be dialing in service, reservation systems and hours in the initial months.

## Getting in without a wasted trip

Confirm hours and reservation policy before you go. As a newly opened venue, published hours and booking rules can shift in the first weeks or months as the club settles into a routine.

Practical steps:

- Check Tribe's official channels or the mall's directory for current hours, since a brand-new rooftop venue's schedule is the least stable fact about it right now
- Book ahead if a reservation system exists; beach clubs of this type often reserve day beds or pool access separately from walk-in bar seating
- Ask at the mall's information desk which lift or entrance serves the rooftop level

Don't assume daytime pool access mirrors evening club hours. Sky beach clubs in this category typically run a day session (pool, lounging, food) and a separate night session (DJs, cocktails, no pool access), and Tribe's Shanghai debut is likely to follow the same split.

## Dressing and behaving like it's a pool party, not a bar

Treat it as swimwear-and-day-bed territory during pool hours, then a dressed-up club scene after dark, the same split Tribe built its Bangkok reputation on.

Bring:

- Swimwear if you plan to use the pool during day sessions
- A cover-up or change of clothes for the transition into evening service
- Sun protection if arriving during daylight hours, since it's an open-air rooftop

Because it's brand new and drawing on an established Bangkok following, expect strong opening-months interest and social media traffic. Arriving earlier in the day, before any evening session ramps up, is the safer bet for space around the pool while the venue is still finding its footing.
