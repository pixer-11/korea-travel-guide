## Forty hectares, laid out by students

These gardens have been here since 1936. They are formal and European in style, spread over 40 hectares of tree-lined allées, rose beds, herb plots and vegetable gardens. They share a campus with the Niagara Parks School of Horticulture, and its students do much of the planting and upkeep. That is why the borders change from season to season.

The rose garden is the main draw, and it peaks in June. Paths are wide and flat, edged with clipped hedges and labelled beds. A full loop takes 1–2 hours. Add another hour if you also want the Butterfly Conservatory on the same grounds, which has its own ticket.

## Reaching the Parkway gardens

The address is 2565 Niagara River Pkwy. That is north of the Clifton Hill and Falls strip, on the scenic road toward Niagara-on-the-Lake.

- **By car:** drive north on the Parkway. The lot is shared with the Butterfly Conservatory and it is paid. Current rates are on the machines and on the Niagara Parks website.
- **By WEGO bus:** the Green Line runs along the Parkway and stops at the gardens and conservatory.
- **On foot or bike:** the Niagara River Recreation Trail runs past the entrance. It is pleasant, but it is a long walk from the Falls.

## Timing the crowds

On weekends the gardens fill up between 10am and 6pm. Tour coaches and conservatory visitors arrive together, and the rose beds get crowded. Try not to plan a Saturday visit for midday.

The quiet hours are easy to reach. Early morning, 7am to 9am, is calm every day. Evenings are quiet too: from 6pm to 11pm on weekdays, and from 8pm to 11pm on weekends.

Google currently lists the grounds as open 6am to midnight. Treat that as this week's schedule, not a fixed rule. Hours shift with the season, and the conservatory keeps much shorter ones, so check the Niagara Parks site before you go.

## Walking the beds properly

These are working gardens with a teaching purpose. A few habits help:

- **Stay on the paths and grass walks.** The beds are student plots, and visitors trampling the edges is the most common complaint from staff.
- **Read the labels.** Most plants are tagged with their names, so it works as a free plant catalogue if you garden at home.
- **No picking.** That includes herbs and vegetables, even when they look ready to harvest.
- **Photography is welcome**, and the rose garden is a popular spot for wedding photos. If a shoot is happening, give it room.
- **Cards work fine** at the conservatory desk and the parking machines.

Pair the visit with other stops along the Parkway:

- **The Floral Clock:** a short drive north, across from the Sir Adam Beck hydro stations.
- **Niagara Glen:** a short way south, with trails that drop down to the river.
- **Whirlpool Aero Car:** further south, crossing above the whirlpool.

If you start early, you can see the gardens and the Floral Clock before the coaches arrive.
