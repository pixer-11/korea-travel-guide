## Two nights in Kallang

TXT play Singapore twice. The five-member HYBE group (Soobin, Yeonjun, Beomgyu, Taehyun and Huening Kai) brings the 'ACT: TOMORROW' world tour to the Singapore Indoor Stadium on 17 and 18 January 2027.

The 'ACT' name follows the group's earlier concert series, including ACT: LOVE SICK and ACT: SWEET MIRAGE. Those tours mixed staged acts and costume changes with the group's catalogue. The organiser publishes the dates, ticket links and seating plans on its official site.

## Reaching the Singapore Indoor Stadium

The stadium sits on the Kallang Basin waterfront. Its pitched roof stands beside the domed National Stadium, and both are part of the Singapore Sports Hub.

- **MRT:** Stadium station (CC6) on the Circle Line is the closest stop. A covered walkway runs from the exit past Kallang Wave Mall to the arena.
- **Alternative station:** Mountbatten (CC7), also on the Circle Line, is a slightly longer walk and is usually less crowded after the show.
- **Interchange:** Circle Line trains connect with the North-South and East-West lines at Dhoby Ghaut and Paya Lebar.
- **Ride-hailing:** Grab and taxis use the Sports Hub pick-up points. Queues there build quickly once the encore ends.

After the show, the Stadium station platform fills quickly. Many fans walk to Mountbatten or wait in the mall until the crowd thins.

## Merch lines, lightsticks and the stadium floor

TXT's fandom is called MOA. At Singapore arena shows, fans usually turn up long before doors open, and the merch line is the reason. Official tour merchandise is sold at booths on the Sports Hub concourse. Payment there is overwhelmingly by card and mobile wallet, which matches how most of Singapore pays.

- **Lightstick:** most of the arena carries the group's official lightstick. At HYBE shows these sync to the seat map, so the colour patterns sweep across the stands.
- **Fan freebies:** small fan-made goods such as photocards, stickers and slogans are traded and given away on the walkways outside. It's an informal swap culture, and a polite ask is all it takes.
- **Fan chants:** fans chant members' names in Korean during the intros. Many attendees learn the official fanchants beforehand from fan-shared guides.
- **Inside:** Singapore venues enforce bag checks. Professional cameras and recording equipment are not allowed at K-pop shows in this arena.

The air-conditioning is strong. A light layer is common among fans who spend hours outside in the January humidity and then sit inside.

## Eating and staying around the Sports Hub

Kallang Wave Mall is the default meeting point, with a food court, chain restaurants and a supermarket under one roof. Old Airport Road Food Centre is one of the city's best-known hawker centres, a short taxi ride east. Fans go there for char kway teow, satay and lor mee.

Visitors flying in for both nights usually stay around Bugis, City Hall or Lavender. All three are a few Circle Line or East-West Line stops from Kallang. Changi Airport is roughly a 20-minute drive from the stadium, which makes a morning flight after the second show workable.
