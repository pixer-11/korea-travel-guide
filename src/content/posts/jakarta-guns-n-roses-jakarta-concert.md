## A ring of concrete off Jalan Sudirman

Stand under the GBK main stadium and look up. The roof is a single steel ring running all the way round the oval, with a hole in the middle where the sky shows through. It was built for the 1962 Asian Games and rebuilt for the 2018 Games, and it still seats tens of thousands. On November 21, 2026, Guns N' Roses are booked to play here.

The stadium's formal name is Stadion Utama Gelora Bung Karno. Bung Karno is the popular name for Sukarno, Indonesia's first president. Locals just say "GBK". The building sits inside the large Senayan sports complex, surrounded by tree-lined paths, the smaller Istora Senayan arena and a ring of shopping malls. Jakarta's concert crowds have filled this oval for every big stadium tour that comes through the city.

## Reaching Senayan without sitting in traffic

Driving into Senayan on a concert night is the slow option. Jalan Sudirman is Jakarta's main business corridor, and it is congested on Saturday evenings even without a stadium show. Rail is your friend here.

- **MRT Jakarta (North–South Line):** Istora Mandiri and Senayan stations are both on Jalan Sudirman, a short walk from the complex. The line runs south to Blok M and Lebak Bulus and north to Bundaran HI.
- **TransJakarta Corridor 1:** the Blok M–Kota busway runs along Sudirman with stops beside the complex.
- **KRL commuter rail:** Palmerah station, on the Rangkasbitung line, is on the western edge of the complex. It's useful if you're staying out towards Tangerang.
- **Ride-hailing:** Gojek and Grab cars and motorbike taxis drop at the edge of the complex. Inner roads are often closed on event days.

Most visitors staying around Thamrin, Kuningan or Blok M can be in Senayan in well under half an hour by MRT.

## November means rain at GBK

The show falls in Jakarta's rainy season. Heavy afternoon and evening downpours are common in November. They can be short and violent, and they turn the walkways round the stadium into shallow streams. The roof covers the seating tiers but not the pitch, so festival-floor standing tickets leave you under open sky.

Pack for it:

- a light poncho rather than an umbrella, since stadiums commonly ban umbrellas from the stands
- a zip bag for your phone and passport
- shoes you don't mind getting wet
- a small towel, because humidity stays high after dark

Check the official site for the bag and item rules for this show. Search policies at Indonesian stadium gigs can be strict about food, drink and power banks.

## Paying, queuing and getting through the GBK gates

The crowd sorts itself out by gate, and your ticket tells you which one. Find your gate number on a map before you leave the hotel. The complex is big enough that walking from the wrong side can take a while.

A few things Jakarta concert-goers do as a matter of habit:

- **Pay by QRIS or card.** Indonesia's national QR payment standard is accepted by most vendors in and around GBK. Keep some rupiah in cash for street sellers on Jalan Sudirman.
- **Don't tip.** Tipping isn't expected at food stalls or by ride-hailing drivers, though rounding up in the app is appreciated.
- **Buy only from authorised sellers.** Scalped e-tickets that don't match your ID are a real risk at name-checked entry.
- **Eat before you queue.** The malls next door have large food courts, and queues for stadium food grow as the show approaches.
- **Learn two words.** *Pintu* means gate and *antre* means queue. Both show up on signs and in shouted instructions from marshals.

## Senayan before the show

Senayan is one of the easier parts of Jakarta to kill a few hours in. Plaza Senayan and Senayan City face each other across Jalan Asia Afrika, right beside the complex. Both have air conditioning, food courts and plenty of places to sit out a storm.

FX Sudirman is a short walk up the main road. Inside the complex, the jogging paths and Hutan Kota GBK, the city forest on the stadium's edge, offer shade and open space. Expect them to fill with fans in band T-shirts as the afternoon goes on. Allow two to three hours if you want to eat, find your gate and settle in without rushing.

## The midnight walk back to Sudirman

The real challenge comes after the last song. Tens of thousands of people leave GBK together, and every ride-hailing app in Senayan surges at the same moment. Pickup points jam up, and drivers often can't get near the stadium.

The calmer way out:

- Walk to the MRT rather than waiting for a car. Check the MRT's last-train time for that night on its official channels before the show, as extended service for big events isn't guaranteed.
- If you need a car, walk 15 to 20 minutes away from the complex first, towards Senayan City or further up Sudirman, then book.
- Agree on a meeting point with your group in advance. Mobile data often slows to a crawl when a full stadium tries to upload videos at once.

It all comes down to an evening on one fixed date, at one large concrete ring in Senayan. Sort out transit, rain gear and your gate number beforehand, and the rest of the night is easy.
