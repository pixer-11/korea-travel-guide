## Why this show matters

HONNE — the British duo Andy Clutterbuck and James Hatcher — built a decade-long catalog of lush, R&B-inflected electronic pop, from early singles like "Warm on a Cold Night" to later records blending synth-funk and soul. A decade in, they're marking the milestone with an anniversary tour, and Kuala Lumpur gets two nights instead of the usual single stop — a sign of how strong their Southeast Asian fanbase has grown since their earlier Malaysia appearances. For fans, back-to-back dates mean more flexibility to catch the show around work or travel schedules, and a better shot at tickets if one night sells out first.

## The venue: REXKL

Both dates are set for REXKL, a converted mid-century cinema on Jalan Sultan in the Chinatown/Petaling Street area of Kuala Lumpur. REXKL has become one of the city's go-to spots for mid-size touring acts, thanks to its raw, high-ceilinged hall and central location. It's a standing, general-admission-style venue rather than a seated theatre, so expect a floor crowd close to the stage and limited perimeter space to hang back. The building also houses cafes, bookshops, and indie retail, so it's worth arriving with time to explore rather than just filing in right at doors.

## Getting there

REXKL sits within walking distance of Pasar Seni LRT station (Kelana Jaya Line) and is a short ride from Merdeka MRT station, both of which connect easily to KL Sentral for onward travel from the airport. Grab (ride-hailing) is widely used and often the simplest option for a night show, since parking in the Chinatown grid can be tight. If you're staying in Bukit Bintang or KLCC, the trip to REXKL by train or car typically runs 15-25 minutes depending on traffic.

## How to visit like a local

Kuala Lumpur gig-goers tend to skip the very front rush and instead post up slightly further back near the sound desk, where sightlines are still solid but the crush is far more bearable — worth considering if you're not committed to barricade. Payment at KL venues has moved heavily toward cashless: expect card and e-wallet (Touch 'n Go, GrabPay) options at bars and merch stalls, though carrying a bit of cash covers any stall that's card-only-adjacent or has connectivity hiccups. Tipping isn't expected at bars or merch tables. Dress light and breathable — the hall gets warm once it fills up — and bring a light jacket only for the walk over, not the show itself.
