## One night under Nervi's dome

The Rome date is Thursday, November 5, 2026. It is part of a short Italian run of Dylan's European tour, with Milan and Bologna dates the same week. If one city doesn't suit you, the other two are close by train.

The venue is the Palazzo dello Sport, the round, ribbed-concrete arena that Pier Luigi Nervi and Marcello Piacentini designed for the 1960 Rome Olympics. Romans still call it the PalaEur. From the inside, the shallow dome reads as a pattern of concrete ribs radiating from the centre. It is a big indoor arena, and the seats rake steeply toward the stage.

## Reaching EUR from the centre

EUR sits south of the historic centre. Its wide, axial streets and white travertine buildings were laid out for the 1942 exposition that never happened. The arena stands on a rise at the southern end of the district.

- **Metro:** Line B to **EUR Palasport**, then a short walk uphill. **EUR Fermi**, one stop on, also works.
- **From Termini:** Line B runs direct, so you don't need to change trains.
- **From Trastevere or the Vatican:** take a bus or tram to Piramide and switch to Line B there.
- **Taxi:** the ranks fill quickly after the show, so walk a few blocks away from the arena before you hail one.

On a Thursday, the trip back is the tight part of the night. Check the last Line B departure on the ATAC site before you go, and don't assume trains run late.

## Doing the night the Roman way

Italian concert crowds arrive late and fill the arena in the final half hour, so expect the entrances to bunch up just before the show starts. Arriving early means shorter lines at security and the bag check.

- **Eat first:** EUR's bars do *aperitivo* from early evening. Order a spritz or a glass of wine and small plates come with it. It's an easy dinner before a weeknight show.
- **Pay:** most Roman bars take contactless cards. Keep a few coins for kiosks and the metro machines anyway.
- **Tipping:** it isn't expected. Rounding up at the bar is plenty.
- **Bags:** pack light. Arenas restrict large bags, and EUR has no convenient luggage storage nearby.
- **Language:** *biglietto* means ticket, *ingresso* is entrance, *uscita* is exit. *Posto a sedere* is a seat; *parterre* or *prato* is the standing floor.

If you have the afternoon free, walk EUR first. The Palazzo della Civiltà Italiana, the arched white cube Romans call the Square Colosseum, is a short walk from the arena. So is the lake park below it.
