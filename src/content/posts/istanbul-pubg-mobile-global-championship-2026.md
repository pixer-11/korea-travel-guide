## A month of squads in Istanbul

The calendar is long. The PUBG Mobile Global Championship 2026 is set for November 1 to December 1, 2026, in Istanbul. That is a full month of international battle royale, and very few fans stay for all of it.

PMGC traditionally runs in stages. A group or league phase whittles down the field, and a Grand Finals weekend closes the event. Every match drops a lobby of four-player squads onto the same map, and points come from placement and eliminations. You don't need to follow a single team to get the scoring after a couple of rounds.

The championship draws a heavy following from East and Southeast Asia. In the stands that means team flags, cheer chants in several languages, and a crowd that reacts to every late-circle fight.

## Venue, tickets and where the details live

The organiser publishes the venue, the stage-by-stage schedule and ticket links on its official PUBG Mobile esports site and social channels. The PMGC ticket listing has the current prices and seating categories.

Istanbul is familiar ground for this tournament: the city hosted PMGC in 2023. Large esports productions here usually take place in indoor arenas on the European side, which the metro serves.

Most travelling fans plan around the finals weekend. The earlier stages tend to be easier to get into and suit anyone in town on a shorter visit.

## Getting in and getting around

Most international fans fly into Istanbul Airport (IST). The M11 metro runs from the airport to Gayrettepe, where it connects with the M2 line through Levent, Şişli and Taksim. Sabiha Gökçen (SAW) on the Asian side is the other airport, and it gets more low-cost flights.

- **Istanbulkart:** one rechargeable card for metro, tram, Marmaray, buses and ferries. Contactless bank cards also work at many gates.
- **Where fans stay:** Şişli, Beşiktaş and Taksim, all on the M2 line or a short ride from it.
- **Weather:** November in Istanbul is cool and often rainy, so a waterproof layer does more good than a heavy coat.
- **Entry rules:** visa requirements depend on nationality. Türkiye's official e-Visa portal is evisa.gov.tr.

## Match-day habits in the stands

Esports crowds in Istanbul are loud and organised. Fan groups usually sit together in team colours, and the noise peaks during final-circle rotations, not kills in the early game. If you want the communal experience, sit near your region's flag block.

- **Phones and power banks:** venues usually allow them, and fans film the big plays. Large banners and poles often face stricter rules at security.
- **Food:** match days run long, so attendees often pick up a döner or simit near the metro on the way in.
- **Paying:** cards are accepted almost everywhere in central Istanbul. Small cafés and street vendors are where Turkish lira cash comes in handy.
- **Off days:** ferries from Kabataş or Eminönü cross the Bosphorus in about 20 minutes. Sultanahmet's Hagia Sophia and Blue Mosque are a tram ride away.

Matches between sessions are streamed on PUBG Mobile Esports' YouTube channels in several languages. Many fans watch the group stage from their hotel and save the arena for the decisive days.
