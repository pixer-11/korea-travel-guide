## A Monday night of "Dale" in Santa Giulia

## Unipol Dome, not Unipol Forum

This is the mistake that strands people in the wrong suburb. Milan has two arenas carrying the insurer's name, and they are nowhere near each other.

The **Unipol Forum** is the older arena in Assago, on the south-western edge of the city, reached by metro M2. The **Unipol Dome** is the newer arena in Santa Giulia, in the south-east, built for the Milano Cortina 2026 Winter Olympics and used for ice hockey during the Games. This concert is at the Dome.

Taxi drivers and ride apps will happily take you to either one. Say "Santa Giulia" or "Rogoredo" along with the venue name, and you will not end up across town while the opening act plays.

## Getting to Rogoredo

Santa Giulia is a redeveloped district beside Milano Rogoredo station. Rogoredo is one of the city's main rail hubs, so you have several ways in:

- **Metro M3 (yellow line)** to Rogoredo FS, direct from Duomo, Centrale and Porta Romana
- **Suburban S-lines** from Passante stations such as Porta Garibaldi and Repubblica
- **High-speed and regional trains**, many of which stop at Rogoredo on routes from Bologna, Florence and Rome

From the station it is a walk into the new district rather than a door-to-door ride. Linate airport is close by on the same side of the city, which suits fans flying in for the night.

Driving is the least comfortable option. Concert traffic merges with Monday evening commuters on the ring roads, and arena parking empties slowly after the encore.

## Getting in and getting out

Security at Italian arenas means bag checks and, usually, limits on bag size, bottles and anything that looks like professional camera gear. Fans normally travel light: phone, ID, ticket, a card for payments. Card and contactless are widely accepted in Milan, including at most venue bars.

Plenty of fans reach Santa Giulia an hour or so before doors to clear the gates without pressure. The bigger crunch comes at the end. Thousands of people funnel back towards Rogoredo at once, and the M3 platforms fill quickly.
