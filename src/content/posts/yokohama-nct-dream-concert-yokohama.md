## Inside K Arena Yokohama

K Arena opened in 2023 and was built for concerts, not sport. It holds around 20,000 people, and the seating rises in a fan shape so the whole room faces a single stage at one end. Nothing wraps around behind the performers the way it does in a converted stadium. Even the upper tiers look straight at the show rather than across it.

That layout changes how a K-pop show feels. The upper levels are steep, so the distance to the stage is mostly vertical. From the top you look down on the full stage picture: the formations, the screens and the sea of synchronised light sticks. The lower floor puts you close enough to follow individual members.

The arena belongs to a larger complex with an office tower and a hotel. Its concourses connect to raised pedestrian decks, so getting between the station side and the entrance mostly means walking on footbridges.
