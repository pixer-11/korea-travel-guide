## A French Concession leftover most tourists skip

Fuxing Park opened in 1909 under French colonial administration, and the layout still shows it: formal parterres, a round fountain plaza, and straight allées of plane trees. It sits at 516 Fu Xing Zhong Lu in Huangpu District, a ten-minute walk north of Xintiandi's shopping lanes. Unlike Xintiandi or People's Square, it rarely appears on tourist itineraries, which keeps it genuinely calm even on weekends.

The park's rating hovers around 4.5 from local and visiting reviewers alike, unusually high for a free municipal park. Ponds flank the eastern side, crossed by small stone bridges. A rose garden blooms in May. Benches line the central lawn, shaded by London plane trees planted over a century ago.

## What's actually inside

The park is compact, walkable in 30 to 45 minutes at an easy pace.

- Central fountain plaza, the park's original French-designed centerpiece
- A Marx and Engels statue, a Soviet-era addition from the 1980s
- Ornamental ponds with koi and stone footbridges on the east side
- Rose garden and manicured flowerbeds near the southern entrance
- Open lawns used for badminton, kite-flying and group dance in the evenings

None of this costs anything to see. There's no admission fee and no ticket booth to navigate.

## Morning tai chi, evening dance

Arrive before 8am and you'll find groups of retirees doing tai chi, sword forms, or ballroom dancing to portable speakers near the fountain. This is the park's real daily rhythm, not a staged performance for visitors. Locals treat it as neighborhood space rather than a sight, so behave accordingly: keep voices down, don't interrupt exercise groups to take photos, and step around card games and mahjong tables set up on stone tables near the western gate.

Cash isn't needed inside the park itself; the nearest food and drink is outside the gates on Fu Xing Zhong Lu and Sinan Lu. Evenings bring social dancing and family strolls, especially after dinner in warmer months.

## Getting there and timing your visit

The nearest metro stop is South Shaanxi Road Station, Lines 1 and 10, about a 5 to 8 minute walk. Exit toward Fu Xing Zhong Lu and head east. Several entrances ring the park; the main gate faces Gao'an Lu on the western side. Spring (March to May) brings blossoms and mild temperatures; autumn (October to November) gives clear skies and colored foliage.

Midday summer heat is intense with little shade over the open lawns, so mornings or early evenings work better. Pair a visit with the nearby Shanghai Sun Yat-sen Former Residence or a walk through the old French Concession's tree-lined side streets, both within 10 to 15 minutes on foot.
