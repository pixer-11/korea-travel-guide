## Five nights on the Thonburi bank

The river is the stage. ICONSIAM sits on the west bank of the Chao Phraya in Khlong San, facing the old Bangkok side across the water. Each year it turns its riverfront into the setting for Thailand's best-known New Year party.

The 2027 edition runs from 27 to 31 December 2026. The five nights mix concerts, drone shows and Thai cultural performances. Everything leads up to New Year's Eve, when a fireworks display stretches 1,400 metres along the river and the crowd counts down into 2027.

The event carries the "Amazing Thailand" name, the national tourism brand of the Tourism Authority of Thailand. Expect it to be staged for Thai families and foreign visitors alike, with plenty of national pride on show.

## How the riverfront works as a venue

ICONSIAM's front faces the river. The open River Park and its terraces look straight across to the Bangkok side. That frontage is why the fireworks work so well here: a 1,400-metre line of bursts runs along a broad bend of the water.

Inside, the mall's ground floor holds SookSiam, an indoor zone styled on regional Thai markets. It's lined with food stalls and craft counters. On countdown nights it's where many people eat before heading back out to the river.

Because the fireworks run so far along the water, you don't have to be at the mall to see them. Riverside hotels, Asiatique The Riverfront downstream and boats on the river all have views of parts of the display. The drone shows and stage performances, though, are centred on ICONSIAM itself.

## Getting to ICONSIAM when the city is on the move

On these nights the rail is quicker than the roads. The BTS Gold Line stops at Charoen Nakhon station, which is linked directly to ICONSIAM. It connects with the BTS Silom Line at Krung Thon Buri station.

The river is the other route in:

- **Shuttle boats** cross from Sathorn Pier, beside Saphan Taksin BTS station (Silom Line, Exit 2).
- **Chao Phraya Express boats** run up and down the river and link with piers on the Bangkok bank.
- **Taxis and ride-hailing** run into heavy traffic on Charoen Nakhon Road on New Year's Eve, and many drivers decline the trip.

When the fireworks finish, thousands of people leave at once. The Gold Line platforms and the piers fill up quickly. Many attendees eat a late meal inside the mall and let the first rush clear before they head home.

## Tickets and what entry looks like

The organiser publishes the dates, programme and any ticket links for Amazing Thailand Countdown on its official site and social channels. ICONSIAM also posts event information through its own channels.

Some parts of a countdown at this scale tend to be open viewing, while others need tickets: reserved zones, riverside dining packages and private boat cruises. Riverfront hotel restaurants and dinner cruises sell their New Year's Eve packages separately, through their own booking pages. The official channels are the place to find out which kind of access you have.

Big events like this one usually involve security screening and bag checks at the entrances. Attendees tend to carry small bags and leave large camera rigs, tripods and bulky items at the hotel.

## Counting down the Thai way

New Year's Eve here feels like a family outing as much as a party. Thai groups arrive together, spread out where there's space and stay for hours. The wishes you'll hear at midnight are "sawasdee pee mai", meaning Happy New Year.

What attendees typically do:

- **Settle in for the long haul.** The best river-facing spots go to people who arrive in the afternoon and stay put through the evening.
- **Eat inside first.** SookSiam and the mall's restaurants are the obvious places to eat, and they fill up as the night goes on.
- **Pay by card or QR.** Thai QR payments and cards are accepted widely in the mall, and small stalls take cash.
- **Keep the royal anthem in mind.** Official Thai events often include tributes to the monarchy. People stand quietly when these play.

December is the cool, dry season in Bangkok, so evenings are warm without the summer humidity. A light layer is plenty, even by the river.

## Making a night of the riverside

The earlier nights, from 27 to 30 December, are the easier way to see the drone shows and performances. They carry less of the pressure of the 31st, and many visitors come to one of these nights as well as New Year's Eve.

Nearby, Asiatique The Riverfront is a short boat ride downstream and has its own night market and Ferris wheel. Wat Arun stands upriver on the same Thonburi bank. Across the water, the Charoen Krung area around Sathorn Pier has older riverside hotels and late-night street food.

Many visitors stay along the river on either bank, around Saphan Taksin or Charoen Nakhon. From there the countdown is a walk or a short boat ride away rather than a fight through gridlock.
