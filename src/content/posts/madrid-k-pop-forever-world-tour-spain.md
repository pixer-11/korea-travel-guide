## Where Madrid's big concerts happen

Most touring pop shows in Madrid play one of a few venues. None of them is named for this tour, but these are the usual candidates:

- **Movistar Arena** (formerly WiZink Center), Salamanca district: Goya station, Lines 2 and 4.
- **Palacio Vistalegre**, Carabanchel: Vista Alegre station, Line 5.
- **IFEMA Madrid**, the trade-fair grounds by Campo de las Naciones: Feria de Madrid station, Line 8, which runs on to Barajas airport.
- **La Riviera**, a club-sized room beside the Manzanares: Príncipe Pío station, Lines 6 and 10.

The Metro shuts at around 1:30am. Fans leaving a late encore usually walk to the nearest station straight away, or take night buses (*búhos*) from Cibeles.

## Concert night, Madrid style

A few Spanish words make the ticket and the signs easier to read:

- **Entrada**: ticket
- **Pista**: the standing floor
- **Grada**: tiered seating
- **Puertas**: doors
- **Acceso**: the entrance gate printed on your ticket

Fans holding *pista* tickets typically queue for hours before doors to get a spot at the barrier. *Grada* holders usually come later, because their seats are numbered.

Lightsticks, fanchants and coordinated banner projects are normal at K-pop shows in Spain. Fan accounts usually share them on social media in the days before a show.

Madrid eats late. Restaurants serve dinner from around 9pm, so concertgoers tend to grab tapas or a *bocadillo* before doors. Card and contactless payment work almost everywhere. Tipping is light: people round up or leave a few coins.

## Getting to Madrid and staying over

Madrid is easy to reach. Barajas airport connects to the centre on Metro Line 8, changing at Nuevos Ministerios, and on Cercanías commuter trains.

High-speed AVE trains from Barcelona, Valencia, Seville and Málaga arrive at Atocha. Some services also use Chamartín. Both stations link into the Metro.

Visiting fans usually stay around Sol, Gran Vía or Malasaña. From there most venues are one Metro line or one change away. Around Goya, hotels sit within walking distance of Movistar Arena. Visitors who ride the Metro all weekend usually load trips onto a rechargeable Tarjeta Multi card from the station machines.
