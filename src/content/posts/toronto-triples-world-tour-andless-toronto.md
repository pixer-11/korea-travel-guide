## A big group in an old room

tripleS has 24 members. Massey Hall opened in 1894. Those two facts set the scale of this show: South Korea's most crowded girl group plays one of Toronto's oldest concert halls on November 11, 2026.

The group comes from the agency Modhaus and was built around fan participation. Through the Cosmo app, holders of digital "objekts" vote on units and title tracks. That makes the fandom, called WAV, unusually invested in who sings what. This guide does not cover which members appear on this tour.

The Toronto date is a Wednesday. It sits inside the North American run of the ANDLESS tour.

## Inside Massey Hall

The room is a tall horseshoe. A main floor sits under two curved balconies, and the side walls carry the hall's restored stained-glass windows. The building is red brick on the outside, with fire escapes running down its face. It closed in 2018 for a long revitalisation and reopened in 2021.

The hall seats a little over 2,500 people. That is small for a K-pop tour stop, and most people end up much closer to the stage than they would at an arena. The balconies wrap tight around the stage, so even the upper levels look down on it from close range.

- **Main floor:** closest to the stage and level with the performers.
- **Balconies:** steeper sightlines, and the best view of formation changes across the stage.
- **Side seats:** close up, but the stage is seen at an angle.

## Getting to Victoria and Shuter

Massey Hall sits one block east of Yonge Street, at 178 Victoria Street at the corner of Shuter. Queen station on TTC Line 1 is the nearest stop. Its Shuter Street entrance puts you about two minutes from the doors.

TMU station, formerly Dundas, is on the same line one stop north. It is a short walk down Victoria Street. TTC fare gates take a PRESTO card or a tap of a contactless credit or debit card, so visitors rarely need a ticket.

Parking downtown is limited and slow to leave on a show night. Most concertgoers come by subway or streetcar. The 501 Queen streetcar stops at Yonge and Queen, a block from the hall.

## Before and after, within a few blocks

The blocks around Massey Hall are some of the densest in the city. CF Toronto Eaton Centre runs along the west side of Yonge, directly across from the hall. Its food court is the easiest place for a quick meal before the show.

Sankofa Square, the former Yonge-Dundas Square, is two blocks north under its wall of digital screens. To the east, St. Michael's Cathedral Basilica stands on Bond Street, practically next door. The Elgin and Winter Garden Theatre, with its stacked theatres, is on Yonge just south of Queen.

For Korean food, Toronto's Koreatown runs along Bloor Street West near Christie station on Line 2. It is about twenty minutes from the hall by subway.
